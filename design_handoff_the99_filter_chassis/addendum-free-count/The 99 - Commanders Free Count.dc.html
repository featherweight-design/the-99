A way into Free only, from the line that counts.

A new player didn’t know Free only existed. On All my cards, the result line’s trailing slot, the one Discover uses for Help me choose, now says how many of these commanders are free to build right now, and one press switches Build from to Free only. It uses the Discover link’s treatment unchanged: sans 12.5 --fg-muted lead, and an accent action with a 50% underline over the whole 44px line. It sits at the right end of the wide rack, and on its own 44px line under the count on the compact one.
