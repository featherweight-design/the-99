The 99 — Decks & deck-builder · Hi-fi

Assemble the 99.

The richest surface in the app. Once a general is chosen, the builder makes finishing the deck fast and honest about gaps: suggested cards that explain themselves, colour-scoped search, live role counters, an always-visible view of what you don't own, and two things you can walk away with as text — the decklist and the shopping list. Advisory, never blocking — export at any size. Three surfaces: /decks (list), /decks/new (create), /decks/[id] (builder). Built mobile-first against the locked brand system + app shell; each state leads with the phone, desktop beside it. Decisions log in The 99 - Decks README.md.

Builder layout
A ledger + workbench split. Group the list the standard way — by card type — or by role. Same logic phone & desktop.
Suggestions ↔ search
One Add panel, two modes. Suggestions are display-only with a reason; search is deliberate. Adding is always a click.
Role counters
A recommended mix — ramp 10 / draw 10 / removal 8 / lands ~37 — each number sourced and explained. A guide, never a gate.
Shopping list
Unowned cards are marked in place and gathered into a filterable list you can total and export.
Exports
One dialog, two plain-text tabs — decklist (commander first) and shopping list — each copy-to-clipboard.
Create flow
Two doors — pre-filled from a recommendation, or a standalone picker of your legal generals. Server validates either way.
