01 — Tone

Flavor in the frame, precision in the data

Playfulness lives in headings, the wordmark, and empty states — the low-stakes, high-personality corners. The moment a table of cards or a mana-curve breakdown appears, the tone goes quiet and legible. It's a tool that happens to have a sense of humor.
