R1 Commanders & Discover · two lanes, two racks

Two destinations. Each carries its own controls.

/commanders is what you own and haven’t built — 148 rows, and availability lives here. /discover is everything you don’t own — about 3,300 rows, and availability is not one of its controls. Every control on a lane renders at all times and greys when it has nothing to act on; nothing is removed. Chips show only the controls you moved off default, and each screen prints one result line.

Art blocks stand in for commander art. Frames follow the refresh: 32px hairline pills, one filled bone button per screen, brass reserved for in use / to buy and for active-filter state.

G · the four calls left to Design
Call 1
Colour match, no pip lit
Any Exactly Any Exactly
Greyed keeps its geometry and loses its ink: wrapper to #171310, both labels to #6E6654, no hover fill, aria-disabled and focusable-but-inert. The selected segment keeps its hairline outline so you can still see which mode you will get back — a control that forgets its own state on the way out is the one thing greying must not do. It fails AA at 2.6:1 by the WCAG inactive-control exemption; the sheet adds one mono line of why, desktop does not (the pips are 22px away).
Call 2
Four or five chips
“dru” × 2 colours × +3 Clear
Desktop shows all five on one line — measured at 612px of 1,058px available, so there is no wrap to design. Phone keeps one line that never wraps and never scrolls: chips fill in control order, the rest collapse into a dashed +N that opens the sheet, and Clear is pinned right. Two rejects: wrapping (the list moves down as you filter, exactly when you are watching it), and horizontal scroll (hides chips behind a gesture and can push Clear off-screen). Multi-value axes collapse before the row does — 2 colours past two pips, never five chips of hue.
Call 3
640 to 1024px
<1024 — sheet ≥1024 — inline rack
The sheet wins to 1024px. The inline rack needs 300px of search, a 190px pip wrapper, a 250px availability group and a 150px order trigger — 890px plus gaps before the list gets anything, so at 900px the rack either wraps to two rows or squeezes the search field below usefulness. One breakpoint, one implementation, chosen with matchMedia and never both rendered. Between 640 and 1024 the persistent row keeps its 44px targets and simply grows wider; the sheet stays a sheet rather than becoming a centred dialog, because the list behind it is still the thing being changed.
Call 4
The Filters badge
Filters Filters 2 Filters 4
A brass 999px pip, #14110E numeral on #C9A24B, mono 10.5/600, from the first active filter — and the button’s border turns brass with it, so the state survives for anyone who can’t read the figure. It counts controls, not values, and it never counts search or the order trigger: both live on the row beside it, and a badge that includes what you can already see makes the sheet look fuller than it is. Ceiling is 4 on the owned lane, 3 on discover, so no 9+ case exists. Rejected: a bare dot (loses the count for nothing) and the count in the label (Filters · 2 reads as a name, not a state).
H · contract — what engineering can rely on
Behaviour
Filtering is live on both lanes; the only commitment control is Clear. The sheet’s primary is a dismiss and says so by naming the count it will reveal. Every control is a URL parameter, so a filtered lane is linkable and survives a reload. Sort is single-select and closes on pick; colours are multi-select. Nothing above the list animates the list.
Accessibility
Pips are a group of toggle buttons with colour names as accessible names — hue is never the only signal (lit pips carry a 1.5px bone border). Colour match is a radio group, aria-disabled and focusable when no pip is lit. The result line is the lane’s single aria-live="polite" region, debounced 300ms, and it is the only thing that announces. Sheet is role=dialog aria-modal, focus trapped, Esc dismisses and never reverts.
Windowing, unchanged
3,301 rows keep the shipped reveal window: the rack states matches against pool once, at the top, and the “25 shown · N more below” receipt stays inside the list where the player reaches it. The rack does not restate it, and the result line is not a second window count.

Colour ordering does work the pips already do, and does it worse — you cannot ask for “Black then Red” without lighting those pips, at which point the order is a grouping of a set you have already narrowed. Confirmed: as drawn in R1 the row identifies the commander, not a printing. The bleed is whichever art the app already holds for that commander and the row names no set, collector number or finish. This frame is the contingency: the moment a printing is named, the 34×48 thumb returns between the rank and the name — radius 3px, no border (the bleed behind it supplies the edge), a 0 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.55) lift so it separates from its own art, and the printing line directly under the name in mono 10.5px with the finish in #C6B79A. Nothing else moves: the content column loses 48px and the figure columns are untouched. If the printing is named, the thumb is not optional — a named printing with no picture of it is the one case where the bleed actively misleads, because the bleed may be a different printing’s art.
