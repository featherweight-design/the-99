Narrow commanders by theme

Every frame is the shipped Discover page rendered from its own components (kaldheim), with the proposal applied as code: a Themes ▾ control built from the rack's two shipped multi-select shapes, the Printed in panel on desktop and the filter sheet plus its second-layer list on phone. Counts are illustrative; the theme names are EDHREC's, the same ones the row's play-style sentence prints.

Pick one or several

Checkbox rows on desktop, 44px pills plus the full list on phone. Several combine with Any (default, like Printed in) or All, a modifier that is greyed until two are picked, like Exactly.

Listed by popularity

Most commanders first, counted under the other controls, with search for the long tail. Mechanics and creature types share one list; typal ones carry a "typal" hint. No grouping: 394 names in two groups is still a scroll.

Reads on the row

One chip per control, as shipped. On each row the picked themes lead the play-style sentence in accent ink ("play styles include Tokens, …"), so why a row survived is on the row.
