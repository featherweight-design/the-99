Precon upgrade result: which store the prices come from

Addendum to the store choice handoff. Upgrade this precon now prices each add at the reader’s own store (PR #3231). The result’s To buy figure and its buy tags said nothing about where they came from. Every frame is the shipped result rendered from that branch on the landing demo’s Prismari Artistry run, with six adds the reader doesn’t own. Store figures are placeholders: TCGplayer about 9% under Mana Pool, Card Kingdom about 18% over.

1 · One line under the facts names the store

  • Under the four facts (Led by, Bracket, To buy, Already yours), the deck Shopping strip’s own provenance line, word for word and in its mono 9.5 --fg-locked: single-copy prices on Card Kingdom · last checked Monday.
  • It covers the To buy figure and every add’s buy tag, so the tags stay bare figures, as on Shopping rows (store choice call 3). The store is named once on the result.
  • The date is the repriced read’s newest price (run.pricesAsOf), so it says how fresh the figures on screen are, not the run’s day (the header keeps that). Past ten days it gains these are behind, as shipped.

2 · The TCGplayer note

  • Beside the store, only when a figure shows: TCGplayer market prices · averages of recent sales, so a listing can differ · last checked Monday. The same short form as the Shopping strip and the cart. Rows carry no note.

3 · An add with no price at the reader’s store

  • The row keeps a bare buy tag: the shipped silence state, so the card still reads as one to buy. Never $0.00.
  • To buy counts it (6) and its figure leaves it out. The line counts it out: single-copy prices on Card Kingdom · 5 of 6 priced · last checked Monday.
  • None priced: To buy shows the count and no figure, and the line reads no prices from Card Kingdom for these · last checked Monday.

4 · The store switch off, and a failed price read

  • Switch off: no line. To buy and the tags draw the run’s stored figures, exactly as today.
  • The price read fails: the panel falls back to the stored figures, which came from the store the run was pressed at. The line goes too, so it never names the reader’s store over another store’s figures. The line follows the reprice, not the preference.

5 · The cart stays Mana Pool’s

  • The result has no cart link of its own. The line always names the reader’s store and never the cart’s, so a TCGplayer reader never sees Mana Pool on the result.
  • A deck started from the run opens on its Shopping list, which already handles this (store choice call 6): Buy these at Mana Pool for a Mana Pool reader, Copy the list and the store’s paste page for everyone else.

6 · Nothing to buy

  • Every add already owned: To buy reads 0 and no line draws, since no figure needs a source.
