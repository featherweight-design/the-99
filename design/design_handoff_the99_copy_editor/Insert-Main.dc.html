Insert a fill-in · copy editor addendum · R1

Editor with fill-ins (ed-fill), after Hugh’s 2026-11-25 ruling: a fill-in can be taken out and put in, like a variable. Drawn over the shipped editor on Help me choose (intake.colorsAny, renamed {colorNames}). Three states at each width, in the order an editor meets them: F1 a fill-in removed and offered back, F2 inserting it at the cursor, F3 none left to insert. Phone and Desktop boards sit to the right.

Calls for the five open items

1 · The insert control

  • Sits right under the New field, before the word count and notes: it acts on the field, the notes only describe it.
  • Insert at cursor then one button per fill-in the line can take and doesn’t use. No menu: the list is one or two, so the choices sit in view (inline options over an extra step).
  • Selecting one drops the chip at the caret. Focus stays in the field (the button doesn’t take it), so on a phone the keyboard stays up. Never placed a caret: it lands at the end.
  • Dashed border with a + = not in the text yet; the solid chip = in the text.

2 · The chip’s ✕

  • Always shown, after the name, on both widths. Hover-only hides it on touch and from a first-time editor; a line rarely has more than two chips, so it adds no noise.
  • Desktop: a 16px round target that fills with 55% accent on hover. Phone: same look, a 36px hit box centred on it, so a tap on the ✕ never starts a drag.
  • Backspace or Delete beside a chip removes it too. The grip and the name still drag.

3 · None left to insert

  • The row doesn’t show. No “nothing to insert” line: an editor who hasn’t removed anything has nothing to look for.
  • The fill-in note already says ✕ takes it out; take one out and it appears under the field (F1). A line with no fill-ins shows neither.

4 · What a fill-in shows today

  • Yes, in the note and on the insert button, never on the chip: the chip’s look is locked, and a value inside a sentence’s chip makes it read twice.
  • Note: {colorNames} reads “White and Blue” here. Button: + colorNames White and Blue.
  • The value is what this page shows, read from the visible text the line matched. A fill-in the page doesn’t show today gets the name alone.

5 · The fill-in note, rewritten

  • With a value: {colorNames} reads “White and Blue” here. Drag it to move it, or select ✕ to take it out.
  • Without one: {colorNames} is filled in by the app. Drag it to move it, or select ✕ to take it out.
  • Was: “…Drag it to move it; it can’t be deleted.”

For engineering

E · How it could work

  • Insert list = the row’s supplied fill-ins minus those in the field’s current value, recomputed on every input. Values: the capture for each hole when the line matched as a pattern (match.ts); none for a fill-in the page doesn’t show.
  • Buttons cancel pointerdown so the field keeps focus and its caret; insert at the saved range, then put the caret after the chip. Keyboard: Tab reaches the buttons after the field, Enter inserts and returns to the field.
  • The validator no longer refuses a missing fill-in (Hugh’s 2026-11-25 ruling); braces naming a fill-in the code doesn’t supply are still refused.
  • Copy, for src/lib/copy/copy-editor.ts: editor.insert “Insert at cursor”, editor.insertLabel(name) “Insert {name} at the cursor”, editor.removeFill(name) “Remove {name}”, editor.fill(name, value?) as call 5.
