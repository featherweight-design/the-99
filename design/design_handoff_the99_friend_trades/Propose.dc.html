Proposing a trade: one sheet

Trade opens the trade sheet: 'Propose a trade to Colin' (two sides, each with its own total, the store provenance once), then the picker. The picker's switch is a bare SegmentedSwitch (Colin's spares / Your spares), a line on wishlist matches (listed first), and the key line ('wants it', 'already offered'). Each tile: the card, Add 10px under it ('✓ In the trade' once picked, ringed), then one line of half-width chips. Wants (fact blue, two-people glyph) only ever names this trade's friend. Offered (muted, out-arrow) names the one friend, or 'N friends' for several; on a phone a several-friends chip drops its arrow so 'N friends' fits (10.5px text under 375 wide). Chips are filtered, so a lone Offered slides left. The Offered chip is a press: a popover 'Also in your offers to' lists each friend with 'Waiting · N days left' and Open ›. A spare may be in two offers (Q9).
