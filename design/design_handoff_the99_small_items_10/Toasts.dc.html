The rule (every toast, every page)

  1. Frame: the shared ToastFrame. Radius 10, --elevated on a 1px --border-strong edge, the shipped shadow. No status dot, no colored edge.
  2. Slot: bottom center of the window, min(420px, 100% − 32px), 16px over the tab bar on a phone, 24px from the foot from md. Not centered on the main column.
  3. Where a page has a foot a toast would cover, its shipped stack keeps the same frame: the deck stack over the quick-add dock, the cart stack over the cart’s foot. Those are the only other slots.
  4. /credits joins the center slot. Its bottom-left 440 slot is the deck slot as it shipped before the stack; /credits has no dock, so nothing needs it.
  5. Action: one text press, --accent-text 600 13.5, underlined at 2px, 44px tall: Try again or Undo. No outlined or filled button in a toast.
  6. Close: the bare ✕ (44px, --fg-faint) only on a toast with no action. Every toast still leaves on TOAST_MS, held on hover and focus.
  7.
