The halo fades with the figure

The halo is the figure's ::before, so it has no motion of its own: it takes the figure's opacity and drift. Frames are the shipped animations paused at each time, on the white plate at your seat (iPad, upright, 2×) so the halo is easy to see; over real art it is fainter.

Fade out · at the save, 2 s after the last step (life-pending-out, 400 ms ease-out, drifting 6 px up)
