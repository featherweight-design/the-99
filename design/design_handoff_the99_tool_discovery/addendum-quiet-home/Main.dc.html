What's new joins the main column when Home is quiet

On a day with no open table, no game to log and no lead story, the main column holds only the search panel and Pick up where you left off, and ends about 600px above the rail. What's new is the one block that can fill that space, so it moves there on quiet days and keeps the top of the rail on busy ones (tool-discovery R8, unchanged).

1 · The rule

  • Home counts the cards that head its main column: an open table (each one), a game to log, the lead story. Two or more is a busy day: What's new tops the rail, as shipped. Fewer than two is a quiet day: What's new sits in the main column under Pick up where you left off.
  • Not counted: the announcement banner (it spans both columns), the welcome credits (they live in the search panel's foot) and the signature ask (a short line). None of them closes the gap.
  • Decided when Home loads. Dismissing the lead or the game to log during a visit leaves What's new where it is; the next visit draws the quiet layout.
  • Below 1180 (tablet portrait, phone) Home is one column and nothing changes: What's new stays under the lead, above the decks.

2 · What changes when it moves

  • Rows draw wide, the action on the right of the text, as they already do below 1180. In the rail they stay compact (action under the text).
  • Its foot fits one line again: Past announcements on the left, Clear all and Show all 7 on the right. The heading, the 2 new count, Choose notifications and the Show all sheet are unchanged.
  • The rail keeps Commitments then Friends, shipped order, and ends early. A short side column next to a long main one reads as resting, where a short main column reads as missing.
  • No Commitments and no Friends either: the rail is not drawn and the main column takes the width, which is already how Home treats an empty rail.
  • Nothing new yet in What's new: its quiet card moves with it.
  • No new copy. Build: one count in dashboard.tsx and a wide flag on WhatsNew (scratch-gen/proposal.diff).

Q1 · On a quiet day, does What's new go under the decks or above them?

A: under Pick up where you left off (drawn above). Your decks stay where they are today, inside the first screen, and the news fills the space that was empty. B: above the decks, right under the search panel (Desktop board, third frame). That is the order below 1180 and on a phone, but the decks drop about 460px, below the fold on a 900px screen.

Recommended: A. On a quiet day the reader came to search or carry on with a deck, and A keeps both where they already are.

Q2 · Where is the line between quiet and busy?

A: two or more of open table, game to log, lead story keep What's new in the rail (drawn). One alone (a lead, or a table) still moves it into the main column. B: any one of them keeps it in the rail. The Desktop board's Table only frame shows B: the table adds 100px to the main column and the same 500px hole stays.

Recommended: A. It is the point where the two columns come out about even (Table and lead frame), so it fixes the gap without making a busy Home longer.
