The wishlist is a page of its own; a friend’s spare is a face under the card you’re short of

Your wishlist lives at /wishlist, one row under Friends in the account menu, and works with no friends at all. A card gets on it from one place, Want it in card details’ footer, which every card in the app opens to, plus the page’s own search. Lines are art rows with a want stepper; when your collection holds enough, the line takes a GOT stamp and moves to Got, where it stays until you clear it. A friend’s page (their row on /friends) shows their wishlist, view only. And where a deck is short of a card, a friend who shares spares with your pod shows as a small chip with their face: Dave has 1 spare. Pressing it names who, and how many. Nothing is bought, borrowed or priced.
