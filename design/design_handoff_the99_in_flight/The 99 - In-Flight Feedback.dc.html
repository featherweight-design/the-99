Re-ruling · in-flight feedback for control flips that fetch · unblocks the region loader

The list stays because it is the previous answer — not because nothing is being fetched.

Three rulings forbid animating, dimming or skeleton-ing the list on a control flip, and all three give the same reason: the flips are “state changes over data already on the client.” That is true of one of the four controls. Scope, sort and discovery availability are server round-trips — list-nav.ts:110-113, only the active (sort, avail) pair is ranked, so the other ordering does not exist until it is asked for. Measured: ~270 ms warm, 2,870 ms worst case.

The prohibition stands; the reason is replaced. The rows on screen are the previous answer, correctly rendered and still pressable, so replacing them with grey bars for up to 2.9 s trades information the reader has for a progress indicator they did not ask for. Under 500 ms the shipped in-control crown is the whole treatment. Past 500 ms one line changes, and it is a line already on the page: each fetching control owns exactly one clause of the header subtitle, and its clause is replaced by its own present participle.

Read RULING-in-flight-feedback.md for the ruling in full and the three document amendments, README.md for the calls and the gap report, in-flight · copy strings.ts for the three strings and the subtitle assembler. Not gated — the in-control half shipped in #411 / #416; this adds one copy state and deletes a ticket.

F · timings, tokens, motion and a11y contract
One gate, one constant. PENDING_GATE_MS = 500 for all three fetching controls — one setTimeout on the flag becoming true, cleared on settle. Per-control thresholds make the product feel different depending on which knob you touched.
Measured window. ~270 ms warm — below the gate, crown only. 2,870 ms worst case for a full recompute — crown plus one clause, for ~2.4 s. Cache-served repeat flips do not navigate and are not this.
No new token, no new hex, no new motion class. The pending clause is --fg-faint mono at the subtitle's own size. The crown is --accent, or base-on-brass on a selected segment.
Motion: one thing moves. The crown's 1,200 ms opacity pulse. The … is one static character. The list has no transition, and the subtitle's text swap has none either. prefers-reduced-motion holds the crown at 0.7 and changes nothing else, because nothing else was motion.
Focus survives the flip. Controls are never disabled, so the pressed control keeps focus and its 2px brass :focus-visible ring throughout. No focus move on arrival.
One announcement, on settle. Pending clause aria-hidden; PendingLabel's accessible name covers in-flight; the shipped live region is debounced to one message per settle. Two announcements for one press is a stutter.
The list region is not touched. No wrapper, no overlay, no aria-busy, no pointer trap. A reader who spots the row they wanted in the outgoing list may press it, and that press must work.
Contrast. The participle sits at the same --fg-faint as the words beside it — it is never dimmer than its own sentence. The 0.55 dim applies only to a control whose label has become a mark.
