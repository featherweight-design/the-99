Two paths, chosen by the sender. One answer, whatever happened.

Add a friend opens on a choice between two cards: They’re on The 99 (today’s friend link, unchanged and free) and Invite someone new, which carries the count as a hand of card backs and greys out at 0. Inside it, the two ways sit on one screen: an invite QR for the table, and an email field for everyone else. The QR holds one invite while it is live and gives it back when it runs out. The newcomer types their own email on their phone and proves it with a code before anything else happens. Every ending the sender can see reads “Invitation sent”. Uninvited visitors meet a face-down card that says the app is invite-only, with no waitlist. /admin gains an Invites tab with the tree, a count on each account, and a top-up on each group.

user.createdSpentThey made their account. The count drops by one; you’re friends.

The sender’s line is always N invites left, plus · 1 waiting or · 1 held by an open QR when it applies. “Waiting” never turns into anything that says which way it ended, except a new friend appearing, which only a real sign-up can cause.

02The invite QR: a countdown ring around the plate

The shipped light plate, with an accent ring that drains over 15 minutes and the time under it; under a minute it turns warning. Expired, the plate fades to grey under “Expired” and one press makes a new one. Once a newcomer claims it, the sheet turns to “Invitation sent”. One live QR per account: showing it again re-shows the same one.

03The newcomer’s screen: the sender’s card, one field, then a code

No shell, the landing’s own ground. The card is face up with the sender’s name and commander, typed “Invitation”. One field (Your email), Send code. The code arrives before any account exists; a right code hands over to Clerk’s sign-up with the email filled and locked. Used, expired, wrong and expired-code states each get one line.

“Their email” and Send invitation, on the same screen as the QR. Sending takes the same time every time. The answer replaces the field: ✓ Invitation sent, with when it comes back. Nothing about the address is ever said again.

For engineering (gaps, not Design’s to settle)

Equal time. “Sending…” lasts the same for every email (a floor on the server’s answer), and the QR claim turns the sender’s sheet at the same moment whatever the email.
One live QR per account. Showing it again returns the same token while it lives; closing the sheet doesn’t release it.
The code. 6 digits, about 10 minutes, a resend cooldown, and a cap on wrong tries per claimed QR. Whether Clerk’s own sign-up code or ours: the screens fit either.
A switch. The chooser needs one (name it); the About entry follows it. Accounts with SOCIAL_FRIENDS_ENABLED off have no /friends and so no way to invite: decide whether invites need it.
The route. The QR’s public page is drawn as /join/[token]; name it as you like.
