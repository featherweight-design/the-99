NEWInviting someone new

/friends opens Add a friend

The 99 is invite-only, so new players arrive through the people already in it. On Friends, press “Add a friend”, then “Invite someone new”. At the table, show an invite QR: they scan it, type their own email, and confirm it with a code. Or type their email here, and The 99 sends the invitation.

Once they make their account, you’re friends. Adding someone who already plays? Pick “They’re on The 99” and send a friend link: it uses no invite.

  1. ·An invite is used only when someone makes an account. One that isn’t used within 7 days comes back.
  2. ·An invite QR holds one invite for its 15 minutes, and gives it back if nobody uses it.
  3. ·Whatever the email, you’re told “Invitation sent”, so nobody can use an invite to find out who’s on The 99.
  4. ·How many you have left shows under “Add a friend”. Invites are given out by The 99, not bought.
