Offline

Card details opens from what the page already holds; the link stays. The sheet then fails to read the printings as shipped (copies.loadFailed). No header or bar change, so no crowding.

What D costs

Only cards already on screen can be added. A card you just pulled that is in none of your decks needs search first, then its details, then the link: three steps per card. A pack of five is five sheets and five saves, five History entries.

What D gets

The smallest change: one gate lifted on a shipped link. It is the per-card route the other three directions want anyway, so D is drawn as its own option and as the floor under A, B and C.
