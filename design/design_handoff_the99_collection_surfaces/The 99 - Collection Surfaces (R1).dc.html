collection surfaces · H11 + H18 · 2026-10-02

A 102px head that never leaves, three cards across, and one place called Collection.

Call 1 — Collection pins from the start; it does not collapse. The phone head is two fixed rows, 102px against 844 (12.1%), and it is position: sticky in server markup with no script behind it. Sets collapses because its open head is 288px. This head is short enough to pin as it is, and that also keeps it clear of the in-flight work: no threshold, no spacer, no ResizeObserver, and nothing measured before the cards paint. So “collapsed” is drawn as scrolled: the tabs and the key scroll away, and the head stays the same height. §1 §2

Call 2 — the search field gets row 1 to itself, except for the view well. It is 262px wide at 390 and 232px at 360, against 163px at 390 on the shipped page (measured from the screens), with 15px type in a 44px box. Row 2 holds Filters, the hairline, the shipped Sort: Name · A–Z trigger and the result count. Below 375px the count drops its noun visually and keeps it for screen readers. While the field is focused the view well steps aside through :focus-within, so the field runs the full 358px. Chips leave the pinned head: they sit on a shelf just below it that is present only while a filter is on, and the Filters badge carries the state once you scroll.

Call 3 — the 3-up tile keeps every mark at 16px; the chip loses its word. The tile is 115px wide at 390 and 105px at 360, with the whole card at 63:88 and the same three-line caption. What drops: the availability chip's word (the glyph keeps its outlined box, and the word moves to the key and the spoken name), the caption's side padding (9px to 7px), and the name's length (about 13 characters before truncating, against about 20 today). A fourth glyph becomes +N, counted from the data and never measured. §3

Call 4 — Proxies is a tab: Cards · Sets · Proxies. The strip is the same on all three routes at both widths, and every tab is a plain link. It replaces the By set and Proxies pills and both breadcrumbs. The way back is the tab you left by. An empty collection gets Cards · Proxies, so the empty state's own Proxies button goes. §4

Call 5 — the phone default stays rows. The 3-up grid is the grid, and the head is the same in both views. Flipping a default under readers who never chose is Product's call, and it is gap 1. §6

§4 · where Proxies lives: one tab strip on three routes
This is not the view-mode segment that set-collections R3 rejected (1c), and the difference is the point. That segment sat in the Rows/Grid well, where the other two options keep the URL path and the filters. A third option there that navigates away is the /commanders defect. This strip sits above the filters, belongs to no filter frame, and changes the URL path on every tap. It is place navigation, and it reads like the app's other route tabs, not like a view toggle. Ruling 15 holds unchanged: each tab is a bare link with no query and no memory, nothing carries across, and Back is a route change.

Why 768–999 pins as well. That band already gets the compact chassis and the sheet, because the wide row does not fit until 987px. It is the same reader problem as the phone with a narrower list, so it gets the same answer. The one-row form at min-[600px] holds search at 301px or more beside every control. The grid is the shipped desktop floor: 3 tiles of about 199px at 768.

It depends on the portal. The comment in filter-bar.tsx says why the sticky rule starts at 1000: a z-[2] sticky box made the rail paint over an open sheet. Pinning here is only safe once the sheet renders outside the sticky box. §7
§6 · rows, the phone's default, and whether 3-up changes it
The head is identical in rows, at both widths and in every state; the rows frame is the last column of §1 at 390. What differs is below the head. Rows keep the shipped key without the availability entries, because the row draws the marker with its word, and a key never glosses a mark the reader cannot find. The tile grid passes withAvailability to CollectionMarkKey, and rows do not.
Recommendation: the default stays rows for R1. First, readers who never touched the toggle would find their surface changed under them, and the stored choice exists to stop that. Second, rows carry the cost track and the full name, which 3-up truncates at about 13 characters. Third, this lets the grid change ship and be judged on the readers who chose it. If Product wants art-first browsing as the default, it is a one-line change in browser.tsx plus the matching CSS rule for the cookieless first visit. Gap 1.
