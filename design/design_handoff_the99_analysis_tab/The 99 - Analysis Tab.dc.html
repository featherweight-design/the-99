4 · the deck-stats ledger — ruled out of this tab

Out. Round 0 mounted Deck Stats R3 whole under the mix; it was never scheduled, and it should not be scheduled here.

Every block on this tab is measured against something — the climb against the bracket rules, the findings against the lists, the mix against EDHREC. The ledger is measured against nothing, by its own package's one-line summary, and that absence is exactly why its own package placed it at the foot of the Deck panel, under the list it describes. That package is the more specific ruling on the block and it still stands.

And it would make this tab the longest page in the app for the reader who came to argue a bracket: four distributions below five findings below the climb, on a tab whose first screen already carries the answer.

What it costs: nothing drawn here changes. stats.ts and stats-query.ts are on main with no component mounting them; that work is the deck-stats package's, at its own position, as its own item.

Where its three sections live instead On the table — the record. Games already draws it at its own altitude. The curve · The colours — facts about the list. Foot of the Deck panel, per design_handoff_the99_deck_stats/ §1. If Hugh wants it here anyway, it mounts below the mix, full width, and nothing above it moves.

8 · opening the tab: fetching the stored reading
8aLoading, outcome unknownreplaces the empty-state flash

Today the tab draws 2a while the stored reading is fetched, then swaps to the reading: a not analyzed flash for the common case. The tab now draws nothing it cannot yet know until the fetch settles. No Overview label, no empty-state ring, no bracket box, no banner, no mix, and no bracket chip in the header. Just the five chip marks in a row at 22, a light passing left to right (the at-wave keyframe, 1.6s), and Loading….

Why a row, not the ring: the ring belongs to not analyzed and to Reading your deck… (a real analysis, 2b). Reusing it here would show the not-analyzed state for a split second, the flash this state exists to remove. The row is the same marks and motion, so it still reads as this tab, but it never appears in an answer. Why no skeleton: the fetch may resolve to 2a or to any analyzed shape. Any skeleton would guess one and be wrong for the rest.

Timing: draw it only after 150ms, so a fast fetch goes straight to the answer with no loader. Once it is drawn, hold it for at least 400ms so it never blinks. It fills a 420 / 320px block so the tab row does not jump. Accessibility: role="status" and aria-busy on the tabpanel. Under reduced motion the marks hold at 0.5 and only the word shows. Better still: the header's bracket chip already needs the stored reading. If the deck page loads it on the server with the header, the tab has it on first paint and this state only appears on a slow client refetch.
