What “see the N cards” opens

1 · Press the opener

Every graded tile and every dependency row with cards ends in one opener: see the N cards, or see the N lines for win speed. It’s the findings’ own control: mono, accent, dotted underline, 44px tall on the phone, and aria-haspopup="dialog".

2 · The ledger opens over the tab

It’s the receipt’s sheet, not a new page. At 1300 it’s a 760 modal, centered over the tab on the shipped 72% scrim. At 390 it’s a sheet from 52px with its header pinned. The tab stays where it was underneath, and Escape, the ×, or a press on the scrim closes it, with focus back on the opener.

3 · The app’s own card row

Each line is the shared card row (row-shell.ts): a 7px plate on --surface-rail, the availability dot, the name, the view’s own tag (the why), the 72px CostTrack, and the points. Only the tag and the points belong to this view. It’s 34px tall at 1300 and 52px with the meta line at 390. Hover shows the shipped HoverPreview (drawn above), and a press opens the card detail. The card stacks over the ledger exactly as it does over the receipt: ← Interaction to step back, and the page behind closes both. Depth stops at two.
