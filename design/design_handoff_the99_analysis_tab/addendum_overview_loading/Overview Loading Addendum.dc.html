The overview says it is being written, instead of saying it never was

This amends the Analysis tab, §2 and §7, and replaces neither. Today, once the bracket lands, the Overview slot falls back to No overview has been written for this deck yet. with Write one disabled while the press is still writing it. That reads as broken. The slot now has four states of its own: analyzing, writing, written and failed. Each is drawn for the first Analyze press and for a re-analysis after edits. Opening the tab is unchanged: one loader (8a) holds the whole tab until the verdict, the overview and the credit lines are all fetched.
