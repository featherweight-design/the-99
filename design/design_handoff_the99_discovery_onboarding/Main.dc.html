Why a bubble

The brief’s likely shape, kept. It is the only form that adds nothing to a screen’s layout: it sits in a top layer, positioned to its control in page coordinates, so it scrolls with the page and never pushes, wraps or reflows a row. A stacked card, a strip or a banner all take height, and the deck page has none to give. Spotlights stay with the tour.

No new component family: it is the FactTooltip and InfoTip surface (--elevated, a hairline, a soft shadow) with the account menu’s diamond and a nib, so it reads as part of the app rather than an overlay on it.

Anchor
The control the tool is reached by on that screen: a door, a tab, a shortcut, a marker. Never a new control. The anchor for each moment is named on its board.
Placement
Below the control, 8px from it, centred and clamped 16px from the screen’s edges; the nib stays on the control’s centre. Above it when below would leave the viewport or cross the phone’s tab bar. It never covers its own control.
Layer
A top-layer popover, absolute in page coordinates. Never in the flow and never fixed. It sits under sheets, dialogs, menus and toasts.
When it arrives
The first time its condition holds AND its control is on screen, once the page has settled (content and art landed) plus 600ms. Not while anything is open over the page: a sheet, a dialog, a menu, a toast, or on Home the banner. A control below the fold waits until the player scrolls it into view on that visit.
Arrival motion
Fade and 4px rise over 200ms on --motion-ease-out, the menu’s entrance; the halo fades in with it. Under reduced motion: no rise, the fade only.
What a tap does
The bubble’s text is not a button. The control it points at is the action: pressing it opens the tool exactly as it always does, and the bubble goes. ✕ closes it. Esc, a press anywhere else, scrolling the control out of view, or leaving the page closes it too. One action, one control: no “Show me” beside the thing it would press.
Spent
A moment is spent once it has been on screen, whatever closed it, and never returns (brief lock 3). Its tool stays in the showcase and the reference.
Credits
Every anchor is a door (a tile, a tab, a shortcut, a door button), never a run press, so no moment can spend. Suggested changes, Proposed cuts, Help me choose and Find a deck all charge inside their own panel, behind the press the player makes there.
Leaving
140ms fade (--motion-exit-quick); instant under reduced motion. Nothing it covered has moved, so nothing settles back.
Screen readers
A labelled, non-modal note, linked from its control by aria-describedby while it shows, and spoken once on arrival through a polite live region. Focus never moves. ✕ is a real button named “Dismiss”, next in tab order after the control. The halo is decoration.
Switch
A moment exists only when its tool’s switch is on for that reader (lock 4). Off, there is no moment and no record of one (S2).
Q2Which moment wins: the first whose control comes on screen, then a fixed order
A visit
One app session: from opening The 99 until it has been left for 30 minutes. One moment per visit at most, across every screen, so a player who opens three decks still sees one line. The 30 minutes is a constant, not a lock.
The rule
Of the moments whose condition holds, the first whose control comes on screen takes the visit. When two controls are on screen on the same load, the fixed order on the right decides.
The loser waits
A moment that loses is not spent. It keeps its claim and fires on a later visit when its condition still holds. A trigger tied to one visit carries forward: Suggested changes’ “first open at 100” becomes the first visit at 100 a moment is free to use.
Why not newest or cheapest
“Newest condition” needs a timestamp per condition that the data does not keep (a deck reaching 100 and a row crossing its limit can land in the same edit). “Cheaper tool first” would still tie between two free tools. A fixed order is one list, testable, and teaches in the order a deck is made.
Home
The Home banner is the announcement channel (lock 8) and wins Home. While it shows, no moment draws there; the Friends Home card waits for a load without it.

Drawn on S3: a deck at 99 / 99 that also has a Shopping row over its limit. Suggested changes’ control, the Analysis tab, is on screen on load; Cheaper stand-ins’ control is only on the Shopping view. Suggested changes takes the visit and stand-ins waits.

Q8The Friends Home card: the one moment drawn as a card

Below Commitments in Home’s right column at desktop, and last at 390, after Commitments. It is a Home card like the others (radius 14, --surface-inset, the section label above it), with a small table of seats in commander art: yours filled, three dashed. One press, Add a friend, the /friends page’s own word, opens /friends. ✕ in its corner ends it for good. It shows once a deck exists, with the switch on, and never to a player who already has a friend. Drawn on M16.
