Discovery R3 · the first-run tour and tools where the need is · 1280 and 390 · Amonkhet

One tour, once. After it, a tool waits where the need is

The tour shows the app’s shape once, over first-run Home, in six spotlit steps, and ends on the Home banner and the note from the creator. After that, nothing new interrupts: a tool shows up only as a short door inside a figure or an empty state the screen already draws, and only while its switch is on. This is the last round of the line. It builds on R1’s moments and R2’s showcase and “?”, and changes neither.

Q7The tour (brief §5, Open 7)
C1When it opens: the first Home visit, not the first sign-in
It opens on a new account’s first visit to Home, once the page has settled plus 600ms (R1’s arrival rule). For a new account that is the first-run checklist.
  • Shown once. It is spent when step 1 appears, whatever ends it, kept per account like a moment.
  • It takes the visit. No R1 moment arrives on a visit the tour ran (R1 Q2: one per visit).
  • Only new accounts. Accounts that exist when it ships are marked seen and never get it (G24).
Why not first sign-in: a first sign-in can land on a friend’s invite link or a shared deck, and every spotlight points at Home. Home is the one screen every new account shares.
C2How many steps: six
Collection, Commanders, Decks, Buying, Pick your look, What’s new. Each is a title and one or two sentences.
  • No welcome step. Step 1 says how long it is: Six stops · about a minute · ✕ skips the tour.
  • Precons are inside step 1, not a step. The way in already sits on step 1’s card (Precons I own), so the spotlight shows it and one line names it. With PRECONS_ENABLED off, the line goes.
  • The note closes step 6 rather than adding a seventh (R2b: the tour ends on it).
C3How skip reads: a bare ✕
Every card has a bare ✕ in its corner, named Skip the tour. Esc does the same. There is no Skip text button and no confirm (one control per action; close is a bare ✕).
  • Skip and finish end alike: the tour is spent and Home is exactly as it was. No toast, no “you can find it in…”.
  • The note stays at the foot of the Reference (R2b) for a player who skipped.
C4The spotlight: a scrim, a cutout, R1’s halo as its ring
  • A scrim at 66% over the whole app, rail and tab bar included, with a cutout around the real control: 5px of room on a slot, 8px on a card.
  • The cutout carries R1’s halo (2px accent at 75%, 6px at 20%), so a lit control reads as the same family as a moment’s, at a different volume.
  • Everything under the scrim is inert, the lit control too. The tour shows the app; it never presses it. Only the card is live.
  • Between steps the cutout glides 240ms on --motion-ease-out; under reduced motion it cuts.
  • The card is a modal dialog with focus held in it; the lit control is named in its description. Spotlights are the tour’s alone (lock 6).
C5Stops that point at locked slots carry a picture
At zero cards Commanders and Decks are locked, so their pages would show a lock. Their cards carry a real staging capture of what opens there, captioned once your cards are in. Buying has no slot of its own. It lives inside each deck, so its spotlight stays on Decks and its picture is a deck’s Shopping.
C6Pick your look: the account menu’s own picker, and the app is the preview
  • The six ThemeMiniatures from the account menu: 3×2 at 1280, 2×3 at 390, the same radiogroup and arrow keys.
  • The pick saves through the same applyTheme (the the99_theme cookie, this browser). There is no Save; the status line says First Frost applied.
  • Selecting repaints the whole app behind the scrim, so the live preview is the real app in that theme.
  • The spotlight sits on the account button, where the picker lives from then on.
C7The ending: the Home banner, then the note
Step 6 lights the Home banner, the new account’s line What The 99 does:, and says it is where new things are announced (lock 9). Under a rule, the card ends on A note from the creator: its opening line and Read the note ›, as R2b promised.
  • Finish ends the tour. Read the note › ends it and opens the note (a dialog at 1280, a sheet at 390).
  • Friends is not named. The Friends section belongs to Home’s dashboard, and a new account’s Home is the checklist, so there is nothing beside the banner to point at (G25).
C8G1: Help me choose gets a door on /commanders
The door goes at the end of /commanders’ guided banner, where a player with no decks already stands: Not sure which? Help me choose →. It follows HELP_ME_CHOOSE_ENABLED (E2). R1’s M12 points at it instead of at Discover in the rail. Its trigger, first /commanders visit with no decks, is exactly when the banner shows, so the moment and its control now arrive together. The rest of R1 is unchanged.
Q4Tools where the need is (brief §3)
C9What a nudge looks like beside its figure
Two or three words and an arrow, in the accent, in a slot the figure already has. It follows the two shipped ones, consider → and balance mana →.
  • The figure becomes the press and opens the tool. A credit tool lands on its tile, and nothing runs.
  • Present while the figure is short; gone the moment it is met.
  • Switch off means absent: the slot shows what it shows today, a dash or the shipped line.
  • Nothing is added to a layout. The role cell’s dash and the Money card’s Shopping line are already there. The deck page gains no row, chip or line (brief §Out of scope).
C10An empty state names the tool that fills it
One quiet line under the empty state’s own buttons, in the shipped door shape (Not sure what you want? Help me choose →, already on /discover). It is never a second button and never a card. Switch off: the line is absent, and the empty state is as shipped.
