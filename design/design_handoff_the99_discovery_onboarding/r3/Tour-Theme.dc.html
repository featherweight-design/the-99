Pick your look: the account menu’s picker, with the whole app as its preview

The card holds the same six miniatures the account menu draws, and selecting one runs the same applyTheme: data-theme on the page, the the99_theme cookie for this browser, and a status line. The page behind the scrim repaints at once, so a player sees their real Home in the theme before going on. The light is on the account button, where the picker lives after the tour.

The same picker, not a copy The miniatures are ThemeMiniature with data-theme on each, as in the popover (3×2, 46px tall) and the sheet (2×3, 52×38 beside the name, a 44px minimum). Selected is the accent ring and the tick, never colour alone. Saving Selecting is saving, exactly as in the menu: no Save, no confirm. Next keeps what is selected. Skipping keeps it too; nothing is rolled back. The pick is per browser, as the themes entry says. The live preview The theme applies to the document, so Home, the rail or tab bar, the scrim’s ring and the card all change together. The card’s own fonts swap to the theme’s display face. The six faces load when the step opens, as the menu loads them when it opens. What is not here No second picker, no new setting and no row in the account menu (one place for a setting). The step only puts the existing picker in front of a new player once.
