One line for every card row

A fast follow to the foil mark addendum’s two-line row (#1886). Hugh’s verdict on it live: the information is worth having, but two lines read empty on desktop for a light row, and one line used to get cluttered on a heavy one. This addendum puts the deck page’s card row back on one 34px line from 768px up, in All and Shopping, and makes it hold the busiest real row by giving each view its own lead, folding marks in a fixed order as the row narrows, and fixing the right-hand columns. The phone keeps today’s rows. Drawn over the shipped deck page, Kamigawa theme, at shipped row metrics. Dashed pink marks what is new.

The calls
#Call
L1One line from 768px up, in All and Shopping. The desktop row is 34px again, the shipped one-line metrics (9px padding, 10px gap, 7px radius). The two-line desktop forms retire, and so does Shopping’s phone-shaped band from 768 to 1024.
L2Per-view lead. All: left run, slot, pips, ✕. Shopping: left run, marker, press, buy tag, ✕. All has no other-ways press; Shopping has no pips and no EDHREC figure.
L3Drop order by the row’s own width, in three tiers: wide (480px and up), middle (360 to 479), tight (under 360). The lists are on board D1. The name ellipsizes; nothing else shrinks.
L4+N: bare mono 11px in --fact, after the marks it stands for. Fine pointer: the fact tooltip lists what folded. Press: the row’s own press, card details. Screen readers still hear every mark.
L5The glint takes the filled dot’s place on one line (3a), centred on the dot so the name keeps its x. Only where the dot would be filled; a dashed or proxied row draws no glint. Under the dot on the phone, as shipped.
L6Fixed right-hand columns: All’s pip track (72px) and ✕; Shopping’s buy tag (84px) and ✕. The marker, the press and All’s price flow left of them and take width only when present.
L7Full name on hover is the shipped card preview (240ms in, 252×352). No name tooltip, so one hover never opens two popups.
L8The press keeps its 26px box and takes 3px off each side of the line, so a row holding it stays 34px.
L9Unchanged: over the limit is the tag’s outline and the key’s over $5; both keys as shipped; a basic land’s stepper row, a precon’s read-only row, and the removal and I got it slots (34px on desktop again).
L10The phone stays as it ships: the 52px All row and option B Shopping, except that All loses the press (L2). One line at 358px leaves a 40px press, a 40px tag and a 44px ✕ beside about 90px of name.
Engineering

decks/decklist-row.tsx: delete TWO_MD, TWO_LG and TWO_SHELLS; from md both views use the one-line shell (ROW_SHELL, 34px), and ROW_SHELL_SHOPPING’s lg becomes md. PHONE_B stays below md.
The grid cell becomes a container (@container); the folds are @max-[479px]: and @max-[359px]: variants on the marks, the +N spans and the marker’s word.
Marks render whole, each with its index; three +N spans (past the third, past the first, all) carry the counts, one shown per tier.
OtherWaysPress draws only inShopping. Pips draw only outside Shopping, in the desktop track. The press gets md:-my-[3px].
The ownership slot draws FoilGlint instead of OwnershipDot when the dot would be filled and the row has a foil finish; the glint’s -2px margin keeps the 8px slot.
AvailabilityMarker needs a hook to hide its word and keep the glyph and count (its tooltip and spoken label stay).
