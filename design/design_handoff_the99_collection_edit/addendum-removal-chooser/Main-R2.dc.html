Choose which deck takes the loss, every case

R2 keeps R1's chooser and your one-copy-left rule, and draws the rest of the matrix: one deck, two, three or more, several copies, and the read-only receipt, at phone and desktop. The Warning board plays every scene.

1. Your rule standsThe chooser shows only on an Edit copies removal or lowering, when two or more decks list the card and exactly one copy is left. A card holds one reservation, so one keeper is all a pick can store. No per-deck count steppers.
2. Tapping a deck row never leaves the app (#2982)A one-card row carries what you'd open the deck to learn: lists N · committed it <date>. In the chooser, a tap picks. Rows with several cards (Replace, Sync, Revert) open inline, the whole row with a chevron, and each card shows its own facts (P7, D6). No navigation anywhere.
3. Three or more decksOne copy left: square marks, pick the ones going short (P3, D3). Two or more left: no chooser. The warning names who keeps the copies and why, and points to Change on the card (the shipped Move a copy), so you're never locked in (P5, D4).
4. Several copies, counts per deckThe figure on the row is the count, capped by what that deck lists (short 1, short 2). A deck listing 2 is still one row, as R1's gap G1 notes.
5. The I got it receipt is read-onlyIts Undo is a whole-action revert, so no chooser. The short deck shows its facts line, and one line says why it's that deck and where to change it (P6, D5).
6. Undo of a removalPuts the previous reservations back. No new UI: the shipped Undo toast, and the deck that went short is whole again.
