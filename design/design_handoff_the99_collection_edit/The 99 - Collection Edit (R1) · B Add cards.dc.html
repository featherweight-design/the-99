A side sheet you can keep typing into, a list you can fix before it saves, and one save.

Design's call — a 480px right side sheet on desktop, not a centred dialog. Adding is a session of ten or forty cards, and the collection behind it should stay visible: the grid is how a player checks “did I already enter that?”. A side sheet leaves 760px of the list in view at 1300. A dialog would cover it, and would make the sheet's growing list compete with its own search. On the phone it's full height, as the brief says.

Design's call — the exact same card, printing and finish entered twice is combined, not flagged. The second entry adds its quantity to the existing line, the line moves to the top and pulses once, and the live region says Already on the list · now ×2. A flag would ask a question with one sensible answer. A different printing or finish is a different holding and always gets its own line.

The printing is the shipped PrintingRegion, opened under its line in a choose role. It has the same tiles, search, finish chips and roving radios. Three things differ, and all three are props, not a fork. The list is every catalog printing, not owned ones only. The Your pick and Shown now badges are withheld, because they mean art. And the intro reads Which printing are you adding?. Nothing in this sheet writes an art preference.

B4a · how a line gets here · the printing is chosen before the line exists

The printing is a required step, with no default (Hugh, 2026-10-09). A guessed printing that's saved in a hurry would be fixed later in History. It's cheaper to ask now. Picking a result puts the line on the list with its printing picker open and nothing selected. The card search is paused until a printing is chosen. Choosing one completes the line at nonfoil, ×1, and hands focus back to the search. A card with only one printing skips the step, because there's nothing to choose. Foil and quantity are the only defaults left: both are visible on the line, and neither depends on telling printings apart.
