History is a fourth tab: one line per thing you did, newest first, each one undoable from today.

Design's call — a tab, Cards · Sets · Proxies · History, at /collection/history. It's a place in the collection, reached and left the way the other three are, and Back is a route change (R1 call 4). It joins the (list) group, so the strip persists, and placeFromPathname gains "history". A link from the stats line was the alternative. It would put the only way to undo an upload behind a 12px mono phrase, and the phone has no stats line at all. History is absent, not disabled on an empty collection with no entries, the same rule as Sets.

An entry is one row: when, what kind, what changed, and Revert. Its kind is a fixed-width mono tag (150px desktop), wide enough for the longest kind and for one more later (From Shopping, sub-project 4, drawn ghosted). The summary is always +N · −N copies across M cards, and it expands to every card. A revert and the entry it undid link to each other. Either side drops its link once the other ages out, but keeps its words.
