What moves
  • Under the count: the last N (A8, A17 first run), over the 99 (A4), fill failed and ran out (A9, the plate). One line, one press.
  • Header chip + list rows: spoken for twice (A6). The chip presses to Shopping filtered to those cards. Unresolved (A15) is a dashed row at the foot of the list, where the lines already sit.
  • Save dock: pending changes (A14), always on screen while a draft is open; it lifts above the phone tab bar. The quiet version strip moves to the Versions tab label (“v2 + draft”).
  • Versions tab: provenance (A7) becomes that tab’s first section. Row marks: proxies (A3, a P), other drafts (A6 soft, the hollow glyph), each in the list key.
  • Toasts: arrivals, update, remove failure, the precon suggestion.
  • Cost: it is the biggest change: a new dock, a new chip, new row marks and a header line, and the brief’s ‘one place to look’ becomes ‘look where it applies’. The dock covers the last list rows until you scroll.
