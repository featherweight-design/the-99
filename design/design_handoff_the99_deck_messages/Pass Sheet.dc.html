Show these opens a sheet of the deck’s own rows

Ruled 2026-10-22: the sheet. The foot’s Show these N (and ‘N in other drafts too’) opens the shipped ModalChrome sheet at 390 and modal at 1300. Its rows are the deck page’s own card row, DecklistRow in the All view, mounted unchanged: ownership slot, name, the auto tag, the bare $ and the fact marks, the 72px pip track, the ✕, the marks folding by the row’s width, the preview on hover. A press opens the shipped card detail, stacked on the sheet, so its close reads ← 1st top-off and closing it lands back on the sheet (registerSheet’s back label). The accent-edge PassMark retires. No row drift
  • The sheet renders DecklistRow itself with the All view’s props, inside the deck page’s providers (optimistic deck, card detail, locks, foil, combo partners), never a copy or a trimmed row. Whatever the list row draws today or later, the sheet draws.
  • The rows sit at the sheet’s inner width, so FOLD’s container query picks the tier (390: middle; 1300 modal: wide), exactly as a list row of that width would.
  • The ✕ removes the card from the deck, as in the list; the row leaves the sheet and the count in the head drops. Undo these N stays in the sheet’s foot; the ledger line keeps Show these and Undo these.
  • Guard: an inventory (docs/superpowers/inventories/pass-sheet.json) whose shares.module names DecklistRow for the deck list and the sheet, so pnpm check:shared fails if the sheet ever imports anything else; the contact sheet photographs both at 390 and 1300.
  • Fixture: Quandrix Lab (Zimone) so the opened card can be a real card image (Sai). Prices are placeholders.
