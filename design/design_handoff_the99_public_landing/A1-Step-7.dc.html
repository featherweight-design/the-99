What changed in step 7

  • Step 7 now runs on two of Sam’s other decks, with a switch at the top of the app: Giada is 103 cards, 4 over, so Proposed cuts and Look deeper show; Zimone is 93 cards, 6 open, so Top off and Mana Balance show. Look deeper is the default view.
  • The switch is two tabs, read-only like every other press in the demo. Its words are the deck names and their counts, nothing new to review.
  • Top off is the shipped list open (top-off-list.tsx): Choose the last 6, the two select presses, the ranked rows (tick, ownership dot, name, cost, synergy, decks, price or held mark), and the commit row Add 6 cards with 99 of 99 when added. Desktop adds the column header and the type column, as shipped. Ticking works; Add is inert.
  • Mana Balance now has a real fix: Blue is 15 of the 17 sources its costs need, so Improve my mana offers Up to 2 Forests → Islands (Swap basics) and a staple the deck runs, Yavimaya Coast, owned and free, with Trade 1 Forest for it. Both presses are inert.
  • The step’s caption is unchanged: Over 99, a deeper look names the cuts. Under 99, Top off fills it from Sam’s cards. Mana Balance checks the lands.
  • Step 4 keeps Ashling at 99 / 99. Step 3’s Help me choose line for Ashling now reads 97 of the 99 are in Sam’s binder, matching step 4.

Strings that are new

  • None the copy table doesn’t already hold: every Top off and Mana Balance string is the shipped one (top-off.ts, mana-balance.ts). The cut reasons are stand-in words, as in R4; the build replays the stored Look deeper output.
