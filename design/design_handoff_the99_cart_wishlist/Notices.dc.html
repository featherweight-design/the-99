The rules behind it

Roll-up. A friend's new matches on one day are one row: "Colin has spares of 4 cards you want", the line naming two cards and "N more". A match found the next day starts a new row. A friend who stops sharing drops their cards from the row on the next read, as today.

Push. At most one trade-match push a day, from the first check that finds something, in the shipped words: one friend "Colin has 4 spares you want", several "7 spares you want / From Colin, Peter and Max." Held overnight as now (9 p.m. to 9 a.m.).

Switching on, or a big import. No push. The matches it finds land as rolled rows only.

Price drops. Cards your decks put on the wishlist never start a price-drop watch; only Added by you lines do.

Friends. Nothing is sent to anyone when your wishlist grows.

Settings line. Trade matches keeps "A friend has a spare of a card on your wishlist." No new setting.
