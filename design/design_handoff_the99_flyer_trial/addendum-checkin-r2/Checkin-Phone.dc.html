Why a hand of cards

The app is about cards, and a numbered row of boxes read like a form. Five cards held in a fan are still a five-option radio group (arrow keys move, one is always checked once picked), with a word on each so the scale explains itself and the “not well / really well” ends can go.

Why the card back

It sits by the intro from the start, so the thanks is a payoff, not a surprise: you answer, and the card turns over for you. It is the shipped compact card (/join’s invitation) with the crowned face, so no new art and no broken images.
