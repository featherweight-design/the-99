# The 99 — deck notices (design handoff · R1)

> **Start with `HANDOFF.md`.** This file is the short why.

**The problem is voice, not placement.** The deck already has a sentence that says the reading might be out
of date: the banner, in the second person, about your edits, with a bone button. Anything else that says
"your bracket may be off" in a similar box reads as the same message. So the notice borrows nothing from it:
not its place (the tab), not its form (a card with a button), not its ink (bone), not its words (*changes*).
It borrows instead from the bracket chip it qualifies: the same row, the same height and radius, and none of
its plate.

**`--fact` because the notices are facts.** Every data notice is a card fact that moved (a Game Changer, a
flag, a combo) or the rule those facts are read against. The card-fact ink is already that meaning.

**Reading quiets, re-analysis clears.** A player who has read the list has made the decision the brief leaves
to them. Charging a credit to dismiss news would be backwards.

**Withholding the overview** rather than a passable warning: the bracket is free and runs regardless; the
overview is the one costly thing and ruling 6 says it should not be spent on an illegal deck. A confirm on a
free press is friction in the wrong place.

## Tried and dropped

- **A dot on the chip's corner.** Changes the one chip everywhere, Home included.
- **A second line in the banner** when both apply. Makes the two signals one box, which is §4.4's failure.
- **+ / − and green / amber for direction.** The pending banner's vocabulary for your own edits.
- **Notices inside the bracket box.** A second place on the page.
- **Clearing only on re-analysis.** Costs a credit on a changed list.
