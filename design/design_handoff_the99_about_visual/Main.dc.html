About as six pages of real screenshots

From 83 text entries to six pages, each a stack of the app’s own screens with numbered labels, the hidden rules drawn as Good to know cards, and the shipped tool cards. Source: Hugh’s six workshop rulings, the audit’s keep list and the walk’s voice rules (docs/superpowers/about-visual/, PR #2283). Every picture on this canvas is a real capture: phone from public/about/shots/amonkhet, desktop from contact sheets. Words are drafts for /copy-review.

1 · Labels are numbered pins, words beside the picture

Each label is a number on the screenshot and a full sentence in a list: beside the shot on desktop, under it on a phone. No words sit on the picture, so a re-taken capture never re-wraps or covers anything. A pin sits on its region’s top-left corner, and its region is a named element measured at capture (today’s locator boxes), never a pixel position. Pressing a label lights its region with the halo the tool cards already use. Five pins a picture at most; a screen that needs more becomes two shots.

2 · Good to know is a card with a small diagram

Each hidden rule is a card: a diagram drawn from the app’s own shapes (a card row, a deck tile, a buy tag, a bracket medal), then one or two plain sentences. It sits in the section of the screen it belongs to, so the ? lands on a screen’s labels and its rules together. A phone swipes a row of them; desktop shows a grid of three. 15 rules across four pages; Home and Commanders have none.

3 · Six pages and an index, not one scroll

About opens on an index of six tiles, each a crop of its page’s first screen. Every page has the six in a strip at the top (the shipped tab strip) and an “On this page” row of the ? panel’s chips, one per screen. The ? and the banner always land on one area, and one long scroll would load every capture in the app to show one.

4 · About stays where it is in the menu

“About The 99”, first in the account menu, as shipped. No new row. The ? on every screen is the other way in, and the banner the third.

5 · The ? opens its page, at its screen

The ? on any screen opens that area’s page scrolled to that screen, with a “‹ Back to …” bar that stays at the top while you read. The ? panel and its search go: one source, as ruled, and six short pages need no search.

0 · The picture matches the device

A phone reader sees phone captures and a desktop reader desktop captures, everywhere: the pages, the tool card pictures, the index tiles and the banner crop. Each screen is captured at both widths, and its labels are anchored on each capture separately.

6 · Tool cards stay, with Show me on them

The shipped tool card, unchanged but for its left link: “How it works ›” pointed at the Reference, which is gone, so it becomes Show me › (the panel’s shipped Show me: opens the screen and lights the control). The card’s press is still Try it. Each screen is three blocks, in order: the labelled screen, Good to know, then Try it.

7 · The banner names the launch, with a crop

Home’s shipped banner, its diamond mark swapped for a crop of the new label’s region from its screen’s capture. “New on Decks: Matchups splits …” and Show me. Two or more launches use the shipped band, one line and crop each. Show me lands on the page with the label lit, marked New, and its tool card right under it.

8 · States

A label whose feature is off for the reader is gone, pin and line, and the numbers close up. A capture still loading draws its frame at the capture’s known size with a quiet skeleton and no pins; the labels read without it. An area with no hidden rules simply has no Good to know heading: no empty state.

9 · What the captures need (engineering)

Today’s captures don’t all hold every label’s region, and there are no 1300 captures of most screens. not in today’s capture marks each label the capture has to bring into frame; The canvas uses today’s real captures as they are.

Gaps for engineering

G1Capture inventory: every section’s capture holds every label region, at 390 and 1300. Today the phone deck shots are taken with the tabs at the top (no Recommended mix or commit band), the desktop deck shot predates Lock cards, and the two Analysis shots are of different decks.
G2Desktop captures: capture:about shoots 390x844 only. Each section adds a 1300x900 frame, in all six themes.
G3Anchors: each label names a region (data-about-region="…", or today’s locator string), measured per capture into shots.generated.ts as the locator boxes are now. A visible label with no box fails readiness, as today.
G4Seen and launches: the announced unit becomes the label or rule (the audit’s option B), each with its own switch and since. Legacy seen ids need translating so nobody is re-told (README errata 3).
G5Tool cards: Show me targets a label id, not an entry id; the eleven cut entry ids the showcase hangs on need new targets before they go (audit decision 4).
G6Pins: two pins closer than 28px — the later one slides right along its region’s top edge. Two regions that overlap entirely are one label.
