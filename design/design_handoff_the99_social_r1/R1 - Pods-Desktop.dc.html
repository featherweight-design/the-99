A pod is a card: its name, its members as chips, and one checkbox for your spares

Pods sort by name, like folders. The card’s meta line says how many and who owns it (“yours” or “Dave owns it”). An owner sees ✕ on each other member and + Add friends; a member sees chips only. The checkbox is each reader’s own grant for that pod (shareSparesWith), so an owner and a member each have one and neither sees the other’s. Everything a pod can do lives in its … menu: Rename, Leave pod and Delete pod for the owner, Leave pod for a member. Pods grant no visibility by themselves, which is why a member who is not your friend is shown by name and nothing else.
