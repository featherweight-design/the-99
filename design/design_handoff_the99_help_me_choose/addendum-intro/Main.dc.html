An intro at the top: what it does, how it helps, what's free

Today the page opens straight into the form under one line ("Say as much or as little as you like"). Nothing says what comes back, why it's worth answering, or that only one box costs anything. R1 adds a panel between the title and the form, drawn from the shipped components and real copy. Everything below is rendered from a scratch copy of main.

1 · What it does

Kicker "How it works", headline "Describe the deck. See which commanders fit it." and a lead that says how the ranking works: every commander is ranked by how many of its decks match your answers, owned or not, each with a line you can check.

2 · How it helps: an example result

The imagery is a result, not decoration: Ghalta's art over the shipped row's facts ("91% of its decks are Dinosaurs", "you own 79 of its 99 · about $135 to buy"), with 2 and 3 fading under it on desktop. Labelled "Example · Dinosaurs · under $150" so nobody takes it for their own result.

3 · Three steps

Answer what you like (every answer optional) · See who fits (closest fits lead, each with the figure that ranks it) · Build it (pick one and a deck starts from your cards, the rest priced). Step 3 is the part the page never mentions today.

4 · Free vs 1 credit

Two tiles. "Free, every time": your answers, the ranked results and every past run in History. "1 credit · Anything else?": only when you write in that box; empty means nothing is spent. Under it: "1 free credit a day, shared by every tool" (from the reader's real daily limit) and "Go to Anything else? ↓", which jumps to the box. Matches the landing page's "Free, always" / "With credits" split.

5 · Out of the way once learned

Open until the player's first run, then folded to one 44px line: "How Help me choose works · Free unless you write in Anything else? ›", which opens it again. Hide folds it at once. Results, History and the form are unchanged.

6 · Build notes

New ChooseIntro component and its own copy file (help-me-choose-intro.ts); no em dashes; never the words AI or model. History lists only runs that read words, so the fold needs its own per-account "seen" mark, like the welcome strip's dismiss, set on the first Find commanders.
