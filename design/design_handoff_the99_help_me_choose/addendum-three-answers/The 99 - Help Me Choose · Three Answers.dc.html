Any of these themes, printed in these sets, built from what I own.

The worked example · Hugh’s query, which returned nothing “I want a deck from the HOB or HOC sets using cards I own. The commander can be one I have to buy. Tribal options are Orcs, Elves, Dwarves, or Wizards.” Today the four tribes are read as all four at once, and no commander backs more than two. HOB or HOC and cards I own have no answer to land in, so they end up under Not used. With this addendum, the free match turns the whole sentence into three chips. No model call is added.

The three chips, and what they add to a row

1 · themes, any of any ofOrcsorElvesorDwarvesorWizards One chip for the whole group, kind any of, with a mono or between names. All-of stays as today: one theme chip each. On a row, the theme it fits best is lit and tagged SETS RANK. Any others of the group it fits are dimmed, and ones it doesn’t fit aren’t shown.
2 · sets printed inHOBorHOC On the chip, codes only. It’s what the player typed and what the Set panel rows carry, and it keeps the chip one width for one set or five. The full names are in the accessible name and in Change answers. Sets are always or, so there’s no join control. There’s no count beside it: the results print no total. On a row it’s a mono code tag after the pips.
3 · build from what I own the 99from cards you owncommander owned or not It scopes the 99 and nothing else. Owned and unowned commanders stay listed together, and the tail says so. It never narrows the list or ranks it. On a row, the commander’s mark and price come first, then 99 from your cards. The budget then caps the commander only.
