Lock cards, in the real toolbar

R2 drew Lock cards in a mock toolbar. This addendum places it in group-toggle.tsx as shipped: the filter pills, cluster 2 (act on the cards), the hairline, then cluster 3 (how the list reads: grouping, sort, view well). It also draws Hugh's L3 note on Clear all auto. Nothing else in R2 changes, and the lock glyph and its inks are R2's. The fixture is HD Yuriko: 40 auto cards, of which Lotus Petal and Ornithopter are locked, and 59 kept.

§

The calls

1 · Last in cluster 2, after Clear all auto

Locking acts on the cards, so it goes on the acting side of the hairline, not the reading side. It sits last, so the tested Keep → Clear order reads unchanged and the pair stays adjacent. On desktop the cluster is pushed right by the spacer, so the button sits just left of the hairline. It is one more child inside the cluster's own wrapper, so the row still wraps between clusters and never inside one.

2 · The bulk buttons' skin and height, not the pills'

It takes BULK_BUTTON_CLASS verbatim (mono 12, --fg-body, --border-strong, radius 8, px 12). On the phone that is min-h 44, the same as Keep and Clear beside it. The ~26px pills are filters, and this is a control on a thumb's line. On desktop it's py 6, about 30px, level with the pair. The icon is R2's closed lock in --fg-muted. The button is min-w 116, so Done takes exactly the same box.

3 · Done: in place, and nothing reflows

In the mode the label becomes Done and the text, border and icon turn --accent (R2). Keep all and Clear all auto stay in place, disabled at the shipped 50%. That way no line of the toolbar comes or goes, and Done stays under the thumb that pressed Lock cards. Disabled, not hidden: a sweep mid-mode would remove cards the player is deciding about. While Keep all's question is open, Lock cards stands down with the pair, because the question replaces the cluster's controls.

4 · 390: no new line, in either deck state

With auto cards, cluster 2 is Keep 40 · Clear auto · 38 · Lock cards: 76 + 134 + 116 + two 8px gaps = 342 of the 358px line. Counts never pass two digits, so it can't grow. That's three lines, as shipped. With no auto cards, Lock cards joins the pills' line (153 + 7 + 116 = 276), so it's two lines, as shipped. The view well keeps its line and its right edge (4b).

5 · Shopping hides it

Hidden under Shopping for the view well's reason: it's absent there, not disabled. Shopping is a list of things to buy, not the deck's composition. The at-rest locks still show on Shopping rows, and the desktop hover shortcut still works there (R2 2e). Switching to Shopping while in the mode ends the mode. Switching between List and Card keeps it, because it's the same toolbar and the same deck (4c).

6 · The mode's line takes the key's slot

MarkKey renders in its own wrapper under the toolbar (px 16 pt 12 / md px 26 pt 14, empty:hidden). In the mode the key hides and the line renders in that same wrapper: the same position, the same insets, above the receipt and the groups. It's one sentence in one place. When a deck had no key entries, the wrapper appears for the mode and goes with it.

7 · Clear all auto skips locked cards (L3)

Its label counts what it will clear: Clear all auto · 38, beside Keep all 40. The two now count different sets on purpose: a locked auto card can be kept but not cleared. A 40 that clears 38 would be the false count this toolbar forbids. The receipt names what stayed: 38 auto cards removed · 2 locked cards stayed. When every remaining auto card is locked, Clear is absent (absent at zero), and Keep all stays (4d).

What changes in group-toggle.tsx

Cluster 2's gate and the hairline's gate each gain filter === "all", so on All both always render. The wrapper's phone rule hidden md:flex at a zero auto count gives way when Lock cards is present, and the kept note stays desktop-only. Cluster 3, the spacer and the well are untouched. At the narrowest desktop widths the row wraps whole clusters, as it does today. Audit F7's probe covers it.
