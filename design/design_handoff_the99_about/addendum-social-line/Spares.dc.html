NEWWhen a friend has a spare

/decks opens a deck’s Shopping filter

Before you buy a card, check whether a friend has one sitting idle. On a deck’s “Shopping” list, a card a friend can spare gets the friends mark beside its price. Press it to see who has one, the pod they share it with, and how many.

A spare is a copy none of their decks use, drafts included. It’s view only, so the trade happens at the table.

Your own spares stay private until you tick “Share my spares with this pod” on one of your pods in “Friends”.

  1. ·You only see spares from friends who share them with a pod you’re both in.
  2. ·When cheaper stand-ins exist too, the same window opens as “Before you buy”, friends first.
  3. ·A spare changes nothing: no price, no cart, and no card moves into your deck.
  4. ·The same mark shows on the cart for all your decks.
