The calls

1. Not one group. Each entry sits where its feature lives.

About is ordered by what a reader is doing, in four chapters, and every entry since R6 stays in the chapter of its task (R6 H3, the launch addendum). The Social line touches three tasks, so its entries go to three chapters: the wishlist is a Collection tab, a friend’s spare is met on Shopping beside the stand-ins, and friends and opponents are about who you play with.

Rejected: a fifth chapter, “Playing with friends”. The wishlist works with no friends at all (its switch stands alone), so it would sit in a friends chapter while describing a solo tool. A chapter that half-fills per switch reads broken, and AboutChapter is a closed set of four that the rail, the sheet and search are built on.

2. The order inside each chapter

Keeping a wishlist closes chapter 01, after Undoing a change to your Collection, as its tab follows History in the strip.

When a friend has a spare follows Cheaper stand-ins in 03: the spare press opens the stand-ins modal, and the two read best side by side.

Friends and pods then Who you played against follow What your games are telling you in 04. Logging a game stays the chapter’s first entry; the two social entries read as a pair, and the second leans on the first (friends, pods, Seat a pod).

3. Switches, and what a reader is told

Friends and the spare entry follow SOCIAL_FRIENDS_ENABLED, the wishlist SOCIAL_WISHLIST_ENABLED, opponents SOCIAL_GAMES_ENABLED. Each is announced by its id when its switch flips, so since: 14 and no bump, as deck-tokens and proposed-cuts did.

Flipped together at the line’s launch, Home’s banner names up to three titles and and 1 more, as for any batch. Lines that name another switch’s feature (a friend’s wishlist, stand-ins, the cart, pods in the game log) are gated per line with captionLineSwitches and keySwitches, so no entry ever names a surface the reader can’t open.

4. Specimens, and what each needs from engineering

S1 mounts PodCard as shipped, with fixture props and no-op handlers. S4 mounts GameMatchups as shipped. S2 and S3 mount parts the R2 plan is building (PRs 4 and 8): the Wishlist tab’s tile, and the stand-ins modal’s friends section. Each must be exported unchanged so the tab, the modal and this page render one tree (E1, E2 on the boards). Their entries ship with R2’s last PR, never before the surface does.
