What D1 says, and why

What goes is the purge, in a player's words: Collection and wishlist; every deck with its versions, games and notes; friends, pods and friend links; tool results, credits and settings. DRAFT
The pods line is the one effect on other people: A pod you own passes to its longest-standing member. Friends aren't told; you leave their lists. DRAFT
What a plan subscriber is told: Your plan ends now and won't renew. Deleting the Stripe customer cancels the subscription at once; there is nothing to manage first. The refund line names a plan's first month too, because the shipped refunds rule refunds it within 14 days.
