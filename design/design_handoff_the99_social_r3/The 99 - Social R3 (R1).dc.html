Opponents and matchups: the table you sat at, inside the form you already use

Logging a game gains one optional row, Across the table, between Pod size and the marks. It draws the table as art seats: you first, then each opponent, then one dashed seat while the pod has room. One picker fills a seat with a friend, a friend’s deck, or anyone by commander. On a loss each seated opponent carries a trophy; one press says who won. The Games tab gains Matchups between the findings and History, one list at a time (Commander, Colors, Friend), each row a result square per game and a count. No rate, no finding, no model.
