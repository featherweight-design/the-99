inputs are Round 2's to confirm.

- **Placement.** Analysis is the second tab. Both live options are drawn side by side (`1a`, `1b`)
  so the rejection is visible, not asserted.
- **What moves.** The mix strip, whole and unmodified. The art band and version line stay.
- **The fold.** One comparator line per block. Three strips are distinguishable at a glance.
- **The synopsis's slot and its five states**, including the verdict's independence from them.

- **Denominators.** Every figure on the canvas prints one in its own cell.

0. **`--bracket-1…5` are proposed and drawn for two themes.** The *rule* is settled — 3 is the
   theme&rsquo;s accent, 1–2 cool beneath, 4–5 hot above — so the remaining four themes are a colour pass,
   not a design one. Worth a review at thumbnail size: the crown must stay countable at 5px.

1. **The ladder's allowances** (*none at 1–2 · 3 at Upgraded*, *none below Optimized*). Which
   brackets each row lights is drawn from the spec's implied rules; the real limits table is Round
   2's. **A data correction, not a redraw** — the ladder's geometry holds whatever the table says,
   and a changed allowance changes which cells light, nothing else.
2. **Synopsis length.** Drawn at four sentences on desktop, three lines on phone. If the accept
   contract permits longer, this block needs a clamp rather than a bigger box.
3. **The violation shape** (section D, fourth card) is reserved, dashed, and has no copy. If Round 2
   decides a violation belongs somewhere other than the verdict block, that card is discarded and
   nothing else moves.

## Red — nothing

No blocking unknown. The frame does not depend on the bracket rule set being final; it depends only
on a verdict being a number or a range, which §6.1a locks.

## What would change this frame rather than fill it in

- **If the mix strip may not move.** Then the tab holds the verdict, receipts and synopsis only, the
  page does not get shorter, and the five-tabs argument has to be made on its own merits. This is the
  one assumption the whole composition rests on.
- **If analysis must be tied to a committed version.** The live-list ruling is load-bearing for the
  denominators, the staleness rule and the mid-build case.
- **If a blended score is wanted after all.** Then §2.2 changes, and so does every block here.
