The 99 · Readability amendment

Commander art wash — scrim system

The wash is right. What broke is that the art was let through at full luminance, and the whole card image was used instead of the art crop — so Magic's own printed title and type line land in exactly the band our labels occupy. Four rules below fix both surfaces without giving up the wash.

What's going wrong
01 · WRONG SOURCE
Full card, not art crop
The gold frame, printed name and type line ride along and collide with our own type at the same size.
02 · NO SCRIM
Art at full luminance
Highlights in Aang's fire and Eldrazi's fog reach L*80+. No amount of white text survives that.
03 · ALPHA INK
Translucent text and chips
“Complete” and the overflow button are alpha fills over art, so the art reads through the chip.
04 · WASH TOO FAR
Art behind dense data
On commander rows the wash runs under stat pills, the synergy bar and the receipts line — all of which need flat ground.
The scrim system · four rules
Rule 1 · Art crop only
Always image_uris.art_crop (626×457), object-fit:cover. Never normal, large or png. On a modal DFC, crop the front face.
NORMAL — FRAME + TYPE LINE
ART_CROP — CLEAN
Rule 2 · Three layers, this order
Dim the art and cover it. Dimming alone loses the drama; covering alone leaves hotspots.
L1
Base plate #0E0C09 — under the art, so a slow or missing image never flashes light ground.
L2
Art at opacity .55 — keeps hue, drops luminance globally.
L3
Gradient scrim — heavy at the label bands, thin in the middle third so the art still breathes.
linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(14,12,9,.90) 0%, rgba(14,12,9,.34) 38%, rgba(14,12,9,.66) 78%, #14110E 100%)
Rule 3 · Full-opacity ink
Nothing over art gets an alpha colour. Use solid brand ink plus a shadow to seat it.
Title over art#F4EFE3 · 100%
Subtitle over art#C6B79A · 100%
Shadow on both0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.6)
Chips on art#14110E + 1px #3A3229
Contrast floor over the scrimmed art: 7:1 for titles, 4.5:1 for meta, measured against the art's brightest pixel — the scrim caps it at L*34.
Rule 4 · Data lives on flat ground
The wash owns one band. Below the hairline divider, everything sits on solid card ground — no exceptions for pills, bars, prices or receipts.
✓
Deck name, subtitle, status chip, overflow — in the wash.
✕
Colour pips, card count, completion bar, price, last-played, stat pills, synergy bar, receipts — below the divider.
divider: 1px solid #2A241D
ground: #1A160F

Dev notes
One shared component. Both surfaces should render the same <ArtWash> — art url, height, and a band prop for the label zone. Divergent scrims are how this drifts back.
No art is a valid state. Missing art_crop falls back to the existing colour-identity gradient at the same opacity — same scrim, no layout shift.
Respect reduced-transparency. Under prefers-reduced-transparency or high-contrast, drop L2 to opacity .3 and push L3's stops to .95 / .6 / .85.
Don't scrim with blur. backdrop-filter on the label band was the other candidate — it costs a compositing layer per card in a 40-deck grid and still doesn't fix hotspot luminance.
