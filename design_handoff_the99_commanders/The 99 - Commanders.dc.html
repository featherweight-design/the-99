/commanders
The 99 — Commanders surface · Hi-fi

Who should lead your 99?

The bridge between Collection and Decks: a ranked list of the generals your collection is already ready to build — and every recommendation explains itself all the way down to the named cards the strength comes from, so a skeptical player can see the receipts and trust the order. Open any general in full and step into building. Built mobile-first against the locked brand system + app shell; each state leads with the phone, desktop beside it. Decisions log + token-referenced notes in The 99 - Commanders README.md.

List treatment
Ranked recommendation cards — each one carries its own evidence. Order stays opinionated.
Score model
Fit /100, a brass bar split into Pool · Roles · Synergy so the number shows where it comes from.
Receipts
Named cards everywhere — a preview on each card, the full list (30+) on detail. No unexplained scores.
Detail container
A dedicated page, not a modal — it's a decision surface and the launch pad into deck-building.
Degraded
Ranking survives without EDHREC — globally (banner + 2-part scores) or for one general (inline note).
Steering
One control — a color-identity filter. It narrows the field; it never re-scores.
