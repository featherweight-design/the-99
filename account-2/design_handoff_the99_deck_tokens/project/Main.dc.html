Make the deck’s Tokens easier to notice

Tokens stays at the foot of Your deck. Every board is the shipped deck page rendered from main (Ashen Banner Parade, real token art), with only the parts named below changed. BEFORE rows are main as it ships today.

T1–T3 · the Tokens box (every concept)

The group becomes a deck box (the Recommended mix and Announced box): a ◆ band with the count and “not in the 99 · from N cards”, the fold chevron at its end. Folded, the band shows a fan of the tokens’ own art. Rows and tiles inside are the shipped ones, unchanged. The box is what says “not the deck”: every group of the 99 above it is a bare head.

A · status line segment (recommended)

“4 tokens from 7 cards ›” joins the deck’s status line after “5 combos one piece away”, with the Tokens strategy glyph. It lands on the box and opens it (the shipped Tokens › landing from card details). Seen on every visit, costs one line segment.

B · token mark on maker rows

A card that makes one of the deck’s tokens carries the token glyph after its bracket marks, in the fact ink, folding by the shipped rule; the key gains “makes a token”. The row already opens card details, whose “Makes a Treasure token · Tokens ›” lands on the box.

C · one line in the To buy box

On Shopping, where you go to buy, the To buy box says how many of the deck’s tokens aren’t in your collection, with Tokens ›. Shown only after a collection read, and only while one is missing and not on the list.

A, B and C combine; each stands alone too. Glyph: the Tokens strategy glyph from strategy-icon.tsx, never a new mark.
