Move a copy to another deck later

A copy went to the wrong deck and, once the receipt was gone, nothing offered the move. The shipped chooser (“Where this copy goes”) gets two more doors: card details and the collection History entry. Every frame is the shipped component rendered from main with the shipped CSS (amonkhet), with this round’s proposal applied. Example card: Swords to Plowshares, one copy, two decks list it.

Calls

1

Card details: “Change” at the end of the In your decks heading (C1), only when you own the card and no copy is free, so some deck is buying it or every copy is held. With a copy to spare there is nothing to move, so no Change. It opens the shipped chooser over card details: sheet on a phone, 520 modal on desktop.

2

The chooser is the shipped one, title “Where this copy goes”, sub “Swords to Plowshares · ×1 in your collection” (C2). Deck names are the control, gets it / buys it, tap the pick again to go back to first committed (C3). It writes the reservation (assignCopyFor) and changes no deck list or version; every claimant’s marks and buy state update.

3

Decks that don’t list it, under “Not in their lists”. One that can take it offers Add it: the card goes into that deck’s draft and the pick is kept for its commit (C4, Undo in the foot line). One that can’t says why in its row, never hidden: has 99 already, outside its colors, its commander.

4

History: “Change” at the end of an add’s “Went to …” line, same rule as call 1, opening the same chooser (H1, H2). The record is never rewritten: after a move the line reads “Went to Giada’s Angels. Now Heroes of the Plains has it.” (H3). In an opened multi-card entry, each row’s line gets its own Change (not drawn).

5

Matches the removal chooser (approved R1): the same row grammar (round mark, neutral default, accent pick, Back to first committed) and the same rule that a deck that can’t do something says why in its row.

For Hugh · three questions

Q1

Decks that don’t list the card? Recommended: Add it, into the draft with the pick kept for commit (call 3). Other option: name them with “not in its list” and no action; you add it from the deck, then come back.

Q2

Decks outside its colors? Recommended: a row each up to two, then one faint line “4 more decks are outside its colors.” Other option: a row each always.

Q3

Your Fabled Passage: did Mutant Menace already list it when you added it? If yes, calls 1 and 4 fix it. If no, call 3 does.
