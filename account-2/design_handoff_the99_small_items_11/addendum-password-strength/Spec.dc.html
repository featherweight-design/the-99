Live password strength · R1

Calls, states and gaps

Brief: docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-11-15-password-strength.md (branch brief/password-strength). This extends design_handoff_the99_small_items_11 (A2's Q1 and K5, which mirror Sign-In.dc.html and Sign-In-Methods.dc.html). Proposal code: password-strength.tsx and strength.ts in scratch-gen.

For Hugh

Meter plus line, in the hint's slot, weak in quiet ink. The line does the talking and the meter only shows the step. Strong never claims breach safety, and Clerk's answer still takes the slot on the press. R1b: a weak line names what to change for this password (a number or symbol only where it helps). Q3 carries the same fix. The press stays pressable when the line says Weak.

Calls

  1. Form: a meter plus a line. Three segments (the landing demo's 4px segment from demo.tsx) and a one-line word with advice. The line carries the meaning and the meter is decoration (aria-hidden). Three steps match the three things a player can do: keep going, fine, done.
  2. Placement: in the hint's slot. The meter goes 8px under the field, and the line goes 6px under the meter, where At least 8 characters. is now. Below 8 the line still is that hint. Nothing goes beside the field or the label.
  3. Weak is quiet ink before any press. --fg-muted, never --danger. Red is only for Clerk's answer (Q2 to Q4).
  4. Weak says what to change (R1b). Clerk scores guessability; it doesn't check character types, so a generic "add numbers and symbols" would send Summer2024! straight into a refusal. The estimator reports which pattern it matched, and the line picks the fix for that pattern. Order: common word or name, then year or date, then run or repeat, then only letters, then the fallback. Numbers and symbols come up only where they really lift the score. This is one line, not a rules checklist (that's out of scope in the brief).
  5. Steps map to Clerk. Under 8 → no estimate. Below Clerk's level 2 → Weak. Level 2 or 3 → Good. Level 4 → Strong. Engineering picks an estimator that agrees with Clerk's level 2 (zxcvbn score, same dictionaries). Where they could disagree, the estimator must err weak.
  6. Hand-off to a refusal. On the press, a refusal replaces the live line in the same slot (shipped role="alert", danger edge), and the meter empties. The next keystroke clears the refusal, as today, and the live line returns at the current strength.
  7. The press never locks. Make my account and Save and sign in stay pressable at every strength. The player chooses, and Clerk judges.
  8. Screen readers. The line is a polite live region (aria-live="polite") that the field names in aria-describedby. Its text only changes when the step changes, and it's set 1s after typing pauses, so a reader hears Weak. Try a longer one… once and not on every key. Each line starts with its word, so no hidden prefix is needed.
  9. Busy (Q5). As shipped, the sign-up's hint goes while Make my account is pressed. The meter and line go with it. K5 keeps them, as it keeps its hint today.

Gaps for engineering

  • G1 · Estimator. A client estimator that matches Clerk's level 2. Recommended: @zxcvbn-ts/core with the common and English packs, loaded lazily on the field's first focus so sign-in's first paint pays nothing. Engineering confirms what Clerk's level 2 is and tests it against Clerk with a small fixed list. The weak reason comes from the same result's match sequence (dictionary/user input → common, date → date, sequence/repeat → run, all letters → letters).
  • G2 · Field's description. Field sets aria-describedby only when a refusal shows. It needs to point at password-strength otherwise.
  • G3 · Agreement test. One test per step boundary, and one asserting that a password Clerk refuses as weak never shows Good or Strong (the brief's lock).
