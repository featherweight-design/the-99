Four small additions: the calls

Role counts that open their cards, the Consider shelf when a role is over target, an archive of past Wizards announcements, and sign-in on Clerk. Each is drawn at 390 and desktop over the shipped pieces it extends; dashed copper outlines mark what is new, and DRAFT marks wording for /copy-review. Source: brief docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-11-08-small-items-11.md, main @ffc1795.

1 · Role counts open their cards (Role-Cards)

  1. Surface: the shipped sheet at 390 and modal at desktop (the pass sheet’s SheetFrame, rows are the shipped DecklistRow). Not the Jobs disclosure (names only, no press), not a jump to By role (one role per card, so its count can disagree).
  2. One component for the strip and What we counted. Head {Role} · rec N, title N cards, A to Z. A card that does two jobs sits in both lists.
  3. Tuning hub re-ruled: the free pass still ranks and recommends no card. A role row may open its cards behind a press, A to Z. No cut order, no Cut button, no reason line.
  4. Cut: the row’s ✕ is the deck list’s own remove with the shipped undo row; the name opens card detail.
  5. Tiles: at or over target → cards →. Under target keeps consider → / suggest →; land keeps its press; a role with no cards draws no press.

2 · Consider with a role over (Consider-Over)

  1. Collapse, not hide: short shelves first, then Synergy · Zimone, then roles at target, then roles over, folded with the shipped shelf fold.
  2. Clause: at target keeps the shipped you have 10 of 10; over reads you have 9 · 1 over · 6 to consider (DRAFT).
  3. Cut entry: your 9 → on a met or over shelf’s head opens item 1’s list. One list for one job; no second control.
  4. Every role met: synergy leads, every role shelf folded below it.

3 · Past announcements (Archive)

  1. Own route /announcements, reached from Past announcements › in What’s new’s foot. A sheet cannot hold 608 posts or a month jump.
  2. Row: FeedItemRow as drawn, in its seen ink with the date, no ✕. Decklists posts keep their two presses and N of M lists.
  3. Switched-off kinds show: the archive is a library, not a feed. Dismissed posts show too. No new switch, no push, no Settings entry.
  4. Navigation: kind segment, sticky month heads, a month jump sheet with counts, and pages of 30 with Show older. Loading, failed and empty-month states drawn.

4 · Sign-in on Clerk (Sign-In)

  1. S4 and U4 stand as drawn on Clerk’s transfer. S4’s button is Use this email (Small items 7’s cap fix), then U3 for the first name.
  2. Invitation-only: an uninvited proven email gets the shipped inviteOnlyCopy with Use a different email. The invitation link lands on U3 with no code.
  3. No interstitial: the press carries the wait (Signing in…, Making your account…); a refusal or expired code stays on the page under the press.
  4. Methods: email code only; the Clerk dashboard is set to match.

5 · Errata (Errata)

  1. Tuning hub README:90 and its audit gain the role-list exception. Small items 7 S4 reads Use this email on the board. Add panel’s shelf order and the announcements foot gain this round’s lines.
