1 · Where the choice starts: the new-deck screen

  • It is the one screen every route already lands on: For you, Discover, the commander page, Help me choose and Decks › New deck. So the choice lives in one place.
  • It sits inline under the deck name as three radio rows, How we build it: Free build, Guided build and Just the commander. There is no extra screen before it and no banner.
  • Free build is pre-marked because it is today’s behaviour, free, and one tap as now. Guided is never pre-marked, so a credit is never spent by default.
  • The press names what happens next: Create deck → for free or empty, and Next · Strategy for guided.
  • Not on the commander page: Help me choose and the picker never pass through it. Not as a step before both: that would be a whole screen for a choice that fits inline.

2 · What the guided build asks

  • Strategy › Bracket › Cards, in Upgrade’s flow chrome (sheet on a phone, 720 dialog on desktop, bounded tracker, bare ✕).
  • Strategy: up to 3 of the commander’s themes that your own cards back, drawn as Upgrade’s strategy cards (glyph, how many of your cards, one sentence, three examples). Nothing is pre-picked.
  • Bracket: the shipped rungs 2, 3 and 4. Nothing is pre-picked. Combos follow the bracket (2 none, 3 late only, 4 allowed), so there is no separate combo question.
  • Cards: Only cards I own, or Buy some cards (each under $12, Upgrade’s cap). Help me choose’s budget shows as Buy within $N.
  • Cards to avoid: not asked. Every card lands in a deck you can edit, and a search-and-pick step costs more than it saves. It is easy to add later.
  • The free build sees none of it. It still takes Help me choose’s answers, as shipped.

3 · A true “start empty”: yes

  • Just the commander is the third row, and it is free: “Ghalta and 99 empty slots. Build it your way.”
  • Today the only way there is to fill the deck and then sweep it with Clear all auto. That sweep’s own copy (“Back to just the commander”) already names this state.

4 · The commander page stops promising a build

  • Phone: the bar loses READY TO BUILD and “We’ll build the whole 99, as far as your collection reaches”. It keeps the synergy picks line, when there are picks, and one button: Start a deck →.
  • Desktop: the button becomes Start a deck → and the seed note under it goes.
  • The page now reads as a commander’s page with one way to start a deck, close to the public page. How the deck is built is chosen on the next screen.
  • Synergy picks still carry over, and the guided build keeps them: the Cards step lists them under It keeps.

5 · Not enough credits

  • The Guided row stays visible but is off. It says why in --warning: “A guided build uses 2 credits, and you have 1.”
  • With payments on, it shows the shipped Get more credits →. With payments off, it says free credits alone can’t run one, and shows no link.
  • The other two rows are untouched. This follows Upgrade’s credits path.

6 · Help me choose carries in, visibly

  • The shipped Built from your answers block stays above the name, and it still drives the free build.
  • Choosing Guided opens the flow with every answer already picked and marked from your answers: the theme becomes the strategy, the bracket the bracket, owned-only becomes Only cards I own, and the budget becomes Buy within $60.
  • A build-around card is listed under It keeps, and the run never cuts it. Every pick can still be changed.

7 · The paid run

  • The confirm is Build toward Big creatures, with uses 2 · 15 welcome credits beside it, as in Upgrade.
  • The deck is created at the confirm, with the free fill, so nothing can be lost. The engine then sharpens 12 to 18 cards and writes the plan.
  • The wait has three steps: Filled ✓, Sharpening (with v2’s clock) and Writing the plan.
  • ✕ leaves while the run goes on. Until it is done, the deck’s tile and page show Upgrade’s running line, Sharpening toward Big creatures · 0:05.

8 · Ready, short, failed

  • Ready: four facts (Strategy, Bracket, Sharpened, To buy), then The plan in the Overview’s Early, Mid and Close shape, with a lead card for each, then Open Ghalta Stomps. The plan is the deck’s Overview, so there is no separate write.
  • There is no swap review. You never saw the free fill, so a list of cuts from it means nothing. The sharpened cards are in the deck, and you can clear any of them.
  • Short (fewer than 12): both credits come back, and the cards it found stay in the deck.
  • Failed: both credits come back and the deck keeps the free build. The presses are Try again (2 credits, same picks) and Open the deck.
Gaps for engineering
#Gap
G1createDeck takes a build kind (free, guided or empty). Empty skips planFill, and the deck starts at the commander alone.
G2The guided run is Upgrade’s engine over the new deck’s free fill (a deck list in place of a precon list). Strategies come from the commander’s themes, backed by owned cards (MIN_BACKING, MAX_STRATEGIES). Runs are keyed per deck.
G3The run writes the plan and stores it as the deck’s Overview in the stages shape. It goes inside the 2 credits, replacing the separate Overview write.
G4The pool input: owned only, the $12 per-card cap, or Help me choose’s total budget. Today the engine has only the per-card cap.
G5Kept cards (the build-around card and the commander page’s synergy picks) go in as locked. The validator refuses a cut of any of them.
G6The run’s state on the deck (running, short or failed), for the tile and the page after the player leaves the flow.
G7Refunds as in Upgrade: a failed run, or fewer than 12 cards sharpened.
G8The commander page’s copy: stickyLabel, stickyPromise and seedNote retire, and the button reads Start a deck →.
