R2, phone and desktop
Tap a printing under the card

1 · Where

A strip of every printing at card shape, with the set code under each, in card details opened from search. On the phone it sits in the sheet's teaser under the price line. On desktop it sits under the title. The printing on screen is ringed.

2 · Tap to view

The art, the price and the set line change in place. Nothing is stored, and closing or paging forgets it (the R1 preview rules). Add to collection and Edit copies open at the printing on screen, as shipped.

3 · Owned cards

Your printings come first: ◆ your pick, then the rest you own (×N). A hairline, then every other printing. Viewing never changes your pick.

4 · The Set field goes back to one job

For a card you don't own it's a plain field naming what's showing. For a card you own it's the shipped picker for your pick, over the printings you own. R1's list inside the Set field (22 printings ▾, Other printings) is retired, so there's one control per action.

5 · Sizes

48×67 thumbs with an 8px gap, so 6 show on a phone. The teaser grows by 98px and the card above shrinks to fit, like the stand-ins door strip. The full phone sheet drops the strip because it covers the art. Pull down to the teaser to browse.

6 · One printing

No strip. The plain Set field says The only printing, as shipped.
