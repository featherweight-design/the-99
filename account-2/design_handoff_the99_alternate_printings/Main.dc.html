Alternate printings from search · R1 (#3002)

The answer in one line

Card details opened from search lists every printing of the card in the Set field's existing printing region, owned or not. Selecting a printing you own makes it your pick, as today. Selecting one you don't own shows it here (art, Set field and price) and stores nothing.

1 · Where the option sits

Card details: the Set field, the shipped disclosure, now drawn whenever the catalog has 2+ printings. The third line reads 22 printings ▾ (unowned), 22 printings · you own 2 ▾ (owned) or ◆ YOUR PICK · 22 printings ▾. On the phone it's the promoted full-width Set row (62px, 40×56 thumb), on desktop the boxed Set field in the meta row. Nothing new is added to the screen.

The search result row: R-A, unchanged (recommended). One press opens card details, where the printings are one more press away, and the + adds as shipped. Printings at 40×56 can't be compared, and a third control crowds the phone row. R-B, the alternative: a printings press (stacked-cards glyph, 44px phone / 34px desktop) before the +, which opens card details with the region already open. It's drawn only at 2+ printings, and the slot is held so rows stay aligned. Your call: A or B.

2 · A card you don't own: preview

Selecting a printing previews it. The art, the Set field and the price tag under the art all move to that printing (the tag already prices the printing on screen). The tile takes the shipped ◇ Shown now badge. Nothing is stored, and closing or paging the card forgets it.

Adding takes the preview with it. The shipped Add to collection opens Edit copies with the previewed printing already chosen as the new printing, and you can change it there. There's no new button, and the choice stays yours.

Not the wishlist: a wishlist line is per card by ruling (Q7, 2026-10-12), so there's no printing to set. Not a deck: a deck's printing is owned-only (Deck printings R2). The buy link stays Buy any printing at Mana Pool, since Mana Pool's link is a name search. The figure on the tag is the previewed printing's own.

3 · A card you own: one region, yours first

IN YOUR COLLECTION · 2 is today's picker exactly: selecting one sets ✓ Your pick (stored), and Use the default again clears it. OTHER PRINTINGS · 20 follows. Those preview only, as for an unowned card, and they're marked not owned. A pick and a preview read apart through the shipped badges: ✓ Your pick is what's stored, and ◇ Shown now is what's on screen. With the region open, picking an owned tile ends any preview.

Past 12 printings the shipped search and set headers apply (newest set first). The finish chips step aside whenever other printings are listed, because a catalog printing's finishes aren't held counts (the choose role's rule).

One printing

The plain shipped Set field, with The only printing under it in faint mono, so the missing list is explained rather than absent. There's no disclosure and nothing to press.

Phone

The sheet stays where the player left it. The tile is the preview at card shape, and the Set row's thumbnail and caption follow it. Dragging the sheet down (the shipped gesture) shows the art full size. Tiles keep the shipped ≥100px floor (two across at 390), with full-width 44px search.

Scope and gaps

Only card details opened from search (the brief's lock). Collection and deck details are unchanged. Offering it everywhere is a follow-up and your call.

G1 card details from search needs the catalog's printings for an owned card (an unowned card already gets them: catalogCard fills printings at quantity 0). G2 preview state in CardModal, fed through the openAtPrinting path (injected at quantity 0, with its art). G3 Edit copies opens with the new printing preset (by scryfall id). G4 (R-B only) a printings count on CardSearchRow. G5 switch SEARCH_PRINTINGS_ENABLED, because the region change touches the shipped picker. G6 new strings go to /copy-review.
