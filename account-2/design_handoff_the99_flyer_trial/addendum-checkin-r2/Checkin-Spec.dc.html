What changes, and how it moves

An addendum to the flyer-trial handoff. It reskins CheckInForm (src/components/feedback/check-in-form.tsx) and rewrites checkInCopy in src/lib/copy/report.ts. CheckInPrompt, the due rule, the snooze, the actions and the stored answers do not change.

Design’s calls

  1. The rating is a hand of five cards. Card shape (63:88), fanned −6°, −3°, 0, 3°, 6° from the bottom centre, each with its number in the display face and a word: Rough, Meh, Okay, Good, Love it. The picked card lifts 10px, straightens, takes the accent wash and wears the crown (◆◆◆). Phone: five equal columns (about 67 by 93). Desktop: fixed 70 by 98, centred. The “not well / really well” ends go; the words do that job.
  2. The chips stay the shipped pills. Turning one on gets a 180ms pop. No icons: Shopping and Precons have no nav icon, and half-iconned chips would look broken.
  3. The card back by the intro is the payoff. A small tilted back (44 by 62, the 99 mark) sits beside the intro from the start. On send it becomes the shipped compact FriendCard (150 by 210, −3°) with the crowned face, and turns face up as the thanks. No new art, no images to break.
  4. Two thanks, chosen by the rating already given. 3 to 5: “Thank you, truly!” and a burst of six crown diamonds. 1 or 2: “Thanks for telling us straight”, same card, no burst. The rating is the real trigger; nothing new is asked.
  5. Sending stays as shipped (Sending…, everything held), with answers dimmed to .6. Failed stays the shipped toast with Try again; only its words change.
  6. Close stays the only button under the thanks, plus the bare ✕. About › Report a problem is named in both thanks.

Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): no rise, no turn, no burst, no chip pop, no lift transition (the picked card is simply drawn lifted). The back cross-fades to the face over 120ms after the 200ms beat, which is the shipped .friend-card rule, and the words appear with it. Low ratings never burst in either mode.

Accessibility

  • The hand is the shipped role="radiogroup" with five role="radio" buttons; each is named “4 of 5, Good”. Add arrow-key movement with a roving tabindex (today’s row has none).
  • Hit boxes: phone cards about 67 by 93, chips 40 tall, buttons 44. The fan tilt never shrinks a hit box; the button is the card.
  • The small back and the diamonds are aria-hidden. The face-up card is one image named “Thank you, a card from The 99”, as the shipped card does.
  • On thanks, focus moves to the heading (tabindex −1) and the thanks container is a polite live region, so a screen reader hears the thanks once.
  • Contrast: card words use --fg-muted on --surface-raised (≥4.5:1 in all six themes, as the shipped labels); the picked card’s word goes to --fg.

Engineering notes (not design calls)

  • Reuse FriendCard with compact, face.crown and word; mount it face down, set up on the next frame with arrival for the 200ms beat. The small back by the intro is a new 44 by 62 drawing of the same back.
  • The burst is six absolutely placed ◆ in --accent-text, CSS keyframes only, removed after 1.5s. No library.
  • Rating words map by index; the stored value stays the number 1 to 5.
  • The thanks variant reads the rating already in state (≤ 2 is low). Nothing new is sent or stored.
  • Tests that assert today’s strings (check-in-form.test.tsx) move to the new keys.
