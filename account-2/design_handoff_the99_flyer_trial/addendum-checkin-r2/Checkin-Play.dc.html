The beats

  1. Pick a card: it lifts 10px and straightens (220ms, a small overshoot), the crown fades in.
  2. Pick a chip: a 180ms pop from .94.
  3. Send: Sending… for the round trip; answers dim.
  4. Thanks: form gives way (150ms fade), the card back rises 24px into place (200ms), turns over (520ms, the shipped curve), then the six diamonds burst and fade (700ms) as the words arrive (200ms fade at 720ms).
  5. Reduced motion: no rise, no turn, no burst, no pop or lift animation. The back cross-fades to the face (120ms, the shipped rule) and the words appear with it.
