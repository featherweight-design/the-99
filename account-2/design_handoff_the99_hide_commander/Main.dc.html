Hide a commander: the calls (R1b)

Brief docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-10-31-hide-a-commander.md and Hugh’s rulings (hidden until undone, a Hidden list, out of For you, Discover and Help me choose, the page still opens with Unhide). Every state at 390 and 1300, drawn over the shipped captures (public/about/pages/amonkhet) and the shipped components each board names. Strings marked DRAFT go to /copy-review. Sources: main @7bf3c89.

1 · The control (Rows)

  1. R1b: at the foot of the row, the right end of the figures line, at both widths. Hugh: the top-right plate drew the eye from the commander. Not a menu (the row has none), not a swipe (can’t be found), not page-only.
  2. A bare eye-slash glyph, 16px, --fg-faint, no plate, in a 44px target that adds no height. After the manapool stamp in Discover. Always drawn, not hover-only.
  3. It sits above the row’s stretched link, so pressing it never opens the page. aria-label and title: Hide {name}.

2 · Confirmation and undo (Undo)

  1. No confirm. “Hidden until undone” is safe because undoing is one press, twice over: right there, and in Hidden any time later.
  2. The row turns into the builder’s shipped Undo row (Ghalta, Primal Hunger hidden · Undo): no toast, no timer, gone when the reader leaves the tab, one per hide. Rows below keep their numbers until the next load.
  3. Failure: a refused hide puts the row back and raises the shared toast with Try again; a refused undo drops its button and says Unhide it from Hidden, as the builder’s does.

3 · Where Hidden lives (Hidden)

  1. A fourth tab on the Commanders strip: For you · Discover · Help me choose · Hidden, at /commanders/hidden. It sits beside the lists it takes from, it is there before anything is hidden (so a reader finds it, and its empty state is real), and it carries the strip’s shipped count once something is.
  2. Not Settings: these are commanders, not a preference, and Settings would hold a list of cards. Not the user menu.
  3. Rows are Help me choose’s result row with Unhide in place of the figures; newest first; the row opens the page. Unhide turns the row into the same Undo row: back in For you or back in Discover.

4 · The commander’s page (Page)

  1. “From search” is the lists’ own search: card search opens card details, not this page. A hidden match lands on Discover’s shipped held state (or its aside above a live list) with a new cause, you hid it, and Open its page.
  2. Hidden: the hero’s shipped note shape leads the body with Unhide; the kicker reads Hidden and the hero Not ranked while hidden. Fit and Build stay.
  3. The page offers Hide too: a quiet Hide from my lists line, under the hero on the phone and under the build note on desktop. Nothing over the art.

5 · Help me choose, pairs, short lists (Edges)

  1. Help me choose: filtered in the candidates’ own statement, before the read picks. Nothing drawn, no count, never named.
  2. Pairs: Hide acts on the card. A two-faced commander is one card (named by both faces, as the row does). Partners are two cards and two rows: hiding one leaves the other. Decks are never touched.
  3. Short list: fewer rows, nothing else. All hidden: the shared zero state, Everything here is hidden, with See hidden. Counts (title line and count line) leave hidden commanders out.

6 · Name and words

  1. Hide, Hidden, Unhide: the words of the control, the tab and the way back are the same family, and “Not interested” reads as a verdict on the commander, which Hugh’s ruling says it is not.
  2. DRAFT strings: the held heading and body, the page note, Hide from my lists, the empty state, all-hidden state and the three failure lines. The Undo row reuses undo.action.
