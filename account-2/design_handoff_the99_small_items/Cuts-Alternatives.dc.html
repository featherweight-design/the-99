Your rule from today: give the player a choice and never lock them in. R1 drew three alternatives under each cut; R1b, from your feedback: the run simply names more cuts than the deck needs, two or three over, and you choose. At 102 the deck is three over, so the run names six and you cut any three. Every row is the shipped CutRow (Suggestion, the card, Cut and Keep, its reason and figures); nothing new is drawn on it. The change is engineering’s: more rows back, each with its reasoning. Copy is placeholder for /copy-review.

How many
  • Three more than you need (one over: four named; three over: six). Fewer when the run can’t back that many with figures it checked, but never fewer than the deck needs unless the run says why.
  • Never a locked card, a Key card or the commander, as today.
For engineering
  • The same Look deeper run, at the same one credit, returns over + 3 cut rows, each with its reason and cite chips held to the same rules and validator. Prompt and schema change only.
  • The panel keeps the rows the run returned; no new control, no pick state.
  • Copy for /copy-review: the head Six to choose from, the lead lines, the foot 6 named for 3 cuts.
  • Open question: a cap for a deck far over (say 15 over). Suggest at most 20 rows.
Unchanged
  • Cut, Keep, Undo, Show it again, History.
