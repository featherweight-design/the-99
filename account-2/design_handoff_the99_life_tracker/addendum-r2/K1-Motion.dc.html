Motion: the strip press, day and night

Both answer a tap, so neither waits a beat and neither delays the change: the state lands on the press, the motion only dresses it. Under reduced motion nothing moves; the state still changes.

1 · A strip button, pressed

→ →
Trigger:active (pointer down, Space or Enter held). Every strip button: monarch, initiative, day/night, dice, undo, Menu. Not the round (a display) or a disabled Undo.
Colourground var(--accent), icon var(--bg), ring off. The same fill as Dice open and a monarch pick, so "lit" means one thing on the strip.
Pushscale(.92), as the panel's round sheet button (End turn's pill is .94).
Timingtransition: transform, background-color, color over --motion-quick (200 ms) on --motion-ease, no beat, both ways: the app's press feedback.
Holder artThe holder's face in the corner scales with the button, so it never detaches.
Reduced motionNo scale and no transition. The accent ground still shows while pressed.

2 · Day and night, spinning

DirectionAlways clockwise, like a dial: the old icon turns out to +90°, the new one turns in from −90°. Night to day keeps turning the same way.
ShapeBoth at once, stacked: out rotate(0→90°) scale(1→.6) opacity(1→0); in rotate(−90°→0) scale(.6→1) opacity(0→1).
Duration, easing320 ms (--motion-duration) on --motion-ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), no beat. No overshoot (the prototype's bounce read as a wobble on a 24 px icon).
First tapOff to day: the faint sun turns out, the lit sun turns in, and the ring lights at once.
Fast tapsA tap mid-spin finishes the running one instantly and starts the next, so the icon always matches the state.
Reduced motionThe icon swaps on the tap; no turn, no fade.

3 · The gain/loss pulse is gone

The life-pulse keyframes and the panel's pulse span go. What a tap shows is the 2 s window: the half lit 18% accent and the pending ±N beside the number (G1). On commit the half fades back over its shipped 120 ms and the number ticks (opacity .7→1, 120 ms). Nothing else moves.
