Help me choose a commander

/decks/new/choose

Know the deck you want, but not who should lead it? Tell “Help me choose” what you’re after, and it finds commanders that fit, whether you own them or not.

Colors, themes, sets, a budget and a bracket narrow the list for free. Or describe the deck in your own words: how it should feel, or how it should differ from your decks. That uses a credit, and up to ten picks come first, each with a line on why.

Pick a commander and your answers carry over to a new deck, ready to fill from your Collection. Every run that reads your words is kept under History, and opening one again is free.

  • Words in the “Anything else?” box are read against every commander your answers allow. Tag a card there with [[ and it is required: only commanders whose decks run it are suggested.
  • Your answers always hold, so no pick falls outside them.
  • Ask for something unlike your decks, and it compares their commanders and themes.
  • The rest of your results follow the picks, in the usual order.

How credits work

opens a deck’s Analysis tab

Some features write something just for you, and those use credits. You get 5 a day, and each one comes back 24 hours after you use it.

The following use a credit:

  • Writing a deck’s Overview
  • Each “Suggested changes” run
  • Each “Look deeper” in Proposed cuts
  • Each new “Help me choose” search where you fill in “Anything else?”
  • Each “Which deck needs it most?” read

Determining your deck’s bracket is free.

Opening a past “Help me choose” run from History is free.
