NEWFriends and pods

/friends

Playing with the same people every week? Make them friends in The 99. Open “Friends” from your account menu, press “Add a friend”, and send them the link, or let them scan the QR code off your screen. Once they accept, you’re friends.

Pick a commander and its art becomes your face, beside your name everywhere a friend sees you. Then gather the friends you play with into a pod, and choose, pod by pod, who gets to see your spare cards.

  1. ·Nobody can find you by searching. A friend link works once and lasts 7 days, and you can take back one that’s still open.
  2. ·Friends see your name and your commander, never what you own. Your spares show only to the pods you share them with.
  3. ·You add your own friends to your pods. Leave one you own and it passes to its longest-standing member.
  4. ·Removing a friend or leaving a pod tells nobody.

NEWWho you played against

/decks opens a deck’s Games tab

When you log a game, “Across the table” lets you seat who you played: a friend, one of their decks, or anyone by their commander. Lost? Press the trophy on whoever won.
