NEWWho you played against

/decks opens a deck’s Games tab

When you log a game, “Across the table” lets you seat who you played: a friend, one of their decks, or anyone by their commander. Lost? Press the trophy on whoever won.

Once a few games carry names, “Matchups” on the deck’s “Games” tab splits your record by commander, colors and friend, and each game in “History” says who was there.

  1. ·Seats are optional. A game with nobody seated counts as it always has.
  2. ·Each square is one game, won, lost or drawn. No percentages, so one game reads as one game.
  3. ·A seat keeps names and decks as they were that night, even if a friend renames a deck or leaves.
  4. ·“Seat a pod” sits a whole pod at once.

Tuning a deck with Suggested changes

/decks opens a deck’s Analysis tab

Want a second opinion? “Suggested changes”, at the bottom of the “Analysis” tab, offers up to five cards to add and five to cut, each with its own reason.
