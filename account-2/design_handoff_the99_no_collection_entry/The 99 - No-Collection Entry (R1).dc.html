A way in without a collection file

Upload stays the first and largest thing on step 1. Under it, one quiet group, No file? Start another way, holds the three ways to give The 99 your cards without one: Precons I own, Import a deck, Add cards by hand. The same group sits under Upload on the empty Collection. Collection stops locking at zero cards; Commanders, Discover and Decks still wait for cards. Nothing new replaces: every write is an add, one History entry per box or deck.

§Open · the seven calls
1Step 1's shape, given ruling 1The alternatives are on step 1, below the upload, as ways to give us your cards, never as a way round the collection. The card keeps Upload a CSV (fill) and Paste a list (hairline). A 1px rule, then NO FILE? START ANOTHER WAY and three text rows. Add cards by hand moves out of the button row into the group, so the buttons say “your file” and the group says “no file”. Hugh confirms (ruling 1 is his).
2Which path leads; is (c) a choice?Precons I own leads the group: the catalog already knows every card in the box, so it is the most cards for the least typing. Then Import a deck, then Add cards by hand. (c) is not a button. Starting empty is what happens when the player leaves: Collection is open at zero cards and its empty state carries the same group. A “Start empty” press would write nothing and only move the lock, which reads as a skip button.
3The own-these offer after an importA second line on the shipped arrival strip, shown when the collection was empty at the import: Already own these? Add the 99 cards to your collection. with Add 99 cards and Not now. It adds every line, no ticks: a player who owns some of them uses the Shopping view's shipped I got it, per card. The same line sits at the head of the deck's Shopping view while the collection is empty, so a dismissed offer can be found again.
4The precon pickA pick mode of the shipped precon list, /decks/precons?pick=own, in rows (tiles are tall; 197 boxes is a list job). Grouped by year, newest first, with the shipped search. Each row: checkbox, name, SET · Mon YYYY, pips, 100 cards. The ownership figure is dropped in this mode (it reads 0 of N everywhere at first run). A twin row picks the plain edition; ticked, it shows one radio pair, Regular / Collector's Edition. A sticky foot: Add 3 boxes · 300 cards, held until one is ticked.
5The later-CSV seamCollection Sync's handoff answers it, so the choice is not redrawn: nothing preselected, Replace and Add unchanged, Sync shown disabled with its shipped never-uploaded reason (your collection was built in the app without one). One addition, on Replace's warning: it names what came from precons and by hand that the file lacks, from the dry run (212 cards you added from 3 precons and by hand aren't in this file and would leave.). See design/design_handoff_the99_collection_sync/.
6The nav lock at zero cardsCollection opens at zero cards (edit switch on), because its empty state is where a player who starts empty starts. Commanders, Discover and Decks stay locked until a card lands (Locked 4: an unowned deck unlocks nothing). The well and the tab bracket span three slots, and the words name three: Commanders, Discover and Decks open once your cards are in. Outside first run the note's button is Add your cards to /collection, since there is no step 1 to go to.
7/commanders and /discover empty statesTitle Nothing to rank yet, body We rank commanders from the cards you own. Add some and every one you can build shows up here., one button Add your cards to /collection, where every way in lives. Moved to copy keys.
§Locked · how R1 holds each
No data lossEvery write is an add through applyCollectionChange: one History entry per box, one per deck. Revert takes each back. Nothing new replaces.
Upload stays primaryThe only fill on step 1 and the empty Collection. The group sits below it in body text, never in a button.
Precon add reusedThe pick calls addPreconForUser per box, as is, no deck started, nothing reserved.
Ruling 1's orderEvery path ends in owned cards. A deck imported and not owned leaves Commanders, Discover and Decks locked.
SwitchesEdit off: the group, the offer and the open Collection are absent, upload alone. Precons off: the Precons row is absent.
