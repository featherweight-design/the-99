No export to upload? Plenty of us don’t have one. If your cards are a few precon boxes, a Moxfield list or a pile on the desk, start there. Pick the precons you own and their cards go into your Collection at the box’s printings, tokens too. Import a deck you play and tick that you own its cards. Or add cards one at a time. A file can come later.

·Commanders, Discover and Decks open once your Collection has a card in it.

·An imported deck’s cards are added as one change, at the printings the list named.
·Your first file asks whether it replaces or adds, and says what a replace would remove.

precons · ordinary edit · caption, second paragraph

Starting from a precon

Open one to read its list, group by group, as rows or as cards. Say you own 74 of Eldrazi Unbound’s 85: start a deck from it and you get the whole list, in the box’s printings. Own the box? Select “I own this precon” when you start, and its cards and tokens go into your collection too. Own a few boxes and don’t want decks from them yet? Pick them under Precons I own, from your Collection, and they’re added without starting anything.

Importing a deck, or updating one

·Paste Moxfield or MTGO text, or upload a CSV from ManaBox, Moxfield or Archidekt.
·Updating shows what the new list changes, and lets you keep cards it leaves out.
·A line we can’t match still comes over, flagged for you to fix.
·Own the deck you’re importing? Tick the box and its cards go into your Collection too.
