T · the plain-text twin and the headers

Same words, one layout

The shipped plain-text rules: the first line is the heading as a sentence, the sign-up link alone after a label ending in a colon (once after the lead, once after the features), the four features as a dash list, then --, the reason, the unsubscribe line with its link alone on the next line, the tagline, the replies line and the site. Straight quotes. addendum-launch/templates/launch.txt holds the placeholders.
