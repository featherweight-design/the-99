T · the plain-text twin and the headers

Same words, one layout

The shipped plain-text rules: the first line is the heading as a sentence, the link alone after a label ending in a colon, the steps as a numbered list, then --, the reason, the unsubscribe line with its link alone on the next line, the flavor line, the replies line and the site. Straight quotes. addendum-launch/templates/launch.txt holds the placeholders.
