One email, the day The 99 opens

Sent once through Resend to every address on launch_list (the landing page’s Tell me at launch and the used-up flyer’s), HTML plus a plain-text twin, on the shipped shell exactly: mark tile, brass top rule, kicker, Georgia heading, the points box, one brass button, the flavor footer. Every frame on this canvas is a screenshot of the template in addendum-launch/templates/, rendered in Chromium at 648 and 375. Everything in it is DRAFT for /copy-review.

What it says

The app is open: kicker ◆ Now open ◆, heading “The 99 is open to everyone” (the flyer’s shipped launched title, less “now”).

What you can do first: three numbered steps in the shipped points box, numerals in place of the diamonds because these go in order: bring your collection, see what you can build, try the writing tools with 15 welcome credits.

One call to action: Sign up free to /sign-up, the landing’s open-state button word, with its fine print “Free to join. No card details.” and the raw link under it.

What it shows

A launch hand: the invite’s plain hand (three 99 card backs in the brass glow) with the top back reading NOW OPEN instead of INVITATION. One static PNG, /email/launch-hand@2x.png, made by the same gen:email-images script from InviteHand with a back word. No sender, no art fetch, nothing that can break at send time.

Images off, its box says what it is (board D).

Do the flyer and invite codes matter?

No, and the email says so. At launch sign-up opens and flyer codes retire (the shipped launchedBody), so one fine line reads “You don’t need an invite or a flyer code. Anyone can join now.”

Where the source shows: only the footer’s reason line. A row with a code_id says “from a flyer”; one without says “at the-99.dev”. The flyer’s label is admin-only and never shown.

Unsubscribe: the line and the page

The line sits in the footer, under the reason: “This is the only email the launch list sends. Unsubscribe and we’ll delete this address now.” It tells the truth about a one-time list (it is purged 30 days after launch anyway) and gives the reader the choice to go sooner.

The page is /unsubscribe?t=…, signed out, no app shell: the shipped offline page’s shape (eyebrow, display title, one sentence, one button). “You’re off the list.” / “We’ve deleted this address from the launch list, so The 99 won’t email it again.” / Visit The 99, so a reader who changed their mind still has a way in (board U).

No confirm step: a link scanner that opens it can only cancel an email that will never be sent again, so one press does it.

Gaps for engineering

G1 · Headers: List-Unsubscribe: <https://the-99.dev/unsubscribe?t=…> and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click on the Resend send, so Gmail and Yahoo show their own Unsubscribe (bulk-sender rule).

G2 · Token: t is an HMAC of the case-folded address, no account, no expiry. Valid: delete the row, show U1. Tampered or cut short: U2. A row already gone (or purged) still shows U1, so the page never says whether an address was listed.

G3 · Send once: a send log per address so a retried job never mails twice; batch under Resend’s rate limit; send from invites@mail.the-99.dev like the rest.

G4 · The hand PNG: InviteHand with a back word prop; reference art/launch-hand@2x.png.
