A seat at the table: one shell with more life, the invite as a hand of cards

Brief: docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-11-14-branded-emails.md. Every email frame on this canvas is a screenshot of the handoff’s own HTML (templates/) at 600 and 375, so what’s drawn is what gets sent. Sender fixture: Colin, with Ghalta, Primal Hunger.

What changed from R1 (your review)

Logo only on top. The crowned 99 tile, larger (56px), centered, with no wordmark beside it. With images off, its alt text “The 99” shows in brass in the same box.

More life, not louder. A brass rule across the top of the panel, a brass kicker over every heading (◆ You’re invited ◆, ◆ One-time code ◆), a bigger Georgia heading, and a footer that closes on a flavor line, “Search your library for a commander.” The invite now leads with a hand of cards: Colin’s card face up, fanned over two of The 99’s card backs, in a soft brass glow. Under the heading, three things waiting inside, taken from the flyer.

Sender. Every email, Clerk’s included, comes from The 99 <invites@mail.the-99.dev> (your answer to Q1).

Copy pass. Every string was rewritten for plain, warm prose: “Enter” over “Type”, “expires” over “works for”, a question for the ignore line (“Don’t know Colin? You can ignore this email.”). It still gets a /copy-review before the build.

Design’s calls (the brief’s Open list), as of R2

1

The shell

A fluid column, 600 max, on #14110E. One panel (#1E1A15, 1px line, 3px brass top rule, 12 radius). Brass kicker, Georgia heading 30, system sans body 16/1.6, fine print under a hairline. One brass button (52 tall, VML for Outlook). Footer: three brass diamonds, the flavor line in Georgia italic, then why it came, “Replies to this email aren’t read.”, the-99.dev. Tables and inline styles only. Board S.

2

Header and mark

Logo only: the tile as a hosted PNG, 56px, alt="The 99" styled brass Georgia so it reads when images are off. No web fonts anywhere.

3

The invite

Subject as today. Preheader “Join as Colin’s friend. Your invite works for 7 days.” The hand: the shipped compact FriendCard face over two 99 card backs in a brass glow, one hosted PNG (560×540 source, shown 280×270), tilts baked in. Kicker, “Colin saved you a seat at The 99”, one line on what The 99 is, three diamond points, Join The 99, the raw link, the 7-day line. No art: the same hand with The 99’s own card back on top. No name: “A friend”. Boards I and P.

4

Which emails get the shell

All five. Codes share one template: the code 38px mono in a brass-bordered box, live text with no spaces, still in the subject. Clerk’s sign-in (“Welcome back”) and reset are the same shell in Clerk’s editor with {{otp}}. Boards C and K.

5

Dark mode

A dark shell declared color-scheme: dark; Apple Mail and partial-invert clients leave it alone. Gmail iOS turns it parchment, still legible, and the images (tile, hand of cards) never invert. Board D.

6

Plain text

First line is the heading as a sentence. The link or code on its own line, the invite’s three points as a dash list, then -- and the same footer, flavor line included. Board T.
