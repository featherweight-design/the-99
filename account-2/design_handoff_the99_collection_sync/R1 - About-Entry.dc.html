Replacing or adding from a file

Drop in a fresh export from ManaBox, Moxfield or any of the others and The 99 asks what the file is for. “Replace” makes your Collection exactly the file. “Add” stacks its cards on top of what you have, which is what you want for a booster haul or a trade binder. Either way, you see every card that moves before anything changes. Replace also tells you if it’ll overwrite changes you made in the app, and names any decks that will read short.

—Replace: anything not in the file leaves your Collection.
—Add: quantities go up, nothing leaves, and your art picks stay.
—Adding a full export doubles your copies, and the screen tells you when that’s about to happen.
—Your very first upload skips the question, since there’s nothing to replace.

Replacing, adding or syncing from a file

Drop in a fresh export from ManaBox, Moxfield or any of the others and The 99 asks what the file is for. “Replace” makes your Collection exactly the file. “Add” stacks its cards on top of what you have, which is what you want for a booster haul or a trade binder. “Sync” takes what changed in the file and keeps what you changed in the app. Whichever you pick, you see every card that moves before anything changes. Replace also tells you if it’ll overwrite changes you made in the app, and names any decks that will read short.

—Replace: anything not in the file leaves your Collection.
—Add: quantities go up, nothing leaves, and your art picks stay.
—Sync: keeps what you changed in the app and takes what’s new in the file. If you both changed a card, it asks.
—Your very first upload skips the question, since there’s nothing to replace.
Adding a full export doubles your copies, and the screen tells you when that’s about to happen.
