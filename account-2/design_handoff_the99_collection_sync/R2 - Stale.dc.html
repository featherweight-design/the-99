A banner that says “looks”, and what Sync may take from the file

The warning heads the step, above all three cards, because an old file is most dangerous under Replace. It never says “is older”: the check reads the file’s cards against your uploads, not its name or date. When Sync is picked under it, a short choice follows the cards (never nested inside one): Only what it adds, picked first; Only its tokens; Everything, removals too. Cancel is the step’s own. Tokens always come with the cards’ choice, by the token lock, so the first two combine; the second is drawn only when it differs.
