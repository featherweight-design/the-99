Three ways to read a game

Decks › Games and Deck details › Games, phone and desktop, drawn three ways: A Seats, B Ledger and C Headline rows. Pick one and the next round fleshes it out. The month boxes and the filters are the same in all three.

A · Seats· recommended

Each game is the table you sat at: your deck first, then each opponent, as art tiles. The winner’s tile is ringed with the trophy in its corner. Most personality, tallest rows.

B · Ledger· dense

A table: Result, Deck, At the table, Won by, Marks, Played. Everything lines up down the month. Best for scanning many games; least art.

C · Headline rows· calm

One line per game that says what happened (“Lost to Peter”). Selecting a row opens the game: a drawer on desktop, a sheet on phone. Marks and notes move into it.
