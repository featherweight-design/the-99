Why I have one moves into the fold

At 173px the two presses cannot share a line (about 200px of label and padding), so today they stack into three 44px targets under every tile. The state line keeps the press that changes Shopping. I have one goes first inside the makers fold, and that is also the only place it can act: a token with no maker refuses the hand mark, and a token with no maker has no fold. Once marked, ✓ I have one takes the state line, where it unmarks. Labels, presses and 44px targets are unchanged.
