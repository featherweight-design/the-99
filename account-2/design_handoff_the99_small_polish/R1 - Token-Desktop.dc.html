One height per row

The grid goes from items-start to stretch, as the precon tiles already do. Rules text sits in the frame under the caption, at most three lines. The space a shorter tile gains goes above its foot, so feet line up along the row. An open fold grows its own row only.

Matches the deck tile

Nothing is drawn over the art, as on DeckCardTile. The art still opens card details. Hover lifts the border to --border-strong (the Pending tile shows it) and, with item 3, brightens the art.

Pending and refused

A pending press keeps its label, takes aria-busy and draws at 60%. A refusal is one line inside the foot, under the state line, in --fg-locked as today. Ownership never takes a warning color.
