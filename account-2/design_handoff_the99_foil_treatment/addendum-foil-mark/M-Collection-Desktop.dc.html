The glint leads the meta line, the slot that speaks about your copies. A collection tile has no ownership dot (every tile is a card you own), so the glint takes the dot’s place: ✦ then the in-use chip, then the fact marks. Copies on the left, the card’s facts after them, so the glint never sits among the Game Changer and tutor marks. The name line is untouched: names keep their full width. Jace is etched (silver). Dina ×3 holds 1 foil and 2 plain, so it shows nothing, as the film does. The key gains foil and etched. Hover shows the one-line tooltip (pointer only).
