Shopping is the same row component. The glint can only appear where you own the card and Shopping still lists it: your copies are sleeved elsewhere and a second copy is the move (Ashling, Zimone). That is the busiest row the app draws: owned dot, name, glint, Game Changer, tutor, the figure, over $5, pips, short, the price tag, ⇄ and ✕. The glint takes 18px of the name. On a row this full the shipped Shopping row already truncates the name; with the glint Ashling truncates sooner, and the full name stays in the row’s label and its card details. Unowned rows never draw it. The Shopping key is unchanged: the deck key above the list carries foil.

A precon tile reads the box, not your collection (printedFoil, as the film does), so a Collector’s Edition puts the glint on every tile, owned or not. That is the worst case for density: 100 glints, one per name line, and the film already says it louder. The regular edition beside it draws none. The precon key carries foil only when a tile in view is foil. The read-only list rows keep the word they already print after the printing (MSC 4 · foil, shipped); see gap M4.
