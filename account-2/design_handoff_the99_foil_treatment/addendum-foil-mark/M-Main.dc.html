Foil treatment · addendum · the foil mark · R3

A glint beside the ownership dot says “this copy is foil”

The film shows foil on art, and nowhere else: not in list views, not at a glance, not in forced colours. This adds one small mark, from the card-mark family, beside the ownership indicator on every tile and row that draws a card you own. R2 after Hugh’s audit: moved beside the dot, Collector’s Edition kept, the precon list row’s word swapped for the glint. R3: All and Shopping share one two-line row at both widths, fact marks on the name line. Source: the foil handoff R1 and R2 on main; the foil rule is unchanged. Boards: Collection and Deck/precon at 1300 and 390, to the right.

The calls
QuestionCalled
The markA glint: one four-point star, solid, no outline. The shape every foil-tracking app and binder sticker already uses for “shiny”, and unlike every glyph the app draws today (bolt, peak, turn, tutor arrow, lemniscate, diamonds, dots).
Its inkThe film’s own colours, as a 135° gradient (pink, gold, mint, blue, violet). It is the one mark in the app that is not one flat ink, and that is the point: it reads as the film, shrunk to a glyph, so a reader who has seen one foil tile decodes it without the key.
Its familyThe card mark’s form rules, reused: bare (no box, no pill, no plate), solid mass, one size on every surface, no motion, absent when it has nothing to say. Not --fact: that ink means “a bracket fact about the card”. Not a slot box: the box is the one availability slot, and a second box would read as a second slot.
Size12px, everywhere. It rides the name line, a 12-13px name and a 10.5px count, which is why the combo glyph on the same lines is 12px too (its key entry says so). 16px stood taller than the name it follows.
WhereBeside the ownership indicator, so the finish reads with “you own this”, never among the Game Changer and tutor marks. Deck tile, precon tile, decklist and Shopping rows: right after the dot, ● ✦, in one flex-none group. Collection list rows: in the count column, ×2 ✦. Collection tile (no dot, every tile is owned): first on the meta line, before the in-use chip and the facts. No column added, and every name keeps its full width.
EtchedIts own variant: the same glint in the etched film’s silver. Same shape, because both say “not a plain copy”; the ink says which, matching the film. Spoken and keyed apart.
Part-foilNothing. The rule stays the film’s: every owned copy of the drawn printing is foil, or etched. A part mark (hollow, half, a count) is a third look to learn for a state card details already spells out (×3 · 1 foil). Outline glyphs are also refused by the card-mark family.
ThemesOne recipe, six themes. Weakest measured: violet #C79BFF on kaldheim --elevated-hover, 7.2:1 (3:1 is the graphics floor). Silver’s weakest is 8.2:1.
Forced coloursThe glint draws in CanvasText. The film is off in forced colours, so the glint becomes the one foil cue left there; same for reduced transparency, where the film is swapped for a 2px edge and the glint is unchanged (it is opaque).
Keyfoil and etched join the key. On /collection they are unconditional, like the fact entries (a paged window). On a deck, its Shopping view and a precon they follow the cards in view, like the deck’s other entries.
Collector’s EditionKept. A precon tile follows the box (printedFoil), so every Collector’s Edition tile carries the glint. One rule on every surface, and in List view and forced colours, where there is no film, the glint is the only cue.
Card rowsOne two-line row for All and Shopping, at both widths (R3, Hugh). Line 1: dot and glint, name, //, ×N, fact marks, ✕. Line 2, indented under the name: pips and figure, then in use or short, the one other-ways press (⇄ and the spare glyph) and the buy tag. This is Social R2’s option B with the facts moved up beside the name, carried to desktop and to All, so the row has one shape everywhere and no mark can squeeze the name. Desktop rows grow from 34px to about 60px.
Precon list rowsThe printed word goes: MSC 4 · foil becomes the glint beside the dot and MSC 4. One vocabulary for foil across the app.
