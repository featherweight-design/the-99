Friends live in the account menu, on one page that starts empty

One row joins the account menu, Friends, and opens /friends. That page holds everything this round adds: the name friends see, your friends, your pods, and the invite links you have open. A friend is added only from a single-use link or its QR code, shown in one dialog (a sheet on a phone). The link opens /invite/[token], which answers every invite state in the same card. Pods are cards on the friends page, each with one inline checkbox for sharing spares. Every list draws its empty state first, because staging holds no social rows.

The calls (§Open, R1 items)
01Placement: the account menu, not a sixth nav item. The tab bar is five wide and already squeezes Commanders to 9px; friends are set up a few times, not visited daily; and R2 and R3 put social data where it is used (deck rows, the log form), not on this page. The row sits second, under About. The rejected sixth tab is drawn on the Placement board. 02Pods are a section of /friends, not a page. A pod is a short list of friends plus one checkbox. A page of its own would hold one card. 03One press makes one link. Invite a friend creates the invite and opens the dialog with the QR code on top, the link and a copy button under it (Share first on a phone, through the native share sheet). The link is single-use, so a second press makes a second link. 04Open links are listed under the pods with the time left, Show (the same dialog, for the table) and Revoke. Only open links are listed: used, declined, revoked and expired links leave the list. A decline is never told, so the list cannot say it either. 05At 10 open links the button is held (aria-disabled), with one line under it saying why. A race past the cap answers tooMany in the shared toast. 06The landing is one card in every state. Signed out it names nobody and offers Sign in only, since the app is invite-only and a friend invite is not an app invite. Open: Accept or Decline, naming the inviter. Expired has its own line; used, declined, revoked and a bad token all read “This invite link no longer works”, so a forged token is told nothing new. 07The display name is never asked for. It starts as the Clerk first name (ensureProfile) and is changed inline wherever someone is about to see it: the page head, the invite dialog, the landing card. 30 characters. 08Remove, leave and delete confirm in the shared delete pane, inside the row’s … menu, as folders do. Each second sentence says what happens and that nobody is told. Removing a member from your pod is a bare ✕ with no confirm, since adding them back is one press. 09Sharing spares is one checkbox on each pod card, unticked by default, never a global toggle and never in a settings block. 10After Accept you land on /friends with a one-line receipt (✕ to close) and the friend at the top of the list. After Decline, the card says the inviter isn’t told.
The locks (§Rulings), and where each one is honoured
Link or QR only, no searchNo search field, name lookup or suggestion anywhere. The only way in is Invite a friend, then a link or a QR code. Friendship is mutualOne Accept writes both sides. No requests, no pending friend. Pods from the owner’s friendsAdd friends lists your friends only. A member who arrived by a hand-on shows by name, marked “not a friend”. Hand-on to longest-standingLeave names the next owner in its confirm. The last member leaving deletes the pod, and that confirm says so. Spares view-only, per podOne checkbox per pod. No borrow, no ask, nothing in the cart. Spare lines themselves are R2. Name and public id onlyRows draw display names and initials. No email, no avatar image, no Clerk id. No model callsNames are typed and shown, never read by a prompt. 30 / 40 / 10 / 7 daysFields cap at 30 and 40; the 11th link is held; every link shows its expiry.
