Social · round 1 of 3 · design R2 · Hugh’s R1 audit, 2026-10-15

R2: friend links, one place to be yourself, and your commander as your face

Hugh found R1 sound but plain, and the word invite misleading. R2 keeps every R1 call and lock, and changes four things. The wording now says Add a friend and friend link, and the route is /friend/[token]. Your name and your commander are set in one place only, the head of /friends. Each player may pick a signature commander, and its art becomes their face everywhere a person is drawn. The friend request is drawn as a card. Pods are drawn two ways for Hugh to choose between. R1’s boards stay to the left for comparison, and every frame R2 does not redraw keeps R1’s layout with R2’s words and faces.

What changed (R2)
19Friend links, not invites. A link only adds an existing player as a friend, so the words never say invite. The button and dialog are Add a friend, the list is Open friend links and each row is a Friend link. The landing’s kicker is Friend request, and the path is /friend/. The function names in friends.ts can stay. 20One place to set yourself. The name line is gone from the dialog and the landing card (Hugh: no alias system). The name and the commander are set only in the head of /friends. 21A signature commander, chosen, never inferred. Press the face in the head, or Choose your commander while it is unset. The picker shows your own decks’ commanders first, then searches every commander in the catalog. One press picks, saves and closes. Use initials instead clears it. Unchosen, a face is initials, and nothing asks again. 22The face is spent everywhere a person is drawn: the head at 64px, friend rows at 40px with the commander’s name under the person’s, pod chips at 26px, the Add friends list, and the friend request card. 23The friend request is a card. Face up when the page may name the sender (open, already friends): the name bar, the art, the colour identity in the frame and pips, the type line, and the rule of friendship in the text box. Face down (the 99’s back) whenever R1 said it names nobody. Your own link shows your own card. Decline flips it face down. 24Pods: A, chips with faces, or B, the pod as a table of art seats over a blur of the same art. Both are drawn on the same pods. Hugh to choose; A is the default if he doesn’t. 25Already friends spends the link (Hugh, G4), and the app stays invite-only (Hugh, G3).
