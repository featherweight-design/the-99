Your commander is your face: its art stands in for initials wherever a friend is drawn

R2 adds one choice, a signature commander, and spends it everywhere a person is drawn: the page head, each friend row (with the commander’s name under theirs), each pod chip, and the friend request card. It is chosen here and only here, beside the name, which is also set here and only here (Hugh: one place to configure yourself). Unchosen, a face is initials, as in R1, and nothing asks twice. The wording moved too: Add a friend, friend links, Open friend links. Frames R1 drew that change only in words (one link sent, remove, after remove, ten open) keep R1’s layout with these faces and words.
