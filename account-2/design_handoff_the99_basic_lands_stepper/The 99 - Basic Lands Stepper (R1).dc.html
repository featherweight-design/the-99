Five Islands in five presses, saved once.

On a basic land's row, the count becomes a stepper: − count +, in the place the ✕ and the ×N held. Presses move the count at once and one save writes the final figure. Every other card keeps its ✕. Adding 5 Islands goes from about 7 actions to 5 presses (or one typed figure); removing 3 is 3 presses with no waiting.

The five open calls
01
Which control
A stepper on basic rows, with a typed count between − and +.

Stepper and tap-the-count in one: the count is the shipped collection stepper's field, so the 35-Island deck is one typed figure, not fifteen presses. No basics panel this round (gap G1): it is the one option that adds a new surface, and the locks ask for a light addendum.

02
What − does at 1
At 1 the − becomes the shipped ✕, and pressing it removes the row.

The row leaves into #185's receipt, Island removed · Undo, so a mis-press is one press away from back. No row held at 0: a count of nought is never drawn. The glyph changes so the press that removes the row looks like removal.

03
Where it sits on a Card view tile
A stepper band across the foot of the art.

44px tall on the phone, 32px on desktop, full tile width. The tile does not grow, so the grid stays even. A basic's art says nothing the name does not, and it has no cost pips to cover. The caption loses its ×N and its ✕ column, as the row does.

 
04
Desktop reveal
Always visible, quiet at rest.

The stepper holds the count, and a count that appears only on hover is a count most visits never see. At rest the glyphs sit in --border-dashed ink, as the ✕ does, and lift to --fg-body on row hover. The tile band follows the same rule.

05
The 1 → 2 change marker
from 6 after the name, the new count in the stepper.

The shipped pair prints 6 → 8 in the name's place. With a stepper, the 8 is already on the row, so the pair loses its right half and keeps what only it knows: the committed figure. The 2px accent edge stays as shipped. Back at the committed count, both go.
