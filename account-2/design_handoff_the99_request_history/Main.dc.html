Request history

Every spare request you made, and every one made of you, with how it ended and when. The data is already kept: a ✕ only marks an ask cleared, never deletes it. Every frame is the shipped shell and the shipped Friends page, rendered from main with the shipped CSS, with this round’s proposal applied.

Calls

1

The way in is “History ›” on Your requests’ head, beside “newest first” (E1). With nothing open but a history, the section stays as its head and “Nothing open right now.” (E2). With no history at all, the section is absent, as today. The answer sheet’s “All your requests ›” is unchanged.

2

One page, /friends/requests: the friend page’s “‹ Friends”, a title, one line, and the shipped segmented switch (compact; fills the width on a phone): You asked · Asked of you. No new menu row.

3

Rows are Your requests’ shipped rows: the card at card shape with the friend’s face in its corner, kicker, title, line. No ✕ and no Ask again: history only shows, and the live list keeps every action (one control per action). The whole row opens card details.

4

Outcomes in the shipped words. You asked: Requested, Accepted, Declined, No answer, Withdrawn. Asked of you: Waiting on you, Accepted, Declined, No answer. The kicker’s date is when it ended (an open one: when asked); the line names the deck and the day you asked.

5

An open request says where to act, never offers it here: “still open, 12 days left” on yours, “Answer it under Waiting on you.” on theirs.

6

Cleared asks show. Withdrawn asks never show on the holder’s side: they were never told, and history must not tell them now.

7

Month groups, newest first, 30 at a time, then “Show older requests” (Collection history’s “Show older changes”).

8

An empty tab names where requests come from (H3), since one tab can be empty while the other is not.

For Hugh · two calls, each with a recommendation

Q1

How far back? Recommended: everything that’s kept. Rows live until the account is deleted, and a player’s requests number in the dozens, so paging covers it with no retention job. Other option: the last 90 days, like Collection history.

Q2

Requests with ex-friends? Recommended: hidden, with a faint foot line only when the player has some: “Requests with people you’re no longer friends with don’t show here. Add them back and they return.” (H2). Rows survive an unfriend, so re-friending brings them back, and every read keeps the friend-grant join (check:social-reads). Other option: keep them as “A former friend”, which needs a read outside the grant.

Gaps for the build

G1

loadRequestHistory(viewerId, { direction, cursor }) in src/lib/social/, joining friend_links on the viewer, newest first, 30 a page. The holder direction leaves withdrawn out.

G2

G3
