Arrange decks, mid-drag

A mode of the Decks page, inside the shipped shell. The grid becomes one column of rows so a drag can’t open a deck.

PHONE · 390
DESKTOP · 1300

Rows: the tile’s art, name and status line; desktop adds the position and commander. The handle (44×52, on the right) is the only move control.

Moving: drag the handle (touch starts at once on the handle; the page still scrolls elsewhere). Keyboard: Space lifts, ↑ ↓ move, Space drops, Esc puts it back. The lifted row raises with the accent edge; a dashed slot shows where it lands.

Done saves the whole order, Cancel drops it (phone: pinned above the tab bar). Filters and search step aside while arranging. In a folder, only that folder’s decks are listed.
