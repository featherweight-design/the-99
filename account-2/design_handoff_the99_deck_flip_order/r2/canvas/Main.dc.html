WAYS TO FLIP

A · Deck strip (recommended). A thin row above the band: ‹ All decks on the left, 2 of 5 ‹ › on the right. It also gives the phone a way back to the list, which it doesn’t have today.

B · Neighbour buttons. Under the header: Previous and Next, each naming the deck it goes to. Clearest, but it’s the tallest.

C · Switch deck. One more button in the header’s actions row, “2 of 5”, opening the whole list (sheet on phone, menu on desktop). Jumps to any deck, not just the next one. Desktop’s ⋯ wraps to a second line at 1300, so it needs room.

D · Next deck at the foot. The shipped deck tiles at the end of the page. No new chrome up top, but you have to scroll to find it. Pairs well with A.

All four: ← and → on desktop (not while typing or in tabs, carousels, menus or sheets). Ends wrap. Hidden when the list holds one deck. The tab you’re on is kept.

CUSTOM ORDER

The shipped sort menu gains Custom and an Arrange decks… row (same menu on phone, from ⇅).

Arrange turns the grid into rows with a handle. The handle is the only move control: drag it, or Space then ↑ ↓ on a keyboard. Done saves, Cancel drops it. Tiles stay links; no drag on the grid.

Saved to your account, so phone and desktop match. In a folder, Arrange orders that folder’s decks. Flipping follows whatever order the list is in.
