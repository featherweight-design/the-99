R3 · AFTER HUGH’S R2 NOTES

One deck strip, four places to put it

The strip is now the same at both widths: ‹ All decks on the left, 2 of 5 and two arrows on the right. The desktop crumb and the hover card are gone (the arrows keep a plain tooltip). Only where it sits changes from board to board.

PLACEMENTS

A1 · Above the art. R2’s A, made consistent. +52px phone, +48px desktop. Farthest from the thumb.

A2 · Under the art (my pick). The strip becomes the first row under the commander image on phone, and a row under the band on desktop. Same height as A1, but it sits beside the title and count instead of above the art, about a third of the way down the phone.

A3 · In the count row. “99 / 99 | 2 of 5 ‹ ›”, sharing the progress bar’s row. No new height at all; the bar gets shorter. No “‹ All decks” link, so phone still has no way back except the tab.

A4 · Pill by the thumbs. A small ‹ 2 of 5 › pill floating bottom right, above the add-card bar. Closest to the thumb and always there as you scroll, but it sits over the card list.

ARRANGE

Centred on desktop: yes, a 720px column with Cancel and Done on its heading.

Inside a folder (my pick: allow it): the menu row reads “Arrange Simic…” and lists only that folder’s decks. There’s still one order: moving them among themselves leaves every other deck where it was in All decks. Limiting it to All decks works too, but then a folder’s order can only be changed from a 30-deck list.
