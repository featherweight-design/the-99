1 · Where it sits

  • Step 7, after Suggested changes and before Get it to 99. Both write swaps for a deck: Suggested changes tunes a deck Wren built, Upgrade starts from a box. The last two steps finish decks (the count, then the shopping), so they stay last.
  • The bar gets nine segments; Step 7 of 9. The section’s lead and the start card say nine of the tools (frames below).

2 · Which precon

  • Prismari Artistry, the Upgrade handoff’s own fixture: led by Muddle (the box’s alternate), bracket 2 → 3, toward Spells, 14 swaps.
  • Chosen because it shows the part no other step does: a different commander leads, and the result opens with the Commander row.
  • Mutant Menace (the landing’s featured precon) would work the same, with whatever its run picks.

3 · What the visitor sees and selects

  • The precon’s name and the read-only chip, as Suggested changes heads its run. Then the shipped result: title and date, the four facts, the Commander row, and the first 3 swaps, then Show all 14 swaps (as step 4’s Show all 99).
  • Select a card: any name opens the demo’s read-only card details. The ring sits on the first added card with its label to the right, so it covers no row.
  • No step tracker (the title and facts already say all three picks) and no foot (Start the deck and Try another strategy write).
