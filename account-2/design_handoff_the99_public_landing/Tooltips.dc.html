The ⓘ on each paid tool: what it does, and what you get

Each tooltip says what the tool does in a sentence, then shows a small picture of what it gives back, drawn from the dummy app on Sam’s cards. The words in the picture are stand-ins for stored output. Phone: a popover under the row on press; desktop: on hover, focus or press. One open at a time; ✕-less, it closes on a press outside.
