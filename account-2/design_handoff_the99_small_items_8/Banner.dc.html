A rule keeps the diamond Mark, as shipped.
  2. One sentence. A label’s line is its lead and the first sentence of its rest. The banner never shows a second sentence; Show me lands on the full label. A rule or a new sentence alone is already one sentence.
  3. Retires LaunchCrop, CROP_SIZES, cropStyle, the crop theme refresh (ThemeRefresh here) and the per-width capture fetch. The banner no longer reads the capture manifest.
  4. Unchanged: the band, its heading, “and N more”, the cap, ✕, focus, and the arrivals’ own lines.
