Card scanning: shoot a batch, confirm each card, review, add

Source: docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-15-card-scan-feasibility.md on scan-real-photos, with its Real photos section (94% right on Hugh’s own taps; sleeved and hazy weakest; about 0.5s a frame; the margin gate). The flow mirrors ManaBox: the index once, Fast or Normal, a batch, a review, then add. Boards: Start (E1, D1 to D7), Viewfinder (V1 to V9), Review (R1 to R4, L1 to L4), Desktop (W1 to W6), About (A1, A2).

The calls (brief, Open for Design)
  1. Every result is a suggestion. Above the gate: the card at card size and one press, Add to the list (V3). Below it: Not sure. Which is it? with the top three at card size and None of these (V6). Nothing is added unseen: a press in the camera, the review, then one Save.
  2. The review is the Add cards list. Review closes the camera onto the shipped sheet with the batch on it (L1). A wrong card: press its name (scanned lines only) and the line opens on your scan and its three closest cards; one more press swaps it (L2). Normal mode uses the shipped required printing step, one card at a time, with Save held (L3).
  3. The viewfinder. No name until three frames agree, about 1.5s at the measured 0.5s a frame; the corners light pink while it reads (V2). On pointerdown the guess freezes and says Held; lift on the button to add, slide off to cancel (V4).
  4. Duplicate or second copy. A card that never left the frame is a duplicate: never offered again, with Add another copy as a deliberate press (R1). The same card after the frame was empty is a second copy: Add a second copy (R2). Fast folds copies into one line ×2 (R3); Normal folds them when the same printing is chosen, with the shipped “already on the list” line (R4).
  5. The index. About 27 MB, once, on the first Scan (D1): a real bar in MB (D2), offline before any download (D3), paused and resuming by itself (D4), a server error as the shared toast (D5). Ready opens straight into the camera (D7).
  6. Light. Dark for a second: one line names the light control top right and rings it (V7); no torch, no control, and the line asks for a lamp (V8); on, the control fills (V9).
  7. Where it lives. A Scan press beside the search in the Add cards sheet, both widths (E1, W1). Not a row and not a head button, so it is there with cards on the list too.
  8. Fast or Normal is one control at the top of the camera, remembered per device. Fast (default): the first printing the picker lists. Normal: you choose each printing on the list, before Save.
  9. Desktop. Scan turns the sheet’s search into a webcam panel and the list fills under it (W2, W3). No webcam, or blocked: Choose photos, several files, one card each, sure ones behind one press and Not sure ones with their top three (W4, W6).
Locks held
  1. It matches the card, not the printing: the player picks the set (Normal) or it takes the first (Fast).
  2. Photos are taken in our app. Nothing leaves for another app; kept frames live only for the session (the fix in L2).
  3. A frame is confirmed before it counts, and one Save writes one History entry that Revert takes back.
  4. Transient errors use ToastFrame. Every phone press is 44px or more; ✕ is bare.
