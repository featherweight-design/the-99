One way in for the page, one control for every card: Want it, in card details’ footer

The account menu gains a Wishlist row, after Friends, before the session buttons. Card details’ pinned footer gains one control between the link-out and Prev / Next: Want it. Every card in the app opens this sheet, so search, Collection, Discover, Commanders, a deck and its Shopping view all reach the wishlist the same way, and no row anywhere gains a button. Pressed, the control becomes the line itself: the star fills, you see what you have, and the stepper sets how many you want in all. Nothing else in the sheet changes.
