One page for the cards you want outside any deck: a search to add, Wanted, then Got

The page sits in the 860px column the account pages use. Each line is the card’s art, its name (opens card details), what you have against what you want, and a want stepper; ✕ takes the line off. A friend’s spare shows as a chip under the name, the same chip as on deck rows (Spares boards). A line is GOT the moment your collection holds the count: it takes a stamp, turns its have line green and moves to Got until you clear it. The route is new, so the whole page is new; the account menu row is on the Add boards.
