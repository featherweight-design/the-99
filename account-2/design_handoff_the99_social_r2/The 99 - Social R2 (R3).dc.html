Your notes on round 2, and what changed

Locks are unchanged. The wishlist is at card level with any printing. A card leaves the list once your collection holds as many as you want (changed this round, see G5). A friend’s wishlist is always visible, with no owned count. Spares are view only and shared per pod. There is no borrowing, no cart change, no model call, and no About entry.
