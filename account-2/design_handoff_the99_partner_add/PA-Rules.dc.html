Each pair ability

The flow is the same for every ability. Only the section title, the rule line and which cards are listed change.

V1 · Choose a Background

Add a Background: any Background, which leads beside the commander and isn’t a creature. Needs canPair to learn Backgrounds (G1).

V2 · Friends forever

Any other Friends forever commander. Same rows and confirm.

V3 · Doctor and companion

Add a companion on a Doctor; Add a Doctor on a companion. A Doctor is a Time Lord Doctor with no other creature types.

V4 · Partner with

Partner with names one card: one row, here to buy. Restricted Partner (Character select, Survivors, Father & son) lists only the same label.
