#491 · add a partner to an existing deck

Add a partner from outside the deck

A player builds a deck, then learns the commander can take a partner that isn’t in the 99. Drawn over the shipped Change commander sheet, its confirm, and Suggested changes’ cut line. Fixture: HD - Ishai (Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker, W/U), 99 cards, 37 lands, v3.

The calls

1Both doors. Change commander gets an Add a partner section above the shipped list (now headed Replace Ishai). The commander’s card details on its deck page gets a Second commander box whose button opens that section. Neither shows without a pair ability.
2Finding one. Yours first, then To buy, by the pair’s figure. Rows reuse the candidate row’s art, mark, figure and Choose, plus widens to when the pair adds colors. A partner already in the 99 is the shipped no-cut case.
3The card that leaves. Suggested changes’ cut line, three suggested, the first picked: a basic land while the deck runs 37+ lands, then the two cards the pair’s decks run least. Cut another card searches the 99. Exactly one pick.
4Wider colors. Only ever wider, so nothing becomes illegal. The confirm says the new identity and how many owned cards in the new colors the pair’s decks run, and lists them on press.
5Confirm, undo, history. Shipped pair CTA, lands in pending changes (Discard undoes). A deck toast with Undo follows. Review: shipped a partner joins. History: the shipped Ishai → Ishai & Bruse Tarl line.
6Every ability. Partner, Partner with, restricted Partner, Friends forever, Choose a Background, Doctor’s companion. Same flow; the title and rule line change.

For Hugh

Q1Both doors (as drawn), or Change commander only?
Q2Backgrounds drawn as supported, which needs G1. Keep, or leave Backgrounds for later?
Q3The toast’s Undo is new on this flow. Keep it, or rely on Discard (and no way back on a brew)?

Engineering

G1canPair has no Background kinds (`partner.ts`). Choose a Background + a Legendary Enchantment — Background, either order; a Background never leads alone.
G2A write for an outside partner with a cut: `changeCommander(deckId, to, {{ cutOracleId }})`. Refused when the cut isn’t in the 99 or is locked, or the deck would end over 100. `offerable()` stops holding the outside case back.
G3Undo from the toast: restores the single commander and the cut card in one write, only while the draft still holds that change unchanged.
G4Suggestions: lands vs 36, then least-run by the pair’s support list (the `growingHits` read). No model spend.
G5The opened-cards count: owned cards in the new colors that the pair’s decks run, from the same support list.
G6A deck already over 100 is not offered the add until it is at 100 or under (not drawn).
