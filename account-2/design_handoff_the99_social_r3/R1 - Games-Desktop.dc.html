Matchups: a square per game, a count, and nothing that reads as a rate

Matchups sits between the findings and History. Its heading line carries the denominator (recorded in 3 of 6 games) and a three-way switch shows one list at a time. A row is the opponent (commander art, color pips, or the friend), one result square per game in the History row’s own W / L / D squares, a trophy count where they won your loss, and the number of games. Below three games a row is a description, at thirty it is the same shape: no colour finding, no headline, no percentage at any N, ordered by games and then name. History rows gain a vs line under the marks, drawn from the snapshot alone. Staging has 12 games across 2 accounts, so the first frames are what a reader actually sees: mostly one-game rows.
