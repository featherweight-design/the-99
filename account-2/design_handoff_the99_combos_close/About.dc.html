Combos one card away⌃

Some decks are one card short of a combo. The line under the deck’s header, beside its version, says how many: “5 combos one piece away”.

Press it for the list. Each combo says where its missing card is:

  • A spare in your collection
  • In another of your decks, with a link to that deck
  • To buy, with its price

Open one to see how it works, step by step, and the mana it needs. You can add the missing card from there.

A card’s details list its combos the same way, with the pieces you already hold.

  1. 1Only two-card combos in this deck’s colors are counted.
  2. 2The turn-again mark is a combo that gives you extra turns.
  3. 3Combos, their steps and their mana come from Commander Spellbook.
