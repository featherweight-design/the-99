Calls that hold for every direction

  1. What opens (open call 3). A shell entry (A, B, Add several) opens the shipped Add cards sheet empty. A per-card press opens it with that card on the list and its printing step open: search’s + (C) uses Add cards so the reader can keep going; card details’ link (D) keeps the shipped Edit copies sheet, which is what it opens on /collection today. No new lighter step: the printing step and the finish chips are already the light part.
  2. Per card and per list (open call 4). Both. The per-card route is card details’ link everywhere (D), under whichever shell entry is chosen. The list route is the direction’s own entry.
  3. After saving (open call 5). Yes: the surface updates in place, because the search row and the deck row already read ownership live. The only new piece is a brief ring on the row that changed.
  4. Reach (open call 6). Every signed-in surface with the shell: your decks, a friend’s deck (a card you see in their deck is often one you have), search, Shopping, the cart. Not the public card page: it has no shell and its reader may be signed out.
  5. Switch off. Every entry here sits behind the collection edit switch, as the sheet does. Off, each surface is exactly as shipped, with no gap.
  6. Return. Closing the sheet (✕, Done, Escape or a save) puts the reader back on the same surface, at the same scroll, with search or card details reopened as they were when the press came from inside them.
