What A costs

A place in the nav that is not a place. Every other slot takes you somewhere and shows where you are; this one opens a sheet. On the phone the bar goes from 74.8px slots to 62.3px, and every label gets tighter for one action most readers use in bursts (a pack, a trade).

What A gets

The most findable answer: one press from every screen, phone and desktop, never hidden by scroll (the bar is fixed, the header is not). It is how ManaBox does it.
