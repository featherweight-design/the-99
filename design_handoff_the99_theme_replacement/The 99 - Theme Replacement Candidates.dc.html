Consequence of the cut

Keeping Oil and Iron and Rune Frost keeps both --accent AA failures at 14px — 4.3 and 4.5, the only two in the set. The link-out ruling that routes outbound ink to --accent-hover therefore stays permanent rather than transitional. I have set the floor for the two new accents at 6.0 so they add no further debt to that account.

4 · Renaming the set

Canopy and Witching Hour are in. With three of the four survivors renamed anyway, the naming system is the thing to decide once — three below, each internally consistent in category, because a set where two names are weather and two are minerals reads as an accident.

Sections 1–3 above use the pre-rename labels — they are the record of how the candidates were measured and proposed. This section is where the names are decided.

Settled · the plane prefix stays

Each theme stays anchored to a plane; the second half is what changes. That anchoring is a real constraint on the name, not decoration — a weather name now has to be weather that could occur on that plane, which is what separates this from a list of moods.

One name did not survive that test. Salt Fog was the first draft for New Phyrexia and it reached for a coastal mood on a plane made of oil and metal. It is now Oil Haze — still a condition of light, unmistakably Phyrexian, and it avoids a second ‘Rain’ sitting next to Kamigawa's.

The one real cost

Display names are free to change — they live in themes · copy strings.ts. Keeping the plane prefix means four of the six ids need no migration at all: amonkhet, kamigawa, phyrexia and kaldheim stay exactly as they are, and the new two take ixalan and eldraine. That is the practical dividend of the decision.

What still needs writing is the fallback for the two removals: users sitting on innistrad or duskmourn have a persisted data-theme that will no longer resolve. Map them to their nearest survivor rather than the default — innistrad → phyrexia and duskmourn → kaldheim, since the pairs were 15–25° apart, so nobody wakes up to a theme they did not choose.

Recommendation

Names: system B, Weather and hour — settled. Plane prefix retained. The six ship as Amonkhet · Low Sun, Kamigawa · Neon Rain, New Phyrexia · Oil Haze, Kaldheim · First Frost, Ixalan · Canopy, Eldraine · Witching Hour — three of the four survivors renamed, two of the six names kept, one plane-fit correction. Not chosen for churn: system A renames the same number of survivors. It is chosen because it keeps the two names already settled in §2 and because it is the only one of the three systems whose category survives a seventh theme — there is always another condition of light, whereas the mineral list runs out and the single words collide with UI copy.

Ixalan · Canopy and Eldraine · Witching Hour. Ember Steppe does not ship.

Canopy takes the wide corridor at 118° and clears its nearest neighbour by 61° — the only candidate that matches the survivors' own 62° spread. Witching Hour takes the narrow one at 300° with 35° of clearance to First Frost (the theme named Rune Frost above §4), which is tighter than ideal and carried by two things hue alone cannot do: a genuinely purple ground against First Frost's teal-tinted slate, and a nine-point chroma gap. Those two, side by side, are the first pair in this set that differ on ground temperature as well as accent.

Ember Steppe is the one to drop because it recreates the fault being repaired. At 33° it sits 24° from Low Sun (Gilded Stone above §4) — closer than Moonlit ever sat to Oil Haze — and 2° from --danger, which is fixed across all six. A destructive-action button and a primary accent within 2° of each other is a worse problem than a redundant theme, and the only lever left is lightness, which is not enough separation to carry meaning.

If you want a warm theme in the set regardless, the honest version is not rust: it is pushing Low Sun itself warmer and giving the vacated slot to a low-chroma bone theme, which separates on chroma rather than hue and so cannot collide with anything. Say the word and I will draw it.
