## Must-nots

- No verdict word (`missing`, `short`, `need`) and no `--warning` / `--danger` on ownership.
- A token never changes a deck's counts, status pill, complete or to-buy state, the Shopping pill, or the `/decks` roll-up.
- Not known is never drawn as not owned. No dashed dot before the read.
- No emblems, no markers. Two tokens of one name are never merged and never told apart by a number.
- No motion. Joining re-renders the control in place.
