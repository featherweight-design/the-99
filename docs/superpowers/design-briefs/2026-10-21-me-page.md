# Brief: The Me page (your own account, in one place)

2026-10-21. For Design. Scoping only: nothing is built, and nothing will be until this brief has a handoff with a
READINESS verdict. Hugh approved scoping it (via d9, 2026-10-21). The product questions at the end are open; the
orchestrator walks Hugh through them, and the answers land here as rulings before Design's round closes.

**Hugh's idea, as relayed:** one page for your own account, with your friends, the features switched on for you
(per-account switches and groups), and a commander picture as your avatar.

**What this brief asks.** What a player comes to the page for, what it holds, what it links to, and what stays where
it is today, on phone and desktop. One Design round.

## The problem

What the app knows about you is spread over four places, and one of them is not ours:

- **Who friends see** (your name and your commander face) is set only in the head of `/friends`
  (`src/components/social/friends-head.tsx`). A player with the friends switch off has no way to see or set it, and
  the account menu draws initials (`src/components/shell/account-menu.tsx:395`, from `src/lib/shell/identity.ts`), not
  the face friends see.
- **Your credits** are on `/credits`, reached from the menu's chip.
- **Your sign-in, email and account deletion** are Clerk's hosted page, reached from "Manage account"
  (`account-menu.tsx:503`), behind the paid-credits deletion warning (`src/components/credits/delete-warning`).
- **What you are trying early** is nowhere a player can see. Switches resolve per reader (account override, then
  group, then app-wide, then the variable: `src/lib/switches/resolve.ts`), and only `/admin/accounts/[clerkId]` can
  explain an account's switches today (`explainAccount`, `src/lib/switches/explain.ts:21`). A friend Hugh has put in a
  test group has no way to know they are in one, or what it turned on.

There is no `/settings` route and nothing in `user_prefs` is player-editable today; the theme lives in a cookie and is
changed only from the menu. Me does not become a settings page in this round.

## What the player comes to Me for

1. **"What do my friends see of me?"** The face and the name, large, with a way to change both.
2. **"What can I do here that others can't yet?"** The features switched on for them early, in player words, each
   linking to where it lives.
3. **"Where is my stuff?"** One line each for friends and credits, linking out. Not a second copy of either page.
4. **"How do I change my email or delete my account?"** A link to Clerk's page, the deletion warning in front.

## Contents, top to bottom (Design owns the layout)

1. **Your face and name.** The `face.tsx` face at its largest size, the name friends see, and the existing commander
   picker and name edit. When no commander is set, the default (question 1) is drawn, marked as a default the player
   can change.
2. **Friends.** A count and a link to `/friends`; the pending friend-link count if any. Drawn only under
   `SOCIAL_FRIENDS_ENABLED`.
3. **Credits.** The held figure (`heldCredits`) and a link to `/credits`. Drawn only under `PAYMENTS_ENABLED`.
4. **Trying early.** The features on for this reader by an account or group override while off for everyone else.
   Each row: the feature's player-facing name, one line on what it does, and a link to where it lives. Empty state:
   the section is not drawn. Group names are question 4b.
5. **Your account.** "Manage sign-in" (Clerk), and Sign out if Design wants it here as well as in the menu.

## What the server can tell the page

- Face and name: `social_profiles`, one row per account (`ensureProfile` creates it on first need).
- Friends and pending links: the existing `/friends` loaders in `src/lib/social/`.
- Credits: `heldCredits`.
- Trying early: `explainAccount` returns, per switch, on or off and the rung that decided it (account, group,
  app-wide, variable, or a parent). "Trying early" is a switch that is on with source account or group while the
  app-wide value and the variable are off. **Gap:** the registry (`src/lib/switches/registry.ts`, 53 switches) carries
  no player-facing name or description; a copy table is needed, and only `feature` and `model` switches qualify
  (`plumbing` is never shown, and the resolver ignores its overrides).

## Every state to draw

At 390 and desktop: a player with friends, a commander face and credits; a new player (initials or the default face,
no friends, no credits); trying-early present and absent; friends switch off (no Friends section, face and name still
shown); payments off (no Credits section). The account menu with the face in place of initials, both widths. The
Moment bubble (question 7) and the first-invite prompt (question 6), if Hugh rules them in.

## Open for Design to call

1. Layout and order of the sections; whether Me is one column at every width.
2. Whether the menu's header block (face, name, email) becomes a link to Me, or Me is a row like the others.
3. How a default face reads as a default without nagging.
4. How the trying-early rows are marked (a small "early" tag, or the heading alone).

## Copy

Every string is a placeholder for `/copy-review`. The trying-early names and lines are a new copy table, one per
qualifying switch, and are the bulk of the words.

## About entries

Me is a feature, so it owes an About entry in the round that ships it (AGENTS.md, design pipeline). Design draws the
artboard in `design/design_handoff_the99_about/`; Hugh decides whether it is owed.

## Not in this brief

- Welcome-credits onboarding (prompt 5, `2026-10-18-welcome-credits-onboarding.md`). Me shows the balance; it does not
  explain credits.
- Editing switches, joining or leaving a group, or any player control over what is on.
- Moving the theme picker, or a settings page.
- Replacing Clerk's page.

## Product questions (open; recommended answers in the orchestrator's walk-through)

1. The default face: the first deck's commander until the player picks, or initials until they pick?
2. Does the face-and-name editor move from `/friends` to Me, or appear on both?
3. Does the menu keep its Friends row once Me exists?
4. Trying early: only features on for you early, or every feature on for you? 4b: Do you see your group's name?
5. Your face's art: the copy you own, or the catalog's?
6. A prompt at your first invite to set your face and name?
7. A Moment bubble pointing at Me?
8. Does Me exist for a player whose friends switch is off?
