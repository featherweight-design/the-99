Overview

This Jeskai artifact deck uses Inspirit, Flagship Vessel to add counters each combat, then multiplies them with proliferate effects like Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus and Kilo, Apogee Mind. It aims to reach twenty counters on Darksteel Reactor and to attack with growing artifact creatures.

Primer

Core strategy
  1. Cast cheap artifacts and ramp, then land Inspirit, Flagship Vessel around turn 3.
  2. Each combat, Inspirit, Flagship Vessel adds two charge counters to Darksteel Reactor or a counter to an artifact creature.
  3. Proliferate with Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus, Surge Conductor and Tezzeret's Gambit to grow every counter pile.
  4. Reach twenty charge counters on Darksteel Reactor to win the game.
  5. Backup: attack with Kappa Cannoneer and Steel Overseer while counters pile up.
Mulligan

Keep 2 to 4 lands; send back 0 to 1, or 5 or more. (The app's fixed line, the same on both sides.)

Keep
  • Solar Array with cheap artifacts starts the counter engine.
  • Surge Conductor with a second artifact to trigger it.
Send back
  • Expensive wipes with no artifacts to cast or tap for them.
  • Lands that miss white or red with no way to fix.
Tips
  • Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus doubles each proliferate, so Tezzeret's Gambit adds two counters to every pile.
  • Kilo, Apogee Mind proliferates whenever it becomes tapped, and Steel Overseer can tap to add counters too.

Key cards

  • Darksteel Reactor Wins the game at twenty charge counters.
  • Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus Doubles every proliferate you do.
  • Surge Conductor Proliferates when another artifact enters.
  • Kilo, Apogee Mind Proliferates whenever it becomes tapped.
  • Tezzeret's Gambit Draws two cards and proliferates.
  • Kappa Cannoneer Grows and goes unblocked with each artifact.
  • Steel Overseer Adds counters to all artifact creatures.
  • Lux Artillery Deals 10 damage at thirty counters.

Weaknesses

  • Moderate: Artifact hate like Collector Ouphe or Vandalblast shuts off or destroys the artifact engine.
  • Moderate: If Inspirit, Flagship Vessel is exiled or countered repeatedly, the steady counter supply slows a lot.
  • Moderate: Wins mostly through counters on Darksteel Reactor, so an exile effect like Farewell or a bounce effect stalls the plan.

Overview

Inspirit, Flagship Vessel leads a Jeskai artifact deck that stacks charge and +1/+1 counters on its artifacts, multiplies them by proliferating, and wins by growing huge artifact creatures or a counter-based finisher.

Primer

Game plan
Early
Etherium Sculptor cheapens artifacts, then Inspirit, Flagship Vessel adds counters to another artifact each combat around turn 3.
Mid
Surge Conductor proliferates for each artifact entering, and Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus makes every proliferate happen twice.
Close
Darksteel Reactor wins at twenty charge counters, and Lux Artillery deals 10 damage once thirty counters are out.
Alternate precon only
Kilo, Apogee Mind leads with haste and proliferates whenever it taps, so counters come from attacking.
Mulligan

4 lands fits a 40-land deck with 11 spells costing five or more.

Keep
  • Thought Monitor with a few artifacts makes a cheap start.
Send back
  • Coretapper with no other artifact leaves counters nowhere to go.
Tips
  • Deepglow Skate doubles counters on your artifacts, and Resourceful Defense moves them onward when a counter-holder leaves.
  • Cyberdrive Awakener turns noncreature artifacts into attackers, and Alibou, Ancient Witness hits for each tapped artifact.

Key cards

  • Steel Overseer Adds +1/+1 counters to every artifact creature.
  • Insight Engine Draws more each activation as counters grow.
  • Kappa Cannoneer Grows and evades with each artifact entering.
  • Threefold Thunderhulk Makes Gnome tokens that carry counters.
  • Empowered Autogenerator Adds more mana as charge counters grow.
  • Thrummingbird Proliferates when it connects in the air.
  • Karn's Bastion Land that proliferates late.

Weaknesses

  • Moderate: An artifact-heavy deck loses its engine to Vandalblast or Collector Ouphe before Inspirit reaches hexproof.
  • Minor: Only 4 of 10 interaction pieces are instant speed, so a fast combo can resolve before sorcery-speed Fumigate acts.
  • Minor: Several lands enter tapped, such as Mystic Monastery, which slows curving artifacts into the commander.
