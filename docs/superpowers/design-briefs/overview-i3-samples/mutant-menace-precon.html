Today (stored Overview)

Overview

The Wise Mothman leads a Sultai counters deck built on radiation. Rad counters mill nonland cards, which grow creatures, make Zombie Mutant tokens and draw cards. Counter doublers and proliferate effects then inflate evasive attackers until combat damage finishes the game.

Primer

Core strategy
  1. Ramp into The Wise Mothman around turn 4, so its enter trigger gives each player a rad counter.
  2. Use Mirelurk Queen and Screeching Scorchbeast to turn milled nonland cards into cards and tokens.
  3. Add Branching Evolution and Corpsejack Menace so every counter placed counts for much more.
  4. Proliferate with Agent Frank Horrigan and Inexorable Tide, then attack with flyers and evasive Mutants.
  5. Alternate win: Glowing One and Nightkin Ambusher stack rad counters so opponents mill themselves out.
Mulligan

Keep 2 to 4 lands; send back 0 to 1, or 5 or more. (The app's fixed line, the same on both sides.)

Keep
  • Mirelurk Queen with early ramp turns rad counters into cards.
  • Cultivate plus a counter doubler sets up the commander well.
Send back
  • Hands with no black or blue source for the commander's colors.
  • Only expensive spells and no ramp or rad enabler.
Tips
  • Raul, Trouble Shooter mills each player, and The Wise Mothman then adds counters to creatures for each nonland card milled.
  • Branching Evolution and Winding Constrictor together make each +1/+1 counter placed far larger.
  • The Master, Transcendent can reanimate a creature milled this turn, which Raul, Trouble Shooter helps set up.

Key cards

  • The Wise Mothman Turns milled nonland cards into counters.
  • Mirelurk Queen Draws and grows when nonland cards are milled.
  • Screeching Scorchbeast Attacks to trigger rad and make Zombie tokens.
  • Raul, Trouble Shooter Mills each player to trigger the commander.
  • Branching Evolution Doubles every counter the commander places.
  • Agent Frank Horrigan Proliferates counters twice on entering and attacking.
  • Glowing One Piles rad counters onto damaged opponents.
  • Vault 12: The Necropolis Makes tokens from rad counters, then pumps them.

Weaknesses

  • Moderate: Only 2 of 8 interaction pieces are instant speed, so a fast combo can resolve before Atomize or Putrefy answers it.
  • Moderate: If The Wise Mothman is removed, much of the mill-to-counter payoff stops, though other cards still trigger from mills.
  • Minor: Wins mostly through combat, so a fog or Propaganda slows the counter-laden attackers.

Overview

The Wise Mothman leads a Sultai deck that hands every player rad counters, mills cards, and turns each nonland card milled into +1/+1 counters, then wins by attacking with a bigger team.

Primer

Game plan
Early
Talisman of Resilience and Cultivate set up The Wise Mothman around turn 4, which gives each player a rad counter and grows creatures off mills.
Mid
Branching Evolution and Winding Constrictor multiply every counter that the milled cards put on your creatures.
Close
Glowing One and Nightkin Ambusher pile on rad counters, and Mutational Advantage shields the countered team from removal.
Alternate precon only
The Master, Transcendent leads instead: it reanimates milled creatures, so the plan shifts from counters to stealing milled threats.
Mulligan

4 lands suit a top-heavy curve with 12 spells costing five or more.

Keep
  • Rampant Growth gets the commander down on time.
  • Guardian Project with cheap creatures refills the hand.
Send back
  • Top-end spells with no Strength Bobblehead or other ramp stall.
Tips
  • Raul, Trouble Shooter mills each player when it taps, and Tato Farmer can put a land milled that turn onto the battlefield.
  • Inexorable Tide proliferates on every spell you cast, so Agent Frank Horrigan's double proliferate grows counters and rad faster.

Key cards

  • Mirelurk Queen Turns each mill into cards and growth.
  • Screeching Scorchbeast Attacks to mill, then makes Zombie Mutant tokens.
  • Vault 12: The Necropolis Turns rad counters into a wide, grown team.
  • Marcus, Mutant Mayor Rewards countered attackers with extra cards.
  • Vexing Radgull Flies in and spreads rad counters.
  • Atomize Clears a blocker and adds counters at instant speed.

Weaknesses

  • Moderate: Only 2 of 8 interaction pieces are instants, so a fast Thassa's Oracle combo usually resolves before the deck can answer.
  • Moderate: Wins mostly through combat, so Propaganda or a fog slows every attacker the counters have grown.
  • Minor: Repeated Swords to Plowshares on the commander stops the mill trigger that grows the team, leaving only the other counter pieces.
