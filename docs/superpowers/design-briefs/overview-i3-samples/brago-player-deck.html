Overview

Azorius flicker deck led by Brago, King Eternal. It fills the board with enter-the-battlefield creatures and re-uses them with Ghostly Flicker, Ephemerate and Conjurer's Closet. It can end the game with the Deadeye Navigator and Peregrine Drake loop, or by casting Approach of the Second Sun twice.

Primer

Core strategy
  1. Play ramp and cheap enter-the-battlefield creatures like Mulldrifter and Cloudblazer to build cards and board.
  2. Cast Brago, King Eternal around turn 4 and connect in the air to blink your permanents.
  3. Re-use triggers with Ephemerate, Ghostly Flicker and Soulherder while Skyclave Apparition clears threats.
  4. Alternate win: Deadeye Navigator and Peregrine Drake blink each other for infinite mana.
  5. Backup win: cast Approach of the Second Sun, then recast it from hand once it returns seventh from the top.
Mulligan

Keep 2 to 4 lands; send back 0 to 1, or 5 or more. (The app's fixed line, the same on both sides.)

Keep
  • Mulldrifter with a flicker spell and early mana starts the engine.
  • Deadeye Navigator beside Peregrine Drake gives a loop.
Send back
  • Hands with only one color of mana and no way to cast the blue spells.
  • Flicker spells with no creatures to target.
Tips
  • Ghostly Flicker and Archaeomancer return each other's targets, and with Peregrine Drake they make an infinite loop.
  • Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines doubles enter triggers, so each blink with Ephemerate draws or removes twice.
  • Brago, King Eternal with Strionic Resonator copies its trigger to blink again for free.

Key cards

  • Brago, King Eternal Blinks permanents after combat damage.
  • Deadeye Navigator Lets paired creatures blink for mana.
  • Peregrine Drake Untaps lands to fuel the loop.
  • Ghostly Flicker Blinks two permanents at instant speed.
  • Mulldrifter Draws cards each time it enters.
  • Skyclave Apparition Exiles a threat when it enters.
  • Approach of the Second Sun Wins after two casts from hand.
  • Soulherder Blinks a creature every end step.

Weaknesses

  • Moderate: Only 4 of 17 interaction cards are instants, so a fast combo from Thassa's Oracle can resolve before the deck answers.
  • Moderate: The deck relies on a few loops or a slow double cast, and Stony Silence or counterspells can stall them.
  • Minor: Removal such as Swords to Plowshares on Peregrine Drake or Deadeye Navigator in response breaks the loop.
