## 1. Role drill-down: press a role count to see its cards

**The job.** A player whose deck is over on a role wants to see which cards fill it, so they can choose what to cut.
Today the counts say *how many* and never *which*.

**What exists today.**
- **Recommended mix strip** on the deck page (`/decks/[id]`), `src/components/decks/role-counter-strip.tsx:127-148`, mounted
  once at `src/components/decks/builder-shell.tsx:1897` for both widths. Five tiles (ramp, draw, removal, wipes, land), each
  `have` of `target`. A tile is a button only when it is under target (it opens that role's Consider shelf, or Suggest, or
  Balance mana on the land tile). At or over target it is a static figure with no action
  (`src/components/decks/role-counter-cell.tsx:3-4`) and turns `--success` (`src/lib/decks/mix.ts:192-193`).
- **"What we counted"** on the Analysis tab, `src/components/decks/cuts-free-count.tsx:163-211`, rows from `cutCounts`
  (`src/lib/tuning/cuts-count.ts:76-128`): role overages (`rec N · K over`), curve crowding, bracket flags, EDHREC spread.
  Each row is a source tag, a label, a mono figure and a note. Nothing is pressable.
- **Stats, Jobs**, `src/components/decks/deck-stats-view.tsx:145-199`: six rows (ramp, draw, removal, wipes, protection,
  finisher). A row with a count above zero is a disclosure that lists the card names, sorted and comma-joined
  (`:177-189`). This is the only per-role card list in the app today, and the nearest precedent.

**The ruling Design must re-rule.** The tuning hub says the free pass names no card:
`design/design_handoff_the99_tuning_hub/README.md:90` ("The free pass names no card") and
`design/design_handoff_the99_tuning_hub/STATES-tuning-hub.md:68` (the Ready row must not "name, rank or order any card").
That ruling keeps the free count from reading as a cut list, so Look deeper (the model) stays the one place that names
cuts. Item 1 asks for the cards behind a count, in both places. Design rules whether "What we counted" may now open a
role's cards, and how that list stays clearly "the cards in this role", not "the cards to cut" (no order by cut
priority, no Cut press per card unless Design rules one).

**The count rule (binding).** A drill-down's list must match the figure the player pressed. Every count here is a label
tally: a card counts in every role it fills (`jobTally`, `src/lib/decks/job-tally.ts:46-59`), not its single
`card.role`. So a card can appear in more than one role's list, and the lists can sum to more than the deck. Engineering
notes for the build, not for Design to draw:
- The strip uses `jobTally` (via `src/lib/decks/label-role-counts.ts`), while "What we counted" reads `profile.roles`
  (`src/lib/profile/build.ts:244-294`, `src/lib/tuning/cuts.ts:109`), which can exclude commanders. The same role can show
  two different figures today. The build makes each list come from the same tally as the figure it opens.
- With labels unavailable, the strip falls back to single-role `card.role` (`label-role-counts.ts:64-66`); Jobs shows
  nothing. The fallback list must still match its figure.

**States to draw.**
1. A strip tile at, over and under target: which are pressable now, and what pressing an at-or-over tile opens.
   Under target already opens the Consider shelf; Design says whether that stays, or the tile offers both.
2. The open list for a role on the strip: card rows, the count matching the tile, at both widths.
3. A card that fills two roles, seen in both lists.
4. A "What we counted" role-overage row, pressable, and its open list.
5. A role with zero cards (nothing to open).
6. Whether a card in the list can be cut from there, or opens its card detail, or both.

**Open calls for Design.**
1. The surface: inline disclosure (as Jobs does), a sheet, or a jump to the decklist filtered to that role.
2. Whether the strip and "What we counted" share one list component.
3. Whether the tuning hub's no-card rule holds for the model's input display, with the list behind a press as the
   only exception.

## 2. Consider with a role over target

**The job.** In Consider, a shelf for a role the deck already has too much of (for example wipes, "you have 5 of 3")
still offers more cards for that role, and gives no way to trim.

**What exists today.** `buildShelves`, `src/components/decks/consider-panel.tsx:49-66`. Shelves are ordered by how
short the deck is; an over-target shelf just sorts later (its gap is negative) and still draws if it has owned
suggestions. The header reads `you have {have} of {target}` (`src/lib/copy/decks-builder.ts:129`), so it prints
"you have 5 of 3". There is no over-target branch and no cut path anywhere in Consider or the Add panel; the only cut
path is Proposed cuts on the Analysis tab.

**The handoff defines nothing for this.** `design/design_handoff_the99_add_panel/README.md:14` orders shelves "by the
coverage the deck is short of"; `:117-120` defines only the targetless "Threats & synergy" shelf (sorts last, header
`no target — this is the deck's plan`). A role past its target is not drawn.

**States to draw.**
1. An over-target shelf header: its wording in place of "you have 5 of 3".
2. Whether an over-target shelf shows at all, collapses, or sorts after "Threats & synergy".
3. A way to cut from there: open the role's cards (item 1's list) from the shelf header, and cut from that list.
4. A shelf exactly at target.
5. A deck where every role is at or over target.

**Open calls for Design.**
1. Hide, collapse or keep the over-target shelf's suggestions.
2. The cut entry: reuse item 1's list (recommended, one list for one job) or a separate control.
3. Wording, for `/copy-review` to settle after Design proposes.

## 3. Past Wizards announcements: an archive

**The job.** Browse every stored Wizards post, not just the last 14 days, by kind and by date.

**What exists today.**
- **Store:** `wizards_announcements` (`src/db/schema.ts:4392-4414`), one public row per post with `kind`, `title`,
  `excerpt`, `hero_image_url`, `published_at`, `first_seen_at`, `promised_decks` and a `baseline` flag for posts
  published before the ingest's first run. Nothing deletes posts, so they are kept forever (about 608 today). Hourly
  ingest from the Wizards sitemap, Announcements category only (`src/lib/announcements/ingest.ts`).
- **Kinds:** `secret-lair`, `commander-decklists`, `wizards-news` (`src/lib/feed/kinds.ts:19`), labeled "Secret Lair",
  "Commander decklists", "Wizards news" (`src/lib/copy/feed.ts:154-156`).
- **Home:** What's new (`src/components/home/whats-new.tsx`, rows `src/components/feed/feed-item.tsx`) shows only
  non-baseline posts from the last 14 days (`FEED_WINDOW_DAYS`, `src/lib/feed/kinds.ts:84`), five at a time with
  "Show all N" expanding in place (`src/components/feed/show-all.tsx:9`). A row is a cropped hero, kicker with age,
  title, two-line summary, and an "N of M lists" line for decklists; pressing it goes out to the post. Nothing links to
  older posts.
- **Switch:** `ANNOUNCEMENTS_ENABLED` gates what readers see. The existing handoff and brief
  (`docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-10-29-wizards-announcements.md:25-26`) never mention an archive.

**Rules for the build.**
- Every stored post, including baseline ones, by `published_at`.
- Filter by kind; dismissing a post on Home does not hide it from the archive (Design may rule otherwise and say why).
- Rides `ANNOUNCEMENTS_ENABLED`. No new switch, no model call, no credit, no push.

**States to draw.**
1. The entry point: from What's new on Home, and where else (Settings' news section, the Me page).
2. The archive list at both widths, newest first, with the kind filter.
3. Grouping or jumping by date (month headers, or a year/month jump) across about 608 posts.
4. A kind with no posts in the chosen range.
5. A Commander decklists post in the archive: whether its "N of M lists" line and early-list link carry over.
6. Loading more (paged or windowed), and a load failure.
7. A reader with a kind switched off in Settings: whether the archive still lists that kind.

**Open calls for Design.**
1. A route of its own or a sheet from Home.
2. Row: reuse `FeedItemRow` as drawn, or a denser archive row.
3. Whether the switched-off kinds show (recommendation: yes, the archive is a library, not a feed).

## 4. Custom sign-in on Clerk (Small items 7, item 5)

**The job.** Build the custom sign-in and sign-up drawn in `design/design_handoff_the99_small_items_7/` on Clerk,
replacing Clerk's hosted page (`/sign-in` today redirects there, `src/proxy.ts:43-48`).

**Why it is Orange.** `design/design_handoff_the99_small_items_7/READINESS.md:7` rates three drawn states as not
buildable on Clerk's own APIs and proposes an app-owned code and server-minted session (as `/join` does,
`src/lib/invites/claim.ts`):
- **S4:** a proven email with no account offers sign-up, with no second code (`README.md:93`).
- **U4:** an existing account's proven email goes straight in (`README.md:98`).
- **A sign-up with no invitation.** Today `/sign-up` takes only an invitation ticket (`src/app/sign-up/page.tsx:3`).

**What engineering found since (installed `@clerk/nextjs` 7.5.20).** Clerk's own flow covers the first two:
- **S4:** `signIn.create({ signUpIfMissing: true })` with the email code strategy. When the verified email has no user,
  the sign-in reports `isTransferable`, and `signUp.create({ transfer: true })` makes the account from the proven
  email with no second code.
- **U4:** the reverse. A sign-up whose email already has an account reports `isTransferable`, and
  `signIn.create({ transfer: true })` signs in.
- **No invitation:** not an API gap. It is a Clerk instance setting (restricted, invitation-only, or open), so it is
  Hugh's launch decision (READINESS `:8`), not something the custom pages can build around.
- If any path still needs a server-side sign-in, `@clerk/backend`'s `createSignInToken`, redeemed with the `ticket`
  strategy, mints a Clerk session with no app-owned session table.

These are read from Clerk's types, not yet run. The build's first step proves both transfers on the dev instance
before any screen is built; if either fails, this item returns to Design.

**What Design rules.**
1. **S4 and U4 stand as drawn** if the transfers hold. Confirm, or adjust what the reader sees between the code and the
   account (S4 still needs *Your first name*, U3).
2. **Sign-up under invitation-only.** Until Hugh opens sign-up, S4 cannot make an account for an uninvited email. Draw
   the state that replaces "Make an account with this email" when sign-up is closed, and the ticket path
   (READINESS `:9` recommends reusing U3).
3. **Other sign-in methods.** READINESS `:16`: methods beyond the email code are a Clerk dashboard setting the custom
   pages would silently drop. State which methods the pages carry (recommendation: email code only, the dashboard set
   to match).

**States to draw (new or changed only).**
1. S4 with sign-up closed: the uninvited email.
2. The invitation-ticket arrival (reuse of U3, or a new state).
3. Any state between a verified code and a transferred account that the transfer adds (for example a brief "Signing
   you in").
4. A transfer that fails (Clerk refuses or the code expired mid-transfer).
