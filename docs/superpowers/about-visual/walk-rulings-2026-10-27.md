- Drop details a player doesn't need (for example "your art picks stay").

### upload-choice: APPROVED (revised)
Caption: "When you upload a new export from ManaBox, Moxfield or any of the others, The 99 asks what you want to do with the update. "Replace my collection" makes your Collection match the file exactly. "Add these cards" adds the file's cards to what you already have. / Either way, you see every card that moves before anything changes. If you replace, it also tells you how many changes you've made in the app that will be overwritten, and calls out any decks it affects."
Keys: 1 "With Replace, any card that isn't in the file leaves your Collection." 2 "With Add, your quantities go up and nothing leaves." 3 "If you add a full export of cards you already uploaded, your copies double, and the screen warns you first." 4 "If your Collection is empty, the file goes straight in without asking."
Sync: the title is "Replacing, adding or syncing from a file". The caption adds ""Sync my collection" brings in what changed in the file and keeps the changes you made in the app." Key 3 becomes "With Sync, the file's changes come in, including cards it no longer has, and the changes you made in the app stay. If you both changed the same card, it asks you which to keep." Key 4 becomes "If your Collection is empty and there's no earlier file to sync against, the file goes straight in without asking."
