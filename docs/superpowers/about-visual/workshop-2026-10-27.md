# About, the visual redo: workshop rulings (Hugh, 2026-10-27)

Why: today's About (83 text entries) reads like technical documentation that no player will ever read. Hugh originally wanted something image-heavy that shows what the app does on each screen.

1. Six areas, each a visual one-pager: Home, Collection, Commanders, a deck (building and analysis), Shopping, Friends.
2. Pictures are real screenshots captured automatically by the contact-sheet tooling, at phone and desktop widths, with labels drawn on top. They're re-taken after UI changes.
3. The ~13 hidden rules sit on their area's one-pager, drawn visually as "good to know", not as a separate text list.
4. Launches: the release banner names the feature with a crop of its screenshot. Tapping it opens the area's one-pager with the new label highlighted and its Show me/Try card beside it. "Seen" means seen, as today.
5. The "?" panel on every screen opens that area's one-pager, scrolled to that screen. One source.
6. The launch rule: use discretion. "Everything gets an About entry" is what caused the bloat. A launch Hugh picks adds one label (and re-takes the screenshot). It adds a "good to know" item only for a rule the screen can't show. A cosmetic change only re-takes the screenshot.
- Keep: the Tools Show me / Try cards.
- Content source: docs/superpowers/about-visual/audit-2026-10-27.md (keep, merge and cut list; the hidden-rule facts), and the voice rules in docs/superpowers/about-visual/walk-rulings-2026-10-27.md.
- Paused until Design returns: the voice walk and the copy sweep, so nothing is rewritten twice. The 15 stale facts still get fixed in place if the redo takes long.
