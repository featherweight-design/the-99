2026-10-15. Handoff `design/social-r2` (Design's PR #1858). The engineering readiness audit is **Yellow**: nothing goes
back to Design, and the resolutions engineering takes are below. The scope is `boards/scope-social-2026-10-12.md` §2
(the wishlist, Hugh's rulings Q7 and Q9) and the spares half of the social foundation
(`boards/plan-social-foundation-2026-10-12.md`).

## Resolutions engineering takes

- **Desktop spare press.** Reserve its column only when `SOCIAL_FRIENDS_ENABLED` is on for the reader, because spares
  load late. The same applies to token rows.
- **Stand-ins modal.** A `Friends with a spare` section. The title reads `Before you buy`, `Friends with a spare` or
  `Cheaper stand-ins`, and waits for both reads. `Nothing cheaper` is hidden when spares exist. When swap tools are off
  and the modal is on the cart, a spares-only host draws it (a Replacing box with no deck line).
- **Collected leaves the list** (G5, Hugh approved it with round 3). A new table holds the one-time strip, with a
  migration and purge coverage. The delete happens in `applyCollectionChange`, `replaceCollection` and
  `setWishlistQuantity`, and reads filter out collected lines. `AGENTS.md` and the `wishlist.ts` comments are updated in
  the same PR.
- **Reads.** `loadFriendSpares` also returns pod names and the friend's face. Spares are served by an uncached,
  signed-in `GET /api/friend-spares`, so no `friendGeneration` goes in any key.
- **Frames drawn at one width only** (D3, S4, S5, O2, O3; OP1's stale backdrop) are built from the other width's rules.
  Errors use `ToastFrame`. Tap targets are 40 and 44px. Focus goes to the stepper, the count gets a live region, the
  strip is `role=status`, and the combined press gets an aria label. Hex values map to tokens (`#1B1D24` is
  `--elevated`). Sort offers Newest and Name.
- **Copy** goes through `/copy-review`: the stale strings file, plus the strings the frames draw that it lacks (listed
  in the audit).
- **About entries 2 and 3 (Wishlist, a friend has a spare) belong to R2's last PR**, because an entry must never describe a
  surface that has not shipped. R3 PR 5 carries entries 1 (friends and pods) and 4 (opponents) only. This is a deferral of
  WHEN, and the line's close-out does not pass without them.
