# Rule 0 table check (build plan)

Brief: `docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-10-16-rule-zero-table-check.md` (#1934). Handoff:
`design/design_handoff_the99_rule_zero/` (#1939, R2 approved by Hugh). Readiness:
`docs/superpowers/readiness/rule-zero-r1.md` (🟡 Yellow, fourteen gaps, every one resolved by engineering; its resolutions
bind this plan and are not restated in full here). Behind `RULE_ZERO_ENABLED`, which needs `SOCIAL_FRIENDS_ENABLED`.

**What it builds.** A pod member seats tonight's players from one pod, gives each seat a deck, and every seated player opens
the same page: the read (each seat's shipped bracket chip and its five finding counts, the table's middle, any deck a
bracket or more away from it flagged with what puts it there), a suggestion of closer decks for a flagged seat's own player
with one tap to swap, and `Log this game`, which opens the player's seated deck with the other seats filled in. A table
lasts 24 hours. No model call anywhere.

## Rulings this plan builds on

Hugh's (brief, Locked 1 to 13, and the orchestrator's relay of 2026-10-16): the read is brackets plus their drivers; seats
are pod members only, with no guest seat; one player sets the table and every seat sees it; the table fills the game log;
it expires after 24 hours; it suggests the player's own closer decks; seated players see each other's chosen deck, and
nothing else of theirs, for the table's 24 hours.

**Open with Hugh: one open table per pod, or several (G7).** Built as ONE unless he says otherwise. The rule lives in one
place (`openTableForPod` under the pod row lock, PR 2), so changing it later touches the setup write and the pod row's
entry, not the read.

**Decided here, beyond the readiness doc:**

1. **Home's table card is read through a route, not inside Home's page load** (readiness gap 1 said "an uncached read
   beside the cached payload"). `check:list-cache` computes Home's tables by following `src/app/page.tsx`'s imports, so any
   loader Home imports that reads `rule_zero_seats` fails it, cached or not, and the seats table carries no stamp trigger
   (a seat written by a setter who is not your friend, and a table's expiry, move nothing a key could carry). So the card is
   a client component on Home that fetches `GET /api/rule-zero/open` after hydration. It is a secondary card at the foot of
   the first screen, it is never stale on expiry or a re-seat, and the guard stays green with no exemption.
2. **A seat's deck is `on delete set null`.** A deck deleted while seated leaves the seat waiting (`picks their own deck`),
   the state T10 draws, rather than removing a player from the table.
3. **The grant also joins pod membership.** The seat read requires the viewer's seat at an unexpired table AND the viewer's
   current membership of that table's pod; seats whose player has left the pod are omitted. A player removed from the pod
   loses the table on their next poll (it reads as not found), and nobody keeps sight of a former member's deck.
4. **Expired rows are not swept by a job.** Every read filters `expires_at > now()`. A pod's expired tables are deleted by the
   next setup on that pod, inside its transaction, and every table goes with the account purge. A job would be scaffolding
   for a table that holds a handful of rows per pod.
5. **No deck id of another player reaches a client.** A seat is addressed by its own seat id. The only deck ids in the
   payload are the viewer's own (Change deck, the suggestion). The game log's prefill is built on the server (PR 6).

## Migration

One migration (next free number at the time PR 2 is cut; 0141 as of this plan; regenerate and re-stamp the ledger on a
collision). Two tables, no `user_data_touch` trigger on either (decision 1):

- `rule_zero_tables`: `id uuid pk`, `pod_id → pods.id on delete cascade`, `set_by text not null` (the setter's user id),
  `created_at`, `expires_at not null` (created + 24 hours), index on `(pod_id, expires_at)`.
- `rule_zero_seats`: `table_id → rule_zero_tables.id on delete cascade`, `user_id text not null`, `seat smallint` (seating
  order, 1 to 8, check), `deck_id → decks.id on delete set null`, `changed_at not null`, `seen_at` (the seated player's own
  last draw of the table, null until first), primary key `(table_id, user_id)`, unique `(table_id, seat)`, index on
  `user_id`.

Purge (`purge.test.ts` coverage fails until both are handled): the account's seats are deleted, and every table it set is
deleted (its seats cascade). Both run in `purgeSocialIn`, before the pod hand-on, since a table belongs to the pod's night,
not to the pod's next owner.

### PR 1. The rules (pure, no database, no caller)

`src/lib/rule-zero/rules.ts` and its test, plus the switch (registry entry, `ruleZeroEnabled`, `flags.test.ts` holds
`needs` and the analysis requirement).

- `seatState(seat)` from one snapshot (readiness gap 9): `{ kind: "illegal", cards }` when `deckLegality` says not legal now,
  even with a stored reading (whole-deck count `built + (100 - size)`, so `87 of 100`, `101 cards, over 100`); `fresh`;
  `stale` (chip kept, `from before its last change`); `unread` (legal, no reading: read on the spot); `waiting` (no deck).
- `measuredBracket(permits, declared)`: a single bracket is itself; a range answered by a declaration inside it is the
  declaration (`declarationAsk` = `answered`); an undeclared or drifted range is measured from its end nearer the middle.
  A drifted declaration is never compared.
- `tableRead(seats)`: the middle is the median of the measured brackets of seats in the read (fresh or stale); a seat is
  flagged `high` or `low` when its distance from the middle is 1 or more; exactly two read seats with different brackets
  are both `split` and the rail draws no band; fewer than two read seats is `notEnough`. Fixtures pinned in the test, each
  a brief or handoff state: `2,3,3,4` flags both ends, `2,2,4` flags the 4, `3,3,3` balanced, `[1,2]` undeclared beside
  `2,2` (measured from 2), `[4,5]` declared 4, a drifted `[4,5]` declared 1 (measured from 4, never 1), a two-seat `2,4`
  split, one read seat beside an illegal one (`notEnough`).
- `driversFor(seat, flag)` (readiness gap 10): `ruledOut` reasons speak `whyLimit` against the bracket below; `assembled`
  speaks `whyCombo` from `lean.decidedBy` and its tutor receipts, with the bracket name taken from the sub-line (not the
  hard-coded `Upgraded`); a `[4,5]` range takes its reasons from `slotDeck`'s bracket-3 violations; a low seat speaks
  `whyLow`, which always says counts can miss cards.
- `suggestionsFor(seat, ownDecks, middle)` (G6): own decks, legal now, with a reading, strictly nearer the middle; nearest
  first, then most recently played; at most three; none is an empty list (the block is absent).
- `startingDeck(decks)` (G8): most recently played, else most recently edited.

### PR 2. The data: tables, the seat grant, writes (dark, no caller but the probe)

The migration and schema; `src/lib/social/rule-zero.ts` (every cross-user read stays in `src/lib/social/`, so
`check:social-reads` holds); the purge; `pnpm probe:rule-zero` on a scratch branch.

- `loadTable(viewer, tableId)`: one statement joins the viewer's seat at the table, `expires_at > now()`, and the viewer's
  membership of its pod; null otherwise (the page's not found, or expired: `tableStatus` tells the two apart for the
  viewer's own seat only). Per seat it returns exactly readiness gap 2's fields (display name, `public_id` only when the two
  are friends, deck name, commander art, permits, declaration and `declarationAsk`, five finding counts, the sub-line,
  `lean` with the combo's names and tutor count, the bracket-3 reasons for a range, card count and legality, fresh or
  stale, `changed_at`) and nothing that links to a deck page or a profile. Readings are read under each seat's owner id
  after the grant is checked, in one repeatable-read snapshot.
- `readOnTheSpot(seat)` (gap 8): a legal deck with no reading is computed with `computeAndStoreProfile` under the owner's id
  and `resolveSwitchEnv(owner)`, stored as theirs. Called from the poll route (PR 3), never in the page's first render.
- `setTable(viewer, podId, seats)`: under the pod row's `FOR UPDATE`, deletes the pod's expired tables, returns the open
  table when one exists (one per pod, G7), else writes the table and 2 to 8 seats. Refuses a seat who is not a pod member, a
  viewer not in the pod, and a deck the viewer picks for a seat that is not the viewer's own or (through `friendDecksIn`)
  their friend's. A non-friend's seat is written with no deck (G3).
- `reseatTable(setter, tableId, seats)`: the setter's `Seats`, same checks, keeps unchanged seats' decks and `changed_at`.
- `setSeatDeck(viewer, tableId, deckId | null)`: the viewer's own seat only, the viewer's own deck only; moves `changed_at`.
  `Undo` is this call with the previous deck.
- `markSeen(viewer, tableId)`: writes the viewer's own `seen_at`.
- `openTablesFor(viewer)`: the viewer's seats at unexpired tables of pods they are still in (Home's card, the pod row).
- `podSetupMembers(viewer, podId)`: members with `hasDecks` for the viewer's friends only (`friendDeckCountsQuery`, G2); a
  non-friend member reads as `picksOwn`.

The probe proves, with fixtures it owns: the grant (a seated non-friend sees the chosen deck and nothing else; an unseated
member, an expired table and a member who left the pod read null), one open table per pod under two concurrent setups, the
writes' refusals, `on delete set null`, and the purge removing both tables' rows.

## Review focus

The inputs no single task's tests reach most naturally, each pinned in the PR that owns it:

1. **A table expiring while open** (PR 3): the next poll answers the expired body and the page draws L4 in place; a press
   on any control after expiry is refused with the shared toast, never a thrown error.
2. **Two members setting a table on one pod at once** (PR 2): the second gets the first's table; the probe races them.
3. **A seated deck deleted, or made illegal, mid-night** (PR 2, PR 3): the seat waits, or reads its card count, and the read
   recomputes without it.
4. **A non-friend's deck reaching a client by another path** (PR 2, PR 6): the payload and the prefill carry no deck id or
   public id of a non-friend; asserted on the serialized payload in the tests.
5. **A viewer removed from the pod while seated** (PR 2): the grant drops at the next poll; the probe removes one mid-test.

## Not in this build

A guest seat; the invite flow itself; any change to how a bracket is computed; a model call; a push channel; a table past
24 hours or a history of tables; several open tables per pod (unless Hugh rules for it).
