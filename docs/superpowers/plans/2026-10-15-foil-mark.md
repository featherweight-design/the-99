# Foil mark addendum (M1, R4): build plan

Handoff: `design/design_handoff_the99_foil_treatment/addendum-foil-mark/` (README, `STATES-foil-mark.md`, the `M-*` boards,
`foil-mark · copy strings.ts`), merged as #1875. Hugh's briefs and rulings, 2026-10-15, are in the README (R2 to R4). The
foil rule is closed: a card is foil when every owned copy of the printing it draws is foil, etched as its own finish
(`soleFoilFinish`); precons read `card.printedFoil`.

**The two-line card row ships to everyone, with no switch** (Hugh, 2026-10-15, on #1875): All from `md`, Shopping from
`lg`, the glint under the dot, Shopping without the EDHREC figure, and the rebuilt Shopping key. His earlier ruling stands:
Social R2's phone option B (below `md`) stays behind `PHONE_SHOPPING_ROW_B_ENABLED`. The glint itself is cosmetic and
gated by data (only a foil copy), so it has no switch either. **About: none**, per the foil handoff's own ruling.

## What this build is held to

- **Every name keeps its x.** The glint sits under the ownership dot at the head of line 2 on the two-line rows, so no mark
  squeezes or moves a name (R4). A test pins the name's left edge with and without a glint.
- **Phone with option B off is byte-identical** below `md` to what ships today: the two-line form reaches `md` and up in
  All and `lg` and up in Shopping, and a probe photographs 390 on and off.
- **Copy**: the strings (`foilMarkCopy`, the key entries, `press to add` for the buy item) go through `/copy-review`.

## PR boundaries

Each PR under about 20 files and 800 lines, no stacking, every UI PR with a contact sheet in
`docs/superpowers/inventories/foil-mark.json`.

1. **The glint and the marks outside the decklist row.** `FoilMark` (`cards/foil-mark.tsx`: the path, the gradient, a
   `useId` per instance, forced colours `CanvasText`, `finish` and `silent` as `CardFactMark`), `foilMarkCopy`, the
   tooltip through `FactTooltip`, the spoken clause on the name, the `foil` entry in `CollectionMarkKey`; the Collection
   grid tile (first on the meta line), the Collection list row (count column), the deck Card-view tile and the precon
   tile (after the dot). Parallel with 2 only through `FoilMark`, which 2 imports, so **2 follows 1** unless 2 carries the
   component in its own first commit.
2. **The two-line card row, for everyone.** `decklist-row.tsx`: All two lines from `md`, Shopping two lines from `lg`
   (Social R2 option B's form, with the fact marks on line 1), the glint under the dot at the head of line 2, Shopping
   without the figure, the other-ways press and the buy tag on line 2, and the precon `readOnly` rows showing the glint
   in place of `· foil`. `shopping-key.tsx`: one grid holding `cheaper stand-ins`, the spare item, `press to add`
   (`over $N`, then the deck's marks). Token rows (`token-row.tsx`, `tokens-shopping-group.tsx`) take the same two-line
   form by the card row's rules, so they do not sit one line high among two-line rows (d9's call, reuse, no Design round).
3. **The deck's keys.** `decklist.tsx`, the Shopping view and the precon view's key: the `foil` entry when a card in view
   has that finish, and the keys in the shipped phone layouts (`/collection` three columns, the deck key wrapping).
