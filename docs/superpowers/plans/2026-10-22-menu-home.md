## The feed model

- `user_prefs.feed_kinds` (jsonb, `{kind: enabledAt}`) and `user_prefs.feed_seen_at`. A kind that is off
  is absent from the map. Unseen means created after `feed_seen_at`; a Home render sets it through
  `after()`, never during the render, and ONLY when there is an unseen item. `user_prefs` carries the
  `user_data_touch` trigger, so an unconditional write would bump the stamp and miss Home's and `/decks`'
  cache on every visit (the `markUpcomingSeen` rule).
- A second cursor, `user_prefs.friends_seen_at`, belongs to the Friends badge alone: unseen trade matches are
  those created after it, and only a `/friends` visit advances it (same only-when-unseen write). Home never
  moves it and Friends never moves `feed_seen_at`, so neither surface clears the other's badge. Both
  columns land in PR 4's migration.
- **Catalog kinds are read, not written.** New set and New precon come from `catalog_first_seen` at read
  time, newer than the kind's `enabledAt`, never a baseline row. No per-user fan-out under the
  `catalog-ingest` lock, and "a kind that is off is not written, no backfill" holds without a write.
- **Per-user kinds persist in `feed_items`** (`user_id`, `kind`, subject ids, `created_at`):
  - Price drop: written by the price job after its transaction commits, outside the lock, non-fatal.
    `wishlist_items.price_at_add_cents` (backfilled with today's cheapest) and `price_noticed_at` (the
    once-a-week rule). Rule (G2, placeholder): the cheapest printing falls 20% or $2 below the price at
    wishlist time.
  - Combo: written after a collection write commits, for two-card variants the write completed.
  - Trade match: detected at read time in `src/lib/social/` (the viewer's wishlist against friends'
    spares), persisted on first notice for seen state, and re-joined against the grant on every read.
- Home reads the feed per request, outside `cachePageForUser`; the reader is registered in `PER_REQUEST`
  (`check:list-cache`). `feed_items` is covered by the purge, including rows that name another account.

## Product calls (decided 2026-10-22 by the orchestrator; Hugh may override)

1. A friend's Decks tab shows status as drawn ("To buy · 8 cards"), although the figure derives from the
   friend's collection: spares already show more of it than a count does.
2. The Friends badge counts unseen trade matches, cleared by a `/friends` visit, until G5 adds answers.
3. With `HOME_FEED_ENABLED` on, the arrivals banner keeps About's line only. Upcoming precons stay on
   `/decks/precons`; the feed's New precon is a released one.
4. One About entry for the line, at PR 8. The line stays off in production until its artboard exists.
5. **Hugh's ruling, 2026-10-27 (no Design round):** from desktop a What's new row (`FeedItemRow`) and a Waiting on
   you trade-match row (`TradeRow`) put the time and the press on one line at the right, the time first and on the
   press's baseline, reading as the phone does. This departs from the boards (`Feed-Kinds`, `Home-States`,
   `Friends` draw the time top-right of the kicker line and the press centred in its own column) and supersedes them
   for those two rows. A row with no press keeps its time alone in that column. On a phone nothing moves. Waiting on
   you's spare-request row (Yes, No and the days left) is not covered and stays as drawn.
6. **Hugh's ruling, 2026-10-27 (no Design round, no About entry):** every What's new row on Home, its lead story and
   every Waiting on you row on /friends carry Your requests' bare ✕ (spare requests, Asker-States `.fi .xx`: 36px at the
   row's top right), at both widths, with a coarse pointer's 44px hit inside the row. A ✕ hides the item for good
   (`feed_dismissals`, keyed on Home's own key; an ask under `ask:<request id>`, and its feed item when it has one) and answers nothing: a
   hidden ask stays open for the asker. The row's whole text column reserves 30px for its coarse hit (the lead 28px) and the desktop right column 34px, as the board's
   `.ya .fi` does. Proof: `pnpm probe:feed-dismiss`.
7. **Hugh's ruling, 2026-10-27 (no Design round):** a price drop's press opens the card on Mana Pool in a new tab, not
   its card details, in What's new's rows and the lead story alike. It is card details' own link out: the URL from
   `manapoolCardUrl` (every printing, keyed by the card's name; Mana Pool links carry no tracking or affiliate
   parameter anywhere in the app), `target="_blank"`, the ↗ and the spoken new-tab suffix. Its label is its own,
   `feedCopy.home.press.priceDrop`, "Buy at Mana Pool" (Hugh's follow-up the same day: #2286 shipped card details'
   six-word "Buy any printing at Mana Pool", over the 3-word press cap; card details keeps that one). This supersedes the boards' "Open the card ›"
   (`Feed-Kinds`, `Home-States`). A price drop whose card the catalog no longer holds draws no press. The push is
   unchanged: it carries its own target (`openCard`), never the row's, and its tap still lands on Home with the card's
   details open.
