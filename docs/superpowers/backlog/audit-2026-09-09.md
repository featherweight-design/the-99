## Rulings

All four calls were taken by Hugh on 2026-09-09 and recorded on their tickets.

1. **#334 — the commander's claim is always hard.** A deck is graded `mature` only at a full body
   (`availability-query.ts:167`) and every claim an immature deck makes is counted soft, so a
   commander leading a half-built brew fell into the free pool. The commander and partner are
   carved out of the maturity rule: leading a deck is a commitment from the moment it is set. An
   immature deck's ordinary cards stay soft. The ticket is now a scoped fix to the grading `CASE`,
   with tests both ways.
2. **#44 — retired.** Closed as `MOOT` in favour of `docs/superpowers/design-pipeline-status.md`.
   Its two live items exist as their own tickets, and two roadmap indexes guarantee one is wrong.
3. **#10 — stays parked, deliberately.** Not blocked and not a design ticket; held on timing. Noted
   on the ticket that the 18-task plan was written against `main` at 2026-09-07, so re-verifying it
   is the first step whenever the work does start.
4. **#161 — closed as `MOOT`.** Never reproduced, and the symptom it describes is specified
   behaviour under availability plus seniority. A genuine mismatch would arrive with a repro and
   earn a fresh ticket.
