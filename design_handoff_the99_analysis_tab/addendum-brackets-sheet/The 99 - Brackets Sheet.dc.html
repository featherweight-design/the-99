All brackets, the sheet behind “see all brackets →”

R3 makes the sheet a reference, not a second climb. All five brackets are columns, nothing marks the reader's own, and each rule's reading and Wizards quote move to a list under the table. Every figure in the grid is a constant in rules.ts.

3a

Opened, at 1300 and 390

Five columns, no marking

The climb already shows where a deck sits, so the sheet only states the rules. Optimized and cEDH are columns like the rest. Their cells read no cap, the same string the climb prints at 4–5. With no marking, there is no tint, no ink rule and no where your cards are label, and so no contrast case left on the sheet. The yours prop and verdict.permits are no longer needed here.

Figures only in the cells

The corner says cards allowed, so each cell is a number (0, 3) or no cap, never 0 allowed. That fits five columns at 700 (about 100px each) and the phone's four. 3 is the one figure in --fg at 600. no cap is --fg-faint on a dashed underline. The header drops its aside and shows the title and close only.

390 transposes

Five bracket columns at 390 would be about 53px, which is narrower than “Exhibition”. So the phone turns the grid: brackets are rows (mark 22 + name), and the four rules are columns (fact mark 16 + a mono 8.5 label that wraps), about 60px each. The figures are the same and so is the notes list. At both widths a reader looks up the bracket and then the rule.

3b

Unrolled: the notes list and where the quotes sit

One entry per rule, under the table

Six entries: the four rules, then tutors with the findings' no limit chip (8.5 mono, --fg-faint, fully rounded, 2/6 padding, per audit finding 4), then Optimized and cEDH under both chip marks. Each entry has a mark column (48, or 40 at 390), then the name, our reading if there is one, and the quote. Entries are split by 1px --border rules. Tutors and the 4–5 note left the table, because both were merged cells saying one thing across every column.

The quote's type

The quote sits 5px under the reading. Plex Sans italic 400, 12 / 1.5 (11.5 at 390), --fg-body, in curly quotes. The reading stays upright 12 / 1.55 --fg-muted. A mono tag follows on the same line, Wizards · Feb 2025, 9.5 (9) --fg-faint, with an 8px gap and no wrap. Mass land denial has no reading, so its quote stands alone.

Sources

Five quotes are verbatim from Introducing Commander Brackets Beta (11 Feb 2025), and tutors is from the 21 Oct 2025 update. The foot keeps the dated rules source and adds Quoted from with both links. The tags are text, so the only controls in the body are those three links.
