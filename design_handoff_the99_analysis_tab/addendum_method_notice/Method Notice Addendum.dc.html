We changed how overviews are written, and the overview says so, with one free rewrite

This amends the Analysis tab and the overview-loading addendum, and implements plan §7.4 with Hugh’s override: this is its own notice, not the stale state. The press rewrites the Overview only. It’s free once per deck per method version, with at most 2 attempts, and runs only when the owner presses it. There’s no new tag and no Notices-sheet entry.

Where it sits: in the Overview, not over the tab

The notice sits under the Overview label, above the held paragraph. It isn’t at the top of the tab, where the stale banner speaks for the whole reading. Placing it here says what the press touches: this text, not the bracket. The bracket box keeps its own date (analyzed 21 Sept) all the way through.

Told apart from the stale banner at a glance

The stale banner is the reader’s change. It’s solid (--surface-rail, --border-strong) and has the tab’s filled button. The notice is ours. It’s dashed (--border-dashed on --surface-sunken), leads with the app’s i mark (the stats InfoTip’s 18px ring, static here) and has the Overview’s own outlined button, the same one as Write one and Try again. The line starts with We’ve changed, never Your deck.

Not dismissible

It’s a caption on the text it sits above, like ruling D’s written before your latest changes, so it’s not a promotion to close. It stays true until the overview is rewritten, by this press or by any later write, and then it goes. It’s quiet and stays inside its section, so leaving it costs little. A dismiss control would also throw away the free run without saying so.

Scope, stated once

Only the overview is rewritten; your bracket and stats stay as they are. That answers the question the press would raise. Nothing is automatic, and an unpressed deck costs nothing. The held overview stays readable underneath, along with View full overview →.
