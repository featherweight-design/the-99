Title
Reviewing what a commit will cost
Route

Chapter
03 — Building the 99
Width
plate
Caption
A draft becomes a version here, and this page is the only place that says what that will cost. Not what the deck holds — the deck page already shows that list — but what committing it asks of the rest of your collection. Cards coming in are counted into four buckets, and a card another deck has already sleeved carries Pull it: take that copy, and the deck holding it is cut a version. It is the one act on this page nobody can take back. Cards going out carry Put back, and the commit sheet below the columns is where the version is actually cut.

Title
Starting a deck
Route
/decks/new · opens the commander picker
Chapter
03 — Building the 99
Width
plate
Caption
Every deck starts with a commander you already own. The picker lists them all with the size of the pool each one opens up — how many of your cards are legal in its colours — and a name is the only other thing a deck needs. Commanders that partner get a second slot, filtered to the ones they can legally pair with. Arriving from a commander’s own page skips the list: the commander is already chosen, any synergy picks you made there travel with you, and the page asks for the name.

why the picker, not the pre-filled door
CreatePreFilled is the same act with the list already answered — same createDeck, same field, same button. The picker is the door reached by intent; the pre-filled one is reached by having already chosen, from a surface chapter 02 documents. An entry drawn on the shortcut would teach the shortcut and leave the front door undocumented. Drawn on the picker, the shortcut is one caption clause — and that clause carries the only thing it uniquely adds: synergy picks made on the commander page travel with you.
