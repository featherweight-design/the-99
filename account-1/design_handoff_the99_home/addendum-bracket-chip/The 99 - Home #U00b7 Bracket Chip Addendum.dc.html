A row with a chip splits line two in two: the plates on one line, the commander and the count on the next.

Marks only, as Hugh ruled on 2026-09-23 (R1). The chip still sits immediately after the status pill, 8px from it. But once the · N cards clause is drawn, no single line fits at any desktop or phone width (§A). So when deck.bracket is set, the pill and chip take line two and the commander and count drop to line three. That is one boolean with no measurement. The row stays 72px. A row with no chip is the shipped row, unchanged. Nothing new: no mark, token or string.

C · variant · the count on its own line, with the progress bar · every row

Every row, with a chip or without, has the same three lines. Line one is the name and stamp. Line two is the pill, the chip and the commander, and the commander is the only thing on it that shrinks. Line three is the tile's 3px progress bar (statusBarStyle, filled in the status's ink, from a zero basis) followed by the whole count, 99 / 99 · 12 cards, which never shrinks. The row stays 72px, with py-[4px] and 4px between lines. That's 17.4 + 4 + 22 / 24 + 4 + 12.6 = 60 / 62 in a 62px content box, which is the most the 72px row allows. Every row is the same shape, so there's no offset in a mixed pair. Worst-case commander: 55px at 360, 85 at 390, 130 at 1300 (Unresolved + 4–5). With no chip it's 142 / 172 / 217.
