/privacy and /terms · one prose page

Signed in in Neon Rain over the shell; signed out in Low Sun. Section names are B1's list in brackets, placeholders for /prose-pass. The pages carry the notice the same way every page does.

The container (one component, LegalPage, for both pages, both states)
  • Measure. The article is held to 680px on desktop (about 75 characters at 15px) and runs the 356px phone measure. Body 15px / 1.65, --fg-body; section headings 18px Plex Sans 600, --fg. Prose is /prose-pass's; the grey bars are line length, not words.
  • Top. Last updated as a mono line in --fg-muted (not --fg-faint, for the same AA reason as the notice), then the title in the theme's display face, 28px phone and 36px desktop, then the opening paragraph.
  • Jumping to a section. Phone: an On this page list under the opening paragraph, one 44px row per section, numbered. Desktop: the same list as a 220px column to the right of the article, position: sticky (never fixed), the section in view marked with the brand diamond in accent. Sections take scroll-margin-top so a jump on the phone clears the header when it returns.
  • Way back. Signed in, the shell is the way back: rail or tab bar, no tab lit, nothing added. Signed out, a 64px header (68 desktop) carries the wordmark, which goes to /, and a quiet Sign in (the shipped secondary button), since a legal page isn't where the primary press belongs.
  • The notice follows the article like every other page. /terms also states the Fan Content passage in its own words in a section (B1's prose); the footer is not repeated inside the article.
