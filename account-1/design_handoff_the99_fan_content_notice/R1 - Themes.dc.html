The link · four states

Quiet by default: the words keep the notice's own ink and size, and an underline (1px, --fg-faint as the line's color, 2px offset) plus a small ↗ say it's a link that leaves. Hover and active lift it to --fg. Focus is the shipped link-out ring, 2px --accent at 2px offset. Visited looks like rest. The underline's color carries no meaning on its own; the text's contrast is the text's.
