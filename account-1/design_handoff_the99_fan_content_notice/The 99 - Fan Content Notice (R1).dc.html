The Fan Content notice, and the /privacy and /terms page

Source of truth: docs/superpowers/design-briefs/2026-10-17-fan-content-notice.md on main (#2030). Every Locked item is Hugh's (L1, L3, L4) and is honoured as written; the Decided link is drawn. This board makes the five Open calls. The notice's words are set, never reworded: they are the brief's, verbatim, on every frame.

The five calls

1 Placement: the foot of the viewport on a short page, the end of the page on a long one. Never position: fixed. The notice is the last child of the page column and the page above it takes flex: 1, so a short page pushes it down to the bottom edge and a long one carries it after its content.
  • Not the root mount. The shell's column is already a viewport tall plus the tab-bar reserve, so a footer after <Shell> makes every short page scroll for the notice alone, and at the end of that scroll the column's 74px reserve sits empty above it (Signed in, R1 and R2).
  • Two mounts of one component, both in shell.tsx: after ShellMain inside the signed-in column, and after children in the bare branch. Every signed-out route, /offline included, renders through the bare branch, so there is no per-page mount.
  • It clears the tab bar and sheets with the column's own reserve. Inside the wrapper, --tabbar-h already resolves to 88px while locked, so the locked bar costs nothing extra (S3).
  • Desktop: in the column beside the rail, never under it. The hairline spans the column; the text starts at the 34px gutter and is held to 720px.
2 Type: IBM Plex Sans 11px / 16px on the phone, 12px / 18px from md; weight 400, no tracking, in --fg-muted. 6.61:1 at its lowest (Witching Hour), 7.35:1 at its highest (Canopy). The two passages are two paragraphs 6px apart. Measured in Plex Sans at 390 (356px inside the gutters): 3 + 3 = 6 lines, 4 + 4 on a 360 phone; desktop 2 + 2 at a 720px measure. 12px on the phone was measured too: 4 + 4 = 8 lines, the wall the brief warned of. Running the passages together saves no line at 390 and blurs two separate statements. --fg-faint is out: 4.12:1 Neon Rain, 4.29:1 Oil Haze. Hairline --border, 20px above the text, 24px below.
3 The link keeps the notice's ink and adds an underline and a small ↗. Underline 1px in --fg-faint at 2px offset; hover and active --fg; focus the shipped link-out ring (2px --accent, 2px offset); visited as rest. --accent as the ink was rejected: louder than a legal line should be, and 4.51:1 in Oil Haze. The ↗ is aria-hidden; the new tab is said in the link's accessible name, the app's existing newTab pattern (a string for /copy-review, G6).
4 Signed out: one treatment for all five, centered on the page's axis. Every signed-out page centers its own text, so the notice does too; same size, same token, same hairline. Each page swaps min-h-screen for flex-1 (G2) and keeps its own composition: /, /friend and /offline center in the space above the notice; /sign-up and /join stay top-aligned on the phone. No per-shell margin tuning.
5 /privacy and /terms: one prose page, LegalPage. A 680px article (356px phone measure), a mono Last updated line, the title in the theme's display face, an On this page list (rows on the phone, a sticky 220px column on desktop), 18px section headings. Signed in, inside the shell with no tab lit. Signed out, a slim header with the wordmark to / and a quiet Sign in. The notice below, as everywhere (board 4).
