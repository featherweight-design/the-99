Find a friend's spares · R1

Two gaps, one goal: finding a card a friend can spare. A friend's Spares tab gains the Collection's own filter frame (search by name or type, Wanted, Color identity, Type, Sort), and a Wishlist tile gains the deck row's spare press, which opens the same Friends with a spare modal and its Ask. Wishlist stays separate from Shopping. Drawn over the shipped friend page and Wishlist tab, rendered from main; new parts are built from shipped components. Friends are Colin and Max; card art is from other handoffs, counts and prices are placeholders.

1 · Spares tab: search and filters

  • The Collection's frame, as shipped: the wide chassis from 1000px, the compact head below it (search, then Filters · Sort · count) with its Filters sheet and chip shelf.
  • Search reads name or type, the same matcher Collection is gaining: every word must start a word of the name or the type line, so wizard finds Gisa, Tam and Zimone, and saga or zombie work the same way.
  • Filters that matter on a friend's spares: Wanted (On your wishlist, A deck needs it), Color identity (the pips, Any or Exactly), Type (counts respect the other filters). Left out: Set, Availability, price. They describe your own copies, not a friend's.
  • Sort: Wanted first (the shipped order, now with deck needs after wishlist cards), Name, Most spares.
  • The tile's caption gains a deck needs it / 2 decks need it under the shipped on your wishlist, so the Wanted filter has something you can see.
  • No results: the shared ZeroState, No spares match “saga”. with Keep only search and Clear all filters. Empty and not sharing: the shipped tab, no frame.

2 · Wishlist: the friend-spare press

  • The deck row's press, on the tile: the outlined 26px box with the spare glyph in fact ink, 44px hit on touch, sitting over the caption's lower right. It is the tile's sibling, never inside its press.
  • One friend: the glyph. Two or more: the glyph and how many friends (2). None: nothing at all, no slot.
  • Requested and accepted marks are the deck row's (accent dot, green tick). The meta row carries the shipped key words, only once a press is drawn.
  • Pressing it opens the shipped spares-only modal: Friends with a spare, kicker on your wishlist, a row per friend with Ask. The ask names no deck and asks for what the line still wants.
  • Loading: the page draws with no presses. One read for every tile lands after it; the presses fade in (160ms, none under reduced motion). A failed read draws nothing, never an error.

3 · A Wishlist filter or sort?

  • No filter. A wishlist is short and the press already says which cards a friend has; hiding the rest would hide what you still need to buy.
  • One sort order: the shipped desktop Sort gains Friends’ spares: cards with a press first, most friends first, then newest. Before the read lands it sorts as Newest, and it re-sorts once when the read lands, only if chosen. The phone has no Sort today and gets none.
