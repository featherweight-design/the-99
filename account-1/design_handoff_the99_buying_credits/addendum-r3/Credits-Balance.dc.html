4support@the-99.dev, a link in both sentences
Design's callConfirmed as one mailto link in the Refunds line and in R4’s slow-return sentence (SupportLink). One change: measured, --accent on the band’s --surface is 4.25:1 in Oil Haze and 4.46:1 in First Frost, under 4.5. I draw the address in --fg with its underline in --accent: 10.60:1 at the lowest, in both sentences, every theme.

The underline is what marks it as a link (it differs from the sentence around it in more than colour), and the accent rule keeps it reading as the app’s link. Same treatment in both places, so SupportLink stays one component. The Refunds line on --bg clears with --accent too (4.51:1 lowest), but one rule for one component is simpler than two. If the text inks addendum (account 1, 2026-10-17) is approved first, its --accent-text clears 4.5:1 on every ground, and the link simply moves to --accent-text with the app’s other text links; this --fg treatment is only needed without it.
