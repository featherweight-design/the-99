10Help me choose at 390, out of credits: Get more credits moves from the dock to the box
Design's callWith L4’s Get more credits → the phone’s dock measures 138px here (Hugh’s reading, about 145) and covers whichever field passes under it, Sets in the report. My call: on the phone, the dock keeps the press and its line, and Get more credits → moves under the Anything else? box, after Kept here for when a credit comes back. That is where the credit is missing: the only words a credit buys here are in that box. The dock drops to 92px. No new string; desktop is unchanged (its line sits beside the press, nothing docks).

Held: Get more credits stays its own line directly under a line that says nothing can be spent (the box’s caption), as README’s placement call says, and shows only while payments are on, as today. If Hugh would rather keep it in the dock, the alternative is the link at 32px there (dock 126px), which still covers most of Sets.
