/collection’s NoResults and /commanders’ ZeroState say the same thing in two drawings: a filled crown on an accent vignette with a filled Clear filters, against three hollow diamonds, no ground and a hairline Clear all filters. Call: one component, the commanders geometry wins. It is already the app’s common shape (/decks and the set page use the same centred Cinzel heading and the same 44 → 32 hairline buttons), it sits quietly under a filter chassis that is still on screen, and its first press names the one fix when there is one. What each surface keeps is its own wording: collection names the query and colors it filtered by, commanders names the card a filter hides.

What wins, and what stays

  • Geometry: ZeroState (three hollow 10px diamonds, Cinzel 600 19, body --fg-muted max 520, gap 12, buttons 48 full width on a phone, 32 from 640).
  • Words: each surface’s own. Collection’s heading keeps its query and color (noResults.heading) and its caption.
  • Collection’s crown and vignette retire here; the crown stays where it means “empty collection”.

Collection’s presses

  • With a query and filters: Keep only search (primary, the one fix) then Clear all filters (hairline).
  • Filters only: Clear all filters alone, as commanders’ no-single-fix case.
  • Clear filters becomes Clear all filters, the chassis’s word; Keep only search gets a copy key. For /copy-review.

For engineering

  • Move ZeroState to a shared place (e.g. ui/) and render it from no-results.tsx; delete NoResults’ own markup.
  • Collection’s breakpoint moves from md to sm with it.
  • /decks, precons and the set page are not in this call; they can fold in later.
