When it shows (README note)

In a friend’s deck list in the game log’s seat picker, after their decks and before Another commander, for every friend whose friend decks switch is on (PickerFriend.withheld), whatever their settings hide or don’t. It seats the friend with no deck, exactly as Just name does. It never says whether a hidden deck exists, how many, or which: no count, no “hidden”, and a friend with no decks listed is not told “has no decks”.

The slot

  • 56 × 40, 6px radius, 1px dashed var(--border-dashed), no ground; the mark centred in --fg-muted.
  • A deck not listed: ? (sans 16). Another commander: the search glyph (16). Just name: the initials (mono 12), as the seat shows them.
  • aria-hidden, as the deck rows’ art is.

For engineering

  • seat-picker.tsx: the three text-only rows gain the slot; nothing else changes (order, copy, condition, what they seat).
  • Rule 0 setup’s Leave it to name keeps its own 58 × 42 slot.
