1 · Replace, not stack. A card sheet opened from a sheet starts at 0, like any card. No peek of the sheet underneath. The receipt's 96px offset and its step down to --surface-sunken (verdict addendum, ruling 1) retire on the phone. The ruling's reason, that a reader can see where a dismissal lands, is now met by the label.

2 · The way back sits in the ✕'s slot. It's a pill, ← {sheet}, top-right at y 22–54. That's the slot the desktop's back pill uses and where the phone's ✕ already went back one layer. The counter keeps its plate on the left. There's no second ✕ beside it: one control, one layer.

3 · What it says. The sheet's short name: Game changers, What would have to go, Cheaper stand-ins, Which deck to pull from. It ellipsizes at 266 wide (354 with no counter). The accessible name is Back to {sheet}, as the phone ✕ ships today.

4 · Paging works in a stacked card. It pages its own list (carousel call 9): the receipt's tiles, the candidates, the stand-ins in sheet order. Teaser, full, the grabber and the 10px lock are unchanged, and paging is still withheld in full. A drag starting within 16px of either edge still goes to the system.

5 · One press, one layer. Escape, hardware Back and the pill each step once. From full: full → teaser → the sheet underneath, with focus on the tile that opened the card. The pill skips the teaser rung and goes straight back, because it names a place, not a state.

6 · Motion. The card rises from the bottom over 240ms cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), the sheet snap's curve. The sheet underneath fades to the scrim under it. Back reverses that in 200ms. It never slides sideways, because sideways is paging. With reduced motion it swaps, and drags still track the finger.

7 · List sheets keep their shipped geometry. Stand-ins still rise from 52 over the card. The card no longer drops to 52 beneath it: it stays put at full size under the scrim. The sheet's ✕ becomes the same pill, ← {card}.

8 · Three deep, on one path only. Card → stand-ins → stand-in is the shipped cap and stays the only three-deep path. Depth no longer costs the card, so it no longer needs to be cut. Nothing in a stacked card opens a further layer, as shipped: no door, no Find a deck, and the combo partner replaces.

9 · Take moves into the teaser. The stand-in's 84px footer was the other half of the shrink. Take now shares the teaser's foot row with All details, and the oracle text gives up one line for in place of {card}. The teaser stays 280 and the card stays 320. In full, Take pins to the sheet's foot.
