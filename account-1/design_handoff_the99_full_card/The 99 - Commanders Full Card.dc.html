Amendment 4 · commanders detail · “Full card”

The commander stops being the one card in the app with its own modal.

Every other card on the commander detail page — every coverage chip, every synergy row — already opens CardModal through the provider mounted at detail.tsx:240. The commander itself opens a bespoke lightbox that draws a picture of a card and tells you to tap anywhere. The rich modal wins, and the lightbox does not survive.

This is not a close call on features — the modal is a strict superset except for one thing, art size, and it answers that with a full-bleed blurred wash of the same art plus real selectable text instead of pixels. It is close on promise: a control labelled Full card that opens a details panel has lied. So the label changes with the destination — Card details, and the accessible name falls back to the shared View details for {name} every other trigger on the page uses.

Read README.md for the decisions log and the deletion list, PARITY-lightbox-to-modal.md for the element-by-element mapping of what the lightbox did and where each piece goes, full-card · copy strings.ts for the copy diff, READINESS.md for what blocks a green. Mock commander is Kykar, Wind's Fury — continuous with the sibling handoffs. Oracle text in the frames is placeholder; production reads cards.oracle_text.

Two amendments, one file
Amendment 1 exists to delete PIP_TEXT, and PIP_TEXT lives in lightbox.tsx, which this amendment deletes outright. They are independent and land in either order — if this one ships first, amendment 1's deletion note is already satisfied and only the six token values remain.

Rejected labels
Keep Full card. Cheapest, and it is the lie. The surface it now opens leads with a 180px thumbnail and four meta fields; a player who tapped for a big picture got a spec sheet.
Details alone. True and weightless. On a page that is already all detail — three breakdown sections, forty named cards — “Details” does not say whose.
Card details. Chosen. Two words, names the subject and the surface, and it is the phrase the app already uses in cardDetailCopy — the close button on the thing it opens is labelled Close card details.

The availability block is the argument in miniature. A player choosing a commander to build wants to know their only copy is already sleeved in another deck — and the lightbox had no slot in which to say it. Also the black pip, drawn at amendment 1's value: the two amendments meet on this frame.
The three things the lightbox did that must not be lost
1 · The art filling the screen. Kept, as the modal's own blurred full-bleed wash at opacity 0.45, blur(22px), scale(1.2) — which is the lightbox's own device (0.28, 3px) taken further. Same atmosphere, and the sharp copy no longer competes with a second legible copy of its own text.
2 · Tap anywhere to close. Kept. The modal's backdrop closes on click, exactly as the lightbox's overlay did. The hint line goes, because at 390px the modal is a full-screen sheet with no visible backdrop to tap — there is a 32px ✕ in the corner and it is where a sheet's close control belongs.
3 · A commander with no art still shows a card. Kept, differently. The lightbox drew a card frame — name, pips, oracle glass over an identity gradient — because the picture was the surface. The modal already puts every one of those elements on screen at larger sizes, so the art slot takes identityGradient and nothing is missing. 282 lines of hand-drawn card frame go with it.

Desktop gets the trigger too — one added prop
The brief asks about mobile, because mobile is where the lightbox lives. But the desktop commander page shows the card art and the oracle text and has no route to the printing picker at all — and the commander is the single card a player is most likely to want alternate art for. So the desktop art panel becomes the same CardDetailTrigger, which is what builder-spine.tsx already does with the commander thumb on /decks/[id].
This is the one thing in this amendment that is more than the brief asked for. Reject it and nothing else changes: the mobile call, the deletion and the label all stand on their own. Take it and the two widths finally behave the same way, which is the actual complaint underneath “mobile opens the bare lightbox”.
No visible affordance is added at desktop — no chip, no ⛶. The art becomes a button with the shared accessible name and the shared pointer cursor, matching the spine. A chip over a 320px art panel on a page that already has a primary Build this deck action would be a third competing target.
Prev / Next — 1 of 1, and left alone
The provider renders 1 of 1 with both arrows disabled when no sibling list is passed, and that is the correct reading here: a commander has no peers on its own page.
Considered and rejected: passing the ranked commander list as siblings, so Next pages to the next recommendation. It sounds free and it is wrong — paging inside a modal would silently disagree with the page behind it, which is still the commander you opened. /commanders is the surface for comparing commanders, and it is one back-navigation away.
And no Add control in the modal on this route — the constraint the recommendations and synergy handoffs both carried. The modal must not assume a deck; the commander page's own Build this deck is the entry point.

The client payload gets smaller. LightboxCommander shipped manaCost, typeLine, oracleText, colorIdentity and imageUri to the client on every commander detail render, to draw a surface most players never open. The modal fetches on click — the pattern the provider's own comment says it exists for.
Accessibility ledger
keptFocus trap, Escape-to-close, focus return to the trigger, scroll lock — both surfaces use useDialogA11y. The trigger is never disabled while the fetch runs, so focus always has somewhere to return to.
gainedOracle text and the type line become real text rather than an sr-only transcript of an image. Selectable, zoomable, reflowing, and one copy instead of two.
gainedThe trigger's accessible name joins the shared one — View details for Kykar, Wind's Fury — so the page stops having two conventions for the same gesture.
gainedA second Escape stage: with the printing region open, Escape collapses it and returns focus to the Set field before a second Escape closes the dialog.
changedaria-label on the dialog becomes aria-labelledby the card name heading — the modal's shipped pattern. The lightbox's “Full card — {name}” label goes with it.
lost“Tap anywhere to close”. The gesture survives on the backdrop; the sentence does not, because at 390px the sheet is full-screen and there is no visible backdrop for the sentence to be about. The 32px ✕ is at the top-right, at a 44px hit box.
noteOne new live-region announcement reaches this route for free: the provider's Loading card details… during the fetch. The lightbox opened instantly because its data was already on the client; this trades an invisible payload for a named wait.
