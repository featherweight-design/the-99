One title block on all three routes, a quieter row 2 on the phone, and one row from 900.

Q1 — The strip sits under the same “Collection” title block on all three routes. That is /collection’s shipped block: p-[28px_34px_0], the title at 30px, the stats line, and Replace. Sets and Proxies take it whole, so the strip’s top edge (y = 109) and its first tab (x = 34) are identical on every route. Below the strip, Sets and Proxies keep their 900px columns, but anchored left at 34px instead of centred, so the whole place shares one left edge. Their section names (“Your sets”, “What you’re printing”) stay their h1s, as E11 rules, at the shipped 28px, directly under the strip. On those two routes “Collection” renders as a styled p, so each page keeps exactly one h1. §B1

Q2 — Below 480px, row 2 hides two words: the “Sort:” prefix and the count’s noun. Both stay in the accessible names. The order is what the reader needs to see, so it never truncates in practice. The decklist’s Sort trigger already reads the bare order (deckSortCopy.triggerOne), so the hidden prefix follows precedent and needs no new copy. With a filter on and the longest order, row 2 is 316px: 42 spare at 390, 12 at 360. The label keeps min-width: 0 and an ellipsis only as a safety net for font fallback. §B2

Q3 — The one-row head starts at 900px, not 600. With the shipped trigger, the longest order, a filter on and a four-digit count, the one-row controls are 518px. At 900 that leaves search 288px beside the 62px pointer rail and 262 beside the 88px touch rail, which is never less than the phone gives it at 390. From 480 to 899 the head is the two-row 102px form with the full trigger and noun. From 1000 the wide chassis is unchanged. §B3
