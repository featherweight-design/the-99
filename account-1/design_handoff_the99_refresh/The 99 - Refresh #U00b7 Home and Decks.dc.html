The pages we are not redesigning — conformance only
Commander detail
Role Coverage chips and EDHREC Synergy stay exactly as they are, full sections included. Touch-ups only: the Order and category controls take the 32px hairline pill, and the rail replaces the page’s own back chrome.
Commanders list
Today’s row keeps every figure — role counts, synergy bar, free and in-use receipts, fit and delta. Touch-ups: one search field sitting with the filters instead of apart from them, live filtering with Clear, and no token query.
Builder
Stays as it ships and the Tuning panel is withdrawn. Touch-ups: buttons to 32px hairline with one filled primary, and the header status pill uses the six shipped values.
Card detail
No change. It already carries the geometry and the availability atoms, and only inherits the button and field styles.
