§5.1 container
It is a card with no scrim. It doesn't take focus, doesn't hold the page and isn't aria-modal. On desktop it sits bottom-left beside the account avatar, where About lives. On a phone it runs full width above the tab bar and never covers it.
§5.2 limit
Up to four new entries, every title is listed. From five, it lists three and then and N more. So the summary always hides at least two, and never reads “and 1 more” in place of a title that would fit.
§5.3 the jump
The entry's title line takes --elevated, the same fill as the contents' current row, so both mean “you are here”. It clears when the entry leaves the upper band or another jump lands. It is held in client state, not :target, so a reload never shows it.
§5.4 contents
In the desktop rail every chapter is open, and the list scrolls inside the sticky rail under a search field that stays put. On a phone the list collapses to four chapter rows, and each one opens its own entries. A search opens every chapter that has a match. This collapses the contents list, never the page.
§5.5 the search
It sits under the Contents label at both widths. It matches any part of a title, ignoring case, and prints the matched run bold. When nothing matches, it says so and says the page is untouched: Nothing titled “mulligan”.
§5.6 the old marks
The eyebrow retires: its sentence becomes the index's heading. The chapter rows keep N new, entry rows in the contents gain new, and the NEW pill on the entry stays.

the mark
Only the title line takes --elevated, radius 6, spilling 10px (8px on a phone) into the gutter. It is not an accent edge. A decklist row's accent edge means an uncommitted change, and this page draws that row as a specimen. The rail's row for the entry carries the same fill, so both say “here”.
how long
The fill holds while the entry is in the upper 30% band, the band useCurrentChapter already watches. It clears when the entry leaves that band or another jump lands. It isn't timed, because a timer would take the mark away from a reader who is still reading the title. It is never written anywhere, so it can't persist across visits (M1).
NEW and the wash together
They answer different questions. The pill now leads the title, so it is the first thing read on the line. A new entry's top rule also turns 2px accent, which is what the eye catches when scrolling. Together they say the entry arrived since your last visit. The wash says the jump brought you here. A contents or search jump to an entry that isn't new gets the wash only (4e).
§4

The expanded contents, and its search

The search runs client-side over the titles the page already holds, and matches any part of a title, ignoring case. It filters the rows of the contents list. Chapter rows with no match drop out of the list while the query stands, and every chapter on the page stays exactly where it was. Drawn for a reader at seen 1, so the new marks carry through into the results. Enter in the field jumps to the first match, and ↓ moves into the list. The query survives a jump, so Back returns to the same results.
