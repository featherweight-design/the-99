ABOUT PAGE R2 R1 was a list of sentences · this one shows the app
what changed, and why

R1 answered the five open questions and got the artifact wrong: a reference page made of paragraphs cannot teach a fit score, a doubled diamond or a dashed cell. Every entry now leads with a real specimen — drawn from the shipped component, at its shipped size — and the prose is its caption.

Locked decisions are untouched: one artifact, redirect not banner, organised by what the reader is doing, auth-gated, menu as the permanent entry. What changes is the unit. An entry is a figure with a caption, and where the thing being explained is a mark or a score, the figure is the explanation and the prose only says what the reader cannot see.

the unit
Specimen, title, caption, annotations. Two widths of specimen: inset (300px, figure left of the prose) for a single row or control, and plate (full column, prose above) for anything with parts that need naming — a score, a symbol key, a status set. Twelve of the twenty-four entries are plates.
the specimens are the components
Every figure is the real thing at its real size: AvailabilityMarker’s 6px diamonds, FitBar’s three segments, StatusPillBadge at 18% over --bg, SetCell’s dashed --fg-locked edge and .28 art. In build they are the components, imported and fed fixture props — not screenshots, and not a second drawing to maintain.
annotations, not arrows
A part is named by a mono label under the specimen, keyed by a small numeral printed beside the part itself. No leader lines, no callout bubbles, no hover reveals — the constraint is no motion and nothing interactive, and a numbered key is the only annotation that survives both. Never more than four per figure.
the art question
Specimens that host card art draw the colour-identity gradient ArtWash already falls back to rather than a Scryfall crop. A fixed commander in the reference would date the page and tie it to one card’s licence; the gradient is the app’s own no-art state and is honest at every width. ⚠ Product call — see READINESS.

Below: the two specimen widths and the annotation key (§1), the “new” mark, unchanged from R1 (§2), the page at desktop, version 0, all four chapters and all twenty-four figures (§3), the behind and current readers (§4), phone (§5), the account menu (§6), and the contract (§7).

Unchanged from R1 and for the same reason: twenty-four accent titles is an accent field. The whole entry — figure included — is inside the anchor, so the specimen is part of the target rather than a picture beside a link.

R1’s undivided rows worked because every row was the same shape. Figures are not: two plates in a row read as one figure with two halves without a --border rule between them. One hairline per entry boundary, none after the last.
§2

The “new” mark — carried over from R1, and now with a second reason

A mono NEW knocked out of a filled accent plate, unchanged. The figures make the case stronger, not weaker: this page now draws the app’s own marks as specimens, so any invented shape would be sitting inches from the four diamonds, two dots and dashed box it must not be mistaken for. A word cannot enter that family.

R1’s argument was that no cardboard appears on /about, so nothing can be mistaken for in use or to buy. and the numeral key is the second new object

§3

The page at desktop — reader at version 0, twenty-four entries, twenty-four figures

Drawn whole rather than sampled. Nothing is marked and no dismiss is drawn — that is the version-0 reader, and it is also exactly what a current reader arriving from the menu sees. Every string in a specimen is a real string from refresh.ts, lanes.ts, availability.ts, game-log.ts or commander-explained.ts; figures are plausible fixtures, never invented vocabulary.

§5

Phone — 390px, where the figures have to earn their place

Three rules, and they are the whole of the responsive design. One: an inset becomes a stack — figure above caption, full width. Two: a plate’s internal grid halves — four tiles become 2×2, three key columns become three rows, the status table drops its progress column and keeps its badge and figure. Three: nothing scrolls sideways and nothing shrinks below its shipped size — a 6px diamond at 390px is still 6px, because that is the size the reader has to recognise in the app.

why first, and not between
Manage account and Sign out are two acts on the account, ordered mildest to most destructive. About is neither an act nor about the account. Dropping it in the middle splits that pair and puts a benign navigation one row above a sign-out.
three engineering costs, restated
MenuItems holds a two-element ref tuple and wraps with % 2 — both literals are the item count. About is the menu’s first link rather than its third button, so busy becomes aria-disabled and the panel must close on press. And inserting first moves the border-t rule onto Manage account.
and no badge, at any width

§7

The contract

THE ENTRYOne <Link> wrapping title, route, optional opens-clause, caption and specimen. Two widths: inset (300px figure, prose right) and plate (prose above, figure full column). A hairline between entries; none after the last in a chapter.
SPECIMENSThe real components, fed fixture props — AvailabilityMarker, OwnershipDot, CostPips, BuyTag, FitBar, FitLegend, SetCell, StatusPillBadge, ShelfLine. Each sits on --surface-sunken inside a --border hairline at radius 8, is aria-hidden, and is inert — no hover, no focus, no handler. Fixtures live in one module, not inline per entry.
ANNOTATION14px circle, --accent hairline and ink on --bg, mono 9px, printed beside the part; a mono --fg-faint line per numeral under the figure. Max four per figure, no leader lines, no arrows, no hover. Key lines are ordinary prose in reading order — the figure is never the only carrier.
ARTNo Scryfall crops. Art-bearing specimens draw ArtWash’s colour-identity gradient with its own ink scrim, which is the app’s shipped no-art state. ⚠ Product call — a real crop would date the page and pin it to one card.
PHONEInset becomes a stack. A plate’s grid halves: 4 tiles → 2×2, 3 key columns → 3 rows, the status table drops its progress column, the set grid drops to 3 cells, Ready-to-build draws one card. Nothing scrolls sideways and no mark shrinks below its shipped size. Contents is four 44px rows in the flow, with entry counts; it does not stick and does not track scroll.
MARKMono 9.5/600, 0.1em, --bg on --accent, radius 3, with an sr-only “New in this version”. On the title line, never inside a specimen’s hairline. Rendered on seen > 0 && entry.since > seen, nowhere outside /about, and never as a badge on the menu or the avatar.
COPYEvery string inside a specimen is imported from the surface’s own copy file, never retyped: a rename in refresh.ts or lanes.ts must reach this page. Captions and key lines are about.ts’s own. Figures in fixtures are plausible, never real user data.
A11YOne h1, four h2, entry titles as h3 inside the link. The route is spoken, not decorative. Specimens are aria-hidden in full, so no component’s internal sr-only text leaks into the link’s name. Index is a nav labelled Contents with aria-current. No live region.
MOTIONNone, and now it is load-bearing: a specimen must not animate, transition, or respond to a pointer, or a reader will try to use it. Components that ship with transitions are mounted with them off. No reduced-motion state, because there is no motion to reduce.
