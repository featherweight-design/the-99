E3 · Cheaper stand-ins (new)
The revised figure shows one type group from the sheet, and the sheet reads its list when it opens. Export its group markup unchanged (StandInGroup: heading, order note, rows, Show all) and let the sheet render it. The figure passes three rows and a total of 8. E1 · Find a deck for it
FitsPicker reads its list when it opens (loadFitsAction) inside ModalChrome, so it can't be mounted inert with fixture props. Export its plate and row markup as they are (FitsPlate, FitsRows) and have the picker render them. That is an extraction, not a fork: one component serves both.
E2 · Change commander
The sheet reads on open too (readChangeCandidates). Export its Row and GroupHeading unchanged, as CandidateRow. If that is declined, the fallback specimen is the door plus the review page's CommanderSection. That fallback loses the figure, which is the entry's subject.
