3b Rank 5’s mark — reissued R6 · asset owed

Confirmed against the files: all ten paths are identical, and only the numeral and a dx nudge differ. The set is additive — 1 is the inner diamond alone, 2 adds the mid ring, 3 adds four cardinal ticks, 4 keeps those ticks and adds four small diamonds outside them, and 5 adds nothing at all, which is why it came out as rank 4 with a different numeral.

The fix is not a fifth ornament. At 22px another stud is a smudge, and the escalation has run out of room at the cardinal points. The silhouette goes from two rings and four dots to three rings, which is a difference the eye takes at a glance and at a blur.

Overview

A ninjutsu deck that converts unblocked one-drops into the top of a very expensive library. Yuriko, the Tiger’s Shadow makes every connection a burn spell, so the curve is built upside down: cheap evasive bodies to carry the trigger, and expensive cards behind them to make it hurt. The interaction is free rather than cheap — Fierce Guardianship and Force of Negation protect the commander on a turn the deck spends its mana elsewhere, which is what lets a two-card win sit on top of a deck that otherwise looks like a tempo list.

Its ceiling comes from the tutor suite rather than the threats: nine searchers across both colours mean the Thassa’s Oracle line is available most games, and the commander’s own draw keeps refilling toward it. Left alone, the deck kills through damage by turn six; interacted with, it pivots to the combo.
