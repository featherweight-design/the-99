The art and the price are the evidence. No row, tile or caption gains a mark.

A chosen printing is cosmetic, like the art preference, and that preference has never put a mark on a tile. So a choice shows as what it changes: the art on tiles, the card view, the hover preview and the commander head, and the figure on a priced tag. Whether it was chosen or is the default is said in one place, the card's In this deck block. Every hue on a row is already booked (the proxy tag's own ruling), and a twelfth mark on a 52px phone row would be noise about the least urgent fact in the deck.

Brief open 9: the rail doesn't tell the two kinds apart. A version is a record of the list, and every version before the line is the same record it was yesterday. Inside an open older version, one mono line says why its art is today's. It draws only on versions committed before the line, and only once. A newer version's diff gains a quiet Printings group under the card changes, only when printings differ from the version before. It counts toward no figure on the rail: v6's +2 / −2 are cards. The first version after the line has no Printings group, since there's no recorded printing to compare it with.
