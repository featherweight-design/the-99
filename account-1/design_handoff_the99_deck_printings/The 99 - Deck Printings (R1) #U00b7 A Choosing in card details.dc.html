Deck printings · canvas 1 of 2 · §A choosing a deck’s printing · 2026-10-09

A deck’s printing is chosen in the block that already says “In this deck”.

Card details opened from a deck already carry one deck-scoped block: the proxy control, headed In this deck. It's absent without a deck, and it's the only part of the modal that writes to the deck. A printing choice is the same kind of thing (cosmetic, one deck, takes effect at once, Hugh 2026-10-09), so it joins that block as one row above the proxy tick. Change opens PrintingRegion role="choose" source="catalog" inside the block. Choosing a tile writes immediately and closes the region, the way the proxy tick does. There is no Save.

No second door. Rows, tiles and the Add panel gain nothing. A printing is a detail you set once per card, not something you do while building, and every row already opens card details.
