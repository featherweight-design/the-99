Rail mark — R1 · ruled 1a · 2026-09-08

The rail head is a diamond. The mark is a crowned numeral. Put the mark back.

The 62px rail ships a bare 26px outlined diamond at the top — the crown's middle diamond, alone, with the numerals gone. It is the only place in the product where the app identifies itself at desktop width, and it currently identifies as a shape the nav also uses for Discover. All three candidates keep the 62px rail and one head slot 14–18px from the top — its height differs (39px in 1a, 21px in 1b, 38px in 1c, against today's 26px) — and the rule from CHROME-rail-and-tabs.md: the mark appears once, at the top, and is never repeated per row.

The head is <Crowned99> either way — nav.tsx already imports it for the wide rail and the mobile header; this adds a variant="rail-62". Crown takes --accent so it themes; numerals take --fg. Rail shown in the shipped slate theme.

Geometry. Crown 6 / 8.5 / 6px, 3px of visible air between points (6px layout gap — a rotated square is 1.414× as wide as its box), numerals Cinzel 900 at 23px, 4px between crown and digits — a 32.5×39 lockup (crown row 12 + 4 + 23) centred in the 62px rail with 14px above.

Cost. The head grows 26 → 39px tall, so the nav block starts 13px lower and the head zone is 14 + 39 + 20 = 73px. Nothing else on the rail moves.

Why. Filled brass reads at a glance where a 1.5px outline does not, it stops colliding with Discover's diamond glyph, and the crown is the half of the mark that is ours — the numerals are a numeral.
Cost. Only the phone header and the wide rail still say THE 99 in words. Desktop never spells the name.
