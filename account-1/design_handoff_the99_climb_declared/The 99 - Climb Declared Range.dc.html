Addendum F · H34 · the Climb under an answered range

The tinted rung follows the answer. The other half of the range reads permitted.

The rule, for both ranges. The rungs drawn do not change: still climb(permits), so a 1/2 deck draws 1 and 2, a 4/5 deck draws 3, 4 and 5. Once the range is answered, only the two rungs inside it trade roles. The declared bracket takes the yours treatment (tint, ink border, full mark, allowances) with the label declared. The other bracket in the range becomes a new role, permitted. Rungs outside the range (the 4/5 deck's rung 3) draw as today.

1 · Which rung is yours

It follows the answer. The head names one bracket in its ink; the one tinted rung must be that bracket, in that ink, or the page draws two answers. The cards' fact is not lost: both rungs in the range still say the cards reach them, one by its allowances, one by permitted.

2 · Answer is the higher bracket

The upper rung claims this is the deck's bracket, with the allowances at that bracket. The lower rung claims only the cards fit here as well: its label and nothing else. Neither says the deck uses less than it is allowed.

3 · Answer is the lower bracket

Not right today, for either range. At 4/5, permits more than this deck uses is false: 4 and 5 share every card limit. At 1/2 it is true but beside the point. The rung above takes the same permitted treatment as in 2, so the rule is symmetric.

Head, marks and inks unchanged. Two new strings, placeholders for copy review: roleDeclared, roleAlso. The permitted rung carries no body. Retired on an answered range: roleHere / roleHereShort and aboveAllowed on the in-range rung. Jump: F1 · F2 · F3 · F4 · states table
