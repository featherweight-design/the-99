# States · You have a card a friend wants R1

## Tile press (friend's Wishlist tab)
| State | Draws |
|---|---|
| Reading your copies | Nothing (shipped tab). No room reserved. |
| Read failed | Nothing. No toast. |
| None of yours | Nothing. |
| You can spare it | Box + spare glyph, `--fact`. |
| Spare, friend asked you (open) | Glyph + accent dot (`asked you`). |
| Spare, you offered (open) | Glyph + accent dot (`offered`). |
| Agreed (their yes to your offer, or your yes to their ask) | Glyph green + tick (`sparing`). |
| Declined / expired / withdrawn | Plain glyph. |
| Every copy in a deck, draft or kept back | Box + committed dot. |
| Sheet open | Box ringed (`--accent` outline 2px, offset 2). |

## Key line (under the head)
Only items a tile draws, in order: you can spare · in your decks · asked you · offered · sparing. No tile with a press: no line.

## Friend's side
- Waiting on you row `Spare offer` with Yes / No / days left, ✕ hides it (an ask's rules). Receipt for the visit as an ask's.
- Their Your requests: the offer once answered reads as an answered ask (`Accepted` / `Declined`).
- Unfriend, purge: as spare requests.
