The bottom fade shows only when there is more to scroll to

The v1 fade is drawn whether the list overflows or not, so it erases the foot of every dashed block v2 added and dims the last folded By deck group. Tying it to overflow fixes all of them at once without touching the blocks.

The rule

  1. The list box’s 36px bottom fade draws only while the box has more content below its visible end: scrollHeight − scrollTop − clientHeight > 1, checked on scroll and on resize (one observer for the box).
  2. So it is never drawn over the no-match block, R2’s failed block, Empty-One B or C, a short list, or a By deck list with every group folded.
  3. It appears and goes without a transition (no new motion).
  4. The dashed blocks keep their shipped padding; nothing else moves. Phone: unchanged, it has no fade.
