R3 · 1Phone · Filters sheet

A failed read is said inside the open sheet, never on top of it

The closeout walk caught R2’s failed-read toast drawn over the open Filters sheet, on its Owned control. The toast belongs to the page, and the sheet is modal, so while it is open the failure moves into the sheet itself.

The rule

  1. While the Filters sheet is open, a failed read is said inside the sheet, in the Friend group’s place: the shipped result.failedByFriend line (“Friends’ spares didn’t load”) and failedRead.retry (Try again). No new string. The toast is not drawn over a sheet.
  2. Said once (R2 ruling 1): a failure said in the sheet raises no toast when the sheet closes. A read that fails while the sheet is shut raises the toast as shipped.
  3. A toast already up when the sheet opens is dismissed, and the sheet opens with the failed line in place.
  4. Try again re-runs the read; the group shows the dashed reading… (Loading A) until it lands, then the friend pills.
  5. Desktop has no sheet: R2 stands as drawn.
R3 · 2Phone · cart header

The field says “Search” on a phone, and the chips get room under it

With a filter on, the Filters pill grows by its count badge and the field loses the width “Search cards” needed. The shortest fix that keeps every control is the placeholder: the search glyph already carries the rest.

The rule

  1. Below 1000px (the compact chassis), the cart’s field placeholder is Search. From 1000px it stays Search cards.
  2. The row keeps its order and sizes: the field takes what is left (flex 1, min-width 0), then the Filters pill with its badge, then the 44px ⇅. Nothing in the row wraps or hides.
  3. At 320 with a badge the field is about 104px: the icon and “Search” at 14px fit.
  4. The field’s accessible name stays “Search cards” at every width; only the visible placeholder shortens.
  5. In the compact form, the chips row sits 16px under the controls row (shipped: 10px, the chassis’s one row gap). The chips to result line gap stays 10px, so the chips read as belonging to what follows, not glued to the field. Desktop is unchanged.
R3 · 3Desktop · cart list

The bottom fade shows only when there is more to scroll to

The v1 fade is drawn whether the list overflows or not, so it erases the foot of every dashed block v2 added and dims the last folded By deck group. Tying it to overflow fixes all of them at once without touching the blocks.

The rule

  1. The list box’s 36px bottom fade draws only while the box has more content below its visible end: scrollHeight − scrollTop − clientHeight > 1, checked on scroll and on resize (one observer for the box).
  2. So it is never drawn over the no-match block, R2’s failed block, Empty-One B or C, a short list, or a By deck list with every group folded.
  3. It appears and goes without a transition (no new motion).
  4. The dashed blocks keep their shipped padding; nothing else moves. Phone: unchanged, it has no fade.

A reader with no friends sees no spare wording at all

The friend count is known at first paint, yet By card and By deck still start a spares read and narrate it: the spare key, the reading clause, the Friend trigger, the column, and a failed toast when it errs. For this reader the cart reads exactly as it would with spares switched off.

The state and its copy

  1. When it applies: the reader has no friends (hasFriends is false, known at first paint). Then the spares read is not made, so it can neither be reading nor fail.
  2. By card and By deck: no spare key item, no Friend trigger (desktop) or Friend group (phone sheet), no Friends’ spares column, no reading clause, no failed toast. Rows never had a spare line for this reader; that stays.
  3. Result line: By card 48 cards (with a filter: 31 of 48 cards); By deck 6 decks · 59 copies. These are the shipped result.byCardNoSpare, result.filtered and result.byDeck, so no new string.
  4. By friend: unchanged, Empty-One C (No friends yet, Go to Friends). It is the one place the cart talks about friends to this reader.
  5. A first friend added mid-visit takes effect on the next load, like the friend count today. Nothing is drawn for the change.
