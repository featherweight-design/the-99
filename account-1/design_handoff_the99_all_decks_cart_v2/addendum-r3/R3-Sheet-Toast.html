R3 · 1Phone · Filters sheet

A failed read is said inside the open sheet, never on top of it

The closeout walk caught R2’s failed-read toast drawn over the open Filters sheet, on its Owned control. The toast belongs to the page, and the sheet is modal, so while it is open the failure moves into the sheet itself.

The rule

  1. While the Filters sheet is open, a failed read is said inside the sheet, in the Friend group’s place: the shipped result.failedByFriend line (“Friends’ spares didn’t load”) and failedRead.retry (Try again). No new string. The toast is not drawn over a sheet.
  2. Said once (R2 ruling 1): a failure said in the sheet raises no toast when the sheet closes. A read that fails while the sheet is shut raises the toast as shipped.
  3. A toast already up when the sheet opens is dismissed, and the sheet opens with the failed line in place.
  4. Try again re-runs the read; the group shows the dashed reading… (Loading A) until it lands, then the friend pills.
  5. Desktop has no sheet: R2 stands as drawn.
