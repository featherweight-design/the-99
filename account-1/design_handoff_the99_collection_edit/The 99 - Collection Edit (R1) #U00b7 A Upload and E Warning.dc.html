§E — One component, rows are always decks. The figure on a deck's row is what it will be short of. That's the same rule at both widths: three rows fit a 390 sheet above its buttons with no scroll. It's a dialog on desktop and a bottom sheet on the phone, and inside the Replace review it's embedded as a section.
