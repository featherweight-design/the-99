Editing copies leaves the card modal. It opens the same sheet as Add cards, with one line per copy you hold.

One editor, not two. Adding cards (§B) and editing copies are the same job: lines of printing × finish × how many, with one Save. So Edit copies opens the Add cards sheet scoped to one card, already filled with what you hold. That answers four R1 problems at once. The card modal stays exactly as shipped, with one link added. Nothing is squeezed into it, and there's no → ×6 after saving. Add another printing adds a line in this same sheet, using §B's required printing step. The phone teaser keeps its foot row, so the swipe up is untouched.

Move is gone. R1's Move meant “these copies are logged under the wrong printing”. In R2 that's just the line's printing chip: tap it, pick the right printing, and the copies go with it. To fix only some of them, lower that line and add the right printing as a new one. Rows stay exactly as shipped. No ⋯, no Undo strips. Removing a card is Remove from collection at the foot of the sheet. That changes the brief's §D: see D.

Only from /collection. The card modal opens from decks, the Shopping list, search and History too. Edit copies draws only when the modal was opened from /collection (a CardDetailProvider prop, collectionEdit). Inside a deck, “edit copies” reads as editing the deck's own count, and that's a different thing with its own controls. Everywhere else the receipt stays read-only, exactly as shipped. Marking bought cards from Shopping stays sub-project 4's job.

D · rows stay as shipped · removing lives in the sheet · a change to the brief for Hugh

The brief's §D puts a remove control on the Collection row. Each shipped row is one button that opens the card, at both widths, with no free cell on the phone (38 · 1fr · 76 · auto). A control inside it would need a sibling wrapper and a new column, and that's the R1 ⋯ you flagged as incongruous. R2 leaves the row alone. Remove is two presses from it: the row, then Edit copies, then Remove from collection. That's no further than the edit itself. The removed card leaves the list, and a toast offers Undo.
