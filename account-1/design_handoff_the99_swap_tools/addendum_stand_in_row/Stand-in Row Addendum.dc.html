A stand-in row says what it keeps, gains and loses

This amends the ranking rebuild (1b) and replaces nothing else. The fact line under each name now compares the card with the one it replaces, in Hugh’s words: keeps, gains, loses. The two synergy shares leave the row, so a row never shows a percentage. They still set the order, which the group heading states. A row runs to 2 lines at 1300 and 3 at 390.

Turn 3 · three ways to mark the groups

In 2a the dots and the verbs were too faint to separate the groups, and one mono run couldn’t hold several items per group. All three options share two changes. First, the comparison moves to its own line spanning the full row, under the price and Take, which roughly doubles its measure (about 560px at 1300, 315px at 390). Second, items are sans 12 and markers are mono, so the typeface itself divides label from content. Items within a group are comma-separated, and whole groups wrap as units. The row count is unchanged: 2 lines at 1300, 3 at 390. The fixtures carry two items per group on purpose, to show the worst case.

3cPlain, plus, struck

The markers are the text itself. Kept items are plain, each gained item carries a +, and each lost item is struck through. A vertical hairline separates the groups. This is the quietest and most compact option, with every item self-describing. The risk is that struck text can read as “no longer offered”. Screen readers get the words.

2a

The row

One mono line, three verbs, fixed order

The line reads {type} · keeps {…} · gains {…} · loses {…}, and any segment can be absent. The verbs are in --fg-faint, what’s kept or gained is in --fg-body, and what’s lost is in --fg-muted. That’s the land row’s rule: the drawback reads last and quietest. The type leads only for other-types rows, as in 1b. For example, Incubation Druid: keeps taps for mana, +1/+1 counters · loses haste.

Two lines at 1300, three at 390

At 1300 it’s the name line plus one fact line of about 58 characters. At 390 the price chip moves up onto the name line, so the fact line gets two lines of about 36 characters. Past the budget, the writer drops gains first and never loses. The line also clamps (1 / 2 lines) as a backstop, but the copy is written to fit.

No figures on the row

The shares in 58% of Gishath decks, 41% of Naya are gone, and so is MV 2: mana value is only named when it differs, and only if it fits. The heading’s by Gishath synergy still says what orders the group. Staples keep their word, staple, before the comparison.

Lands keep their mana line

Design’s reading, which Hugh may overrule

A land row already says what it keeps and loses. The mana line is the keeps (makes white and red, untapped) and the drawback is the loses (1 damage for either). Rewriting it as keeps white and red · loses 1 life would drop the rung words that carry the ladder. So lands keep 1a’s line unchanged; only the 390 price moves.
