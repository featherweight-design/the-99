04 — System posture

The dials, set

Density

Loosen the browse, keep the work tight

Deck view, recommendations, and collection browse get room to breathe. Import review, the issue inspector, and editing stay dense and fast. A little global air is fine; the working surfaces are not the place for it.

Motion

Breathe, not chaos

Motion concentrates on the crab, loading, and celebrations. Browse surfaces get gentle life on hover. A little chaos is welcome — he is fighting monsters — but it always settles. Fully usable static; honors reduced-motion.

Material

A whisper, felt not seen

Candle-glow vignettes and warm light pooling behind hero moments. Barely-there grain on big empty areas only. Surfaces stay clean; the glow gives the kaiju something to loom out of. Never on dense data.

Named constraint · not a palette re-open

Contrast & engagement

Palette hues stay locked. But the current theme reads low-contrast and a touch inert — so this pass audits text/UI contrast and lifts anything below AA (the way the brand ramp already did once for faint). Engaging comes from the crab and the moments, not from louder color.
