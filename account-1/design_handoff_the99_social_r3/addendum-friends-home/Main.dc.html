Friends on Home · R1

Friends is the last section on Home at both widths: under Commitments in the right column at 1280, after Commitments at 390. It is a section, not a card: a label, up to four faces, one line and one Add a friend. On the first-run checklist it is an unnumbered row under step 3, live from the first visit.

CALL 1 · WHERE IT SITS Look at Home at 1280, H1–H4; Home at 390, P1–P4
  • 1280: the right column, under Commitments. The left column stays the deck work: Ready to build, then Pick up where you left off.
  • 390: last, after Commitments. Home keeps one block order at every width, so 640–1179 is the phone’s order too.
  • The banner spans both columns above the body and keeps its place. Friends never moves above it, and the two show together (H2, P2).
  • Quiet: no surface, border or ✕. The column’s one card stays Commitments.
CALL 2 · HOW MANY FACES Look at H3 and P3
  • Four slots, both widths. Four friends or fewer: every face. Five or more: three faces, then +N. The row never grows or wraps.
  • The face is the shipped Face at 40: signature commander, or initials. Name under it. Each face opens that friend’s page.
  • +N is a count, not a press. The heading carries the total (FRIENDS · 7) and All friends › is the one way to the rest.
  • Order is /friends’ own, by name. No new sort.
CALL 3 · THE CHECKLIST ROW Look at C1–C3 and CP1–CP3
  • Last, unnumbered, under step 3 with a wider gap. The kicker stays Step 1 of 3: a friend isn’t on the way to a first recommendation, and it’s open while 2 and 3 are locked.
  • Not done: a dashed + slot. The whole row opens Add a friend. With a link open, the row shows its time left and Show.
  • Done is one friend: ✓ in --success, their face, and the row opens /friends. It stays. With the invite-only launch, a newcomer usually arrives done.
