Wishlist remembers the printing you want · R1

A wishlist line can name a printing. Any printing stays the default. The choice is made in the card window, where R2 and R3 already let you browse printings, and it sets the tile's art and price, the card window's Mana Pool link and the price-drop alert. Shopping stays deck-only (your ruling, Social R2 call 10 stands). Drawn from the shipped Wishlist tab and card window, with alternate printings R3 patched in. Prices and art are placeholders.

1 · The tile

  • Under the caption, a third mono line: the price, then the wanted printing with the wishlist's bookmark, $24.50 ▮ SLD 2125. The tile's art is that printing's.
  • Any printing reads as the price alone ($3.99): the cheapest, as every buy tag does. No words for the default.
  • Sold out: sold out ▮ SLD 2455, art kept. The choice stands until you change it.
  • Phone: the set code only (SLD). The number joins from md, where the tile is wide enough. The card window always names both.

2 · Where you choose

  • In the card window only. The tile stays one press that opens it (minimal controls).
  • Desktop: the Wishlist box gains a Printing line under the stepper. Select a printing in the strip (R2) and the line offers Want SLD 2125; once wanted it shows the thumb, set and price, with Any printing to go back.
  • Phone: the R3 printings panel, on a card in your wishlist, gets a foot: Wanted: any printing and the same Want SLD 2125 press. The teaser stays 280. The full sheet's Wishlist box shows the same line.
  • A card opened from a tile with a chosen printing opens on that printing (R2's preview starts there). Wanting a printing never changes your collection pick.

3 · Price, Mana Pool link, price-drop alert

  • Price: the tile and the Printing line show the chosen printing's figure.
  • Link-out: Buy SLD 2125 at Mana Pool in place of Buy any printing at Mana Pool. Sold out falls back to the any-printing link.
  • Price-drop alert: watches the chosen printing. Choosing or changing one resets the baseline to its price then. The feed line reads at SLD 2125. for at the cheapest printing.. A sold-out printing never alerts, as today.
  • Sold out in the box: Mana Pool has none now. Any printing is $1.49.

4 · Phone and desktop

  • One design at both widths. Two differences: on the phone you choose in the panel's foot, since the Wishlist box sits in the full sheet; and the phone tile drops the collector number.
  • Unchanged: Shopping, deck slots (owned only), R3's browsing, the teaser height.
