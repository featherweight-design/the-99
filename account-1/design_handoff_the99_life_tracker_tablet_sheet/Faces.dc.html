Coin faces · four options (R3)

Hugh on R2: centre the emblems, give HEADS / TAILS room from the ring above it, make day and night Magic's own, and take the colour off the mana pips and let them fill the face. Every emblem now sits in the centre of the inner ring (r 35 of 50); the word has its own band between that ring and the edge (baseline r 43, caps clear of the ring by about 3%). Toss, timing and size are unchanged from the Coin board. The symbols are Magic's own as drawn in the mana-font set (the same family as the app's public/mana); a build would add the two or seven glyphs it uses to the app's icons.

A · Day and night

Heads is Magic's day symbol, tails its night: the markers printed on the day/night card and on daybound and nightbound faces (Innistrad: Midnight Hunt). Replaces R2's generic sun and moon.

  • The most Magic-literal pair, and it says heads or tails by shape alone.
  • Day's open centre keeps it light; night is a solid disc, so the two sides read differently even mid-flip.

B · Spark and planeswalker

Heads is the spark, tails the planeswalker symbol: the front and back markers of the Magic Origins planeswalkers who transform when their spark ignites. A second Magic-specific pair, offered beside day and night.

  • Every player knows the planeswalker symbol.
  • The spark is the less familiar of the two marks.

C · Mana, struck pips

Your mana option without colour: each pip is a disc cut into the coin with its symbol left standing in the metal, set larger (about 23% of the coin across, against 15%) so the five fill the ring. Tails is one large {C}.

  • Reads as mana pips at a glance, in the theme's own metal.
  • About 17px a pip on the phone's 72px coin.

D · Mana, bare symbols

The same five symbols engraved straight into the face with no discs, larger still (21% each). Tails is the {C} diamond alone.

  • The lightest of the four; the coin's metal shows most.
  • The symbols' own shapes vary in weight (the sun is thinner than the skull), so the ring looks less even than C.
