The coin toss

Drawn to match the dice: the dice library throws a d20 that tumbles across the table, bounces and settles, then the result card arrives with a ring that draws itself. Today's coin only spins flat in place. The new coin has a rim, rises and falls in an arc, turns end over end, rattles as it lands, and gets the same landing ring. Tablet 120px, phone 72px, at the shipped spot (38% down, centred). Tails is shown.
