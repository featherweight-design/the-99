What this pass commits to

One bone-filled button per screen and nothing else filled. Five hues, each with one meaning. Filters as a persistent column with a token field, never a hidden menu. Art at the top of every card that names a commander. Availability’s strings and severities are reproduced, not redesigned, and free still says nothing.

Next, if this reads right: the Collection grid at this vocabulary, mobile widths for all seven, and the loading screens re-checked against the new type weights — they keep their layouts, but the manifest wait in the builder should use the same rhythm.
