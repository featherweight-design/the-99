/collection
The 99 — Collection surface · Hi-fi

Your table, laid out

Every required state for the entry point of the product: flip through ~2,900 owned cards, search and filter to answer "do I own this?", open any card for its full text — and the deliberate two-step flow to replace a collection from a fresh export. Built mobile-first against the locked brand system; each state leads with the phone, desktop beside it. Decisions log + token-referenced component notes in The 99 - Collection README.md.

List treatment
Rows ⇄ grid view toggle. Dense rows lead on mobile; art grid on wide screens. 1a / 1b.
Filter pattern
Sticky bar above the list; active filters become removable chips. Same bar phone & desktop.
Card detail
Full modal overlay — big art, oracle text; full-screen sheet on phone.
Review flow
One scrollable review — summary banner, issues, sticky confirm. Alt shape at 2b.
Replace safety
Type-to-confirm — full replace can't happen by accident.
Scope
Read-only browse. No inline editing — the export stays source of truth; replace to update.
Browse treatment · two directions · ships as a view toggle
1a
Dense rows
— scan-first. Quantity, color, name, type, set per line. Mobile default.
