Let players choose their card store · R1

Each player picks one store, and its prices and buy links replace Mana Pool’s everywhere a price appears. Stores: Mana Pool (the default), TCGplayer and Card Kingdom. Every frame is the shipped page rendered from main with this round’s parts patched in (Settings, a deck’s Shopping list, the all-decks cart, card details, the Wishlist tab, the Home feed and What each deck is worth). Store prices are placeholders: TCGplayer about 9% under Mana Pool, Card Kingdom about 18% over.

1 · The setting: Settings, first section, one radio row per store

  • Settings gains Store at the top, above Notifications: it is the one setting that changes what every price in the app says. Settings stay in one place; the user menu gains nothing.
  • One row per store, drawn as Your decks’ rows with the upload choice’s radio ring: the name, then one line on what its prices are and whether it takes a whole list. A fourth store is one more row (the list is one array), so nothing is redrawn. A segmented switch was ruled out: three names barely fit at 390 and a fourth would not.
  • Saved as it changes, like every Settings section (the page sub already says so). A failed save puts the old store back and says We couldn’t save that. Try again. in the shared social toast.

2 · The nudge: a Change store link where people buy, no first run

  • No first-run step, banner or toast. Instead the provenance line on the deck Shopping strip and the all-decks cart ends with · Change store, a link to /settings#settings-store, which washes the section for two seconds as the deck ⋯ menu’s links do.
  • That line already names the store on every visit, so a player at a card shop reads Mana Pool where they buy and has the change one press away. It costs no row, no dismiss and no stored flag.

3 · Price, buy link and the label naming the store

  • Figures stay bare where they sit in rows and tiles (buy tags, Wishlist tiles, the feed’s drop): one store at a time, so no tag repeats its name.
  • The store is named once per surface, where the shipped copy already names Mana Pool: the strip and cart line (single-copy prices on Card Kingdom), the buy links (Buy these at Mana Pool, Buy any printing at TCGplayer, Buy FDC 4 at Card Kingdom, Buy at TCGplayer on Home), and deck cost (Card Kingdom prices, 2h ago).
  • Every shipped string that says Mana Pool is listed with its store form in the copy file, as one function of the store.

4 · The TCGplayer accuracy note

  • Short form, after the store on the strip and cart: TCGplayer market prices · averages of recent sales, so a listing can differ. Deck cost: TCGplayer market prices, 2h ago.
  • Card details: market price after the tag (after from $1.27 when that shows). The tag’s spoken sentence says it too.
  • Long form, once, in Settings’ TCGplayer row: Market prices, an average of recent sales, so a listing can cost more or less.
  • Rows, tiles and the feed carry no note: the strip above them says it.

5 · Mana Pool is the default: one place

  • The foot of Settings’ Store section: Mana Pool is the default. Changing store changes every price, buy link and price-drop alert. Nowhere else.

6 · No whole-list cart link

  • Where the store has none (TCGplayer, Card Kingdom), the strip and the all-decks cart draw the figure and the provenance as today, and in the link’s place one line: Card Kingdom has no link for a whole list. Buy each card from its details. The line about what the link sends goes with the link.
  • Driven by the store’s own flag, so a store that gains a cart link later just shows it.

7 · No price from the chosen store

  • Rows: the bare buy tag, as shipped for a card with no figure (silence is a state). The strip counts it out: 11 of 14 priced. None priced: no prices from Card Kingdom for these, no figure.
  • Card details: the shipped dashed no stock tag, and the link becomes Look for it at Card Kingdom (a search, not a claim it sells it). Wishlist tile: no price line. Home: no drop is raised.
