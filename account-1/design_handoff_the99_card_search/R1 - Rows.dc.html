The result row

One row at both widths, in the Wishlist search’s grammar: card art, the name, one quiet mono line under it. A press anywhere on the row opens card details; the row holds no other control. Every signal is the reader’s own (lock 8). Each case is drawn at phone (top) and desktop (bottom).

How the line reads
  • ×N owned or not owned always comes first. It is the first question, and a row is never blank.
  • Then · in {deck}, the first of the reader’s decks holding it by name, and +N for the rest. The deck name gives way first, never +N.
  • The wishlist is the bookmark at the end, filled in the accent, with on your wishlist as its accessible name.
  • The accessible name of the whole row is one sentence: Ashling, the Limitless, 2 owned, in HD - Elementals and 1 more deck, on your wishlist.
  • No type line, mana cost or price. Lock 3 names three things, and the card is one press away.
