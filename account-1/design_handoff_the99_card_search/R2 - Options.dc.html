2 · The rail: is ⌘K worth showing? No.

The shortcut is for people who already know it, and they learn it once. It doesn’t need a permanent place in a 62px rail. It still lives in three places a keyboard reader looks: the slot’s name tag on hover, the open field (where ⌘K sits at its right edge until you type), and the About entry’s key line.
