12Should the landing open All details itself?
Design's callYes. While a Find a deck landing waits, the next card opened at 390 opens at the full sheet rather than the teaser, its door scrolled into view and haloed for 2.4s. Once only: the landing ends there, and every later card opens at the teaser as it does today. Nothing is pressed: the read still starts only from the picker’s own press, so no credit moves.

Why: a landing’s job is to put the tool in front of the reader in one go, and every other Show me does. Haloing All details first would be two halos for one landing. The page already opens a tool’s tab and view for its landing; opening the sheet to its full height is the same kind of move. For engineering: the deferred landing hands the provider a one-shot openFull for the next load() when the card carries a fits door; a card without one (not Commander-legal, or the reader has no deck) opens at the teaser and the landing keeps waiting.
