The landing Try it is a link to the deck page (or the tool’s own page), with the tool named in the address. The page scrolls the control into view and gives it R1’s halo for 2.4 seconds, with no bubble: the card already said what the tool is for. Never spends A credit tool lands on the tile or press that opens it. The run press inside is never pressed for the reader, and the cost chip on the card matched the tile’s. Spends a moment Landing on a tool through Try it spends that tool’s R1 moment, since the reader has now met it. Switch off No card at all. Its group shrinks; a group left with no tools is not drawn.
