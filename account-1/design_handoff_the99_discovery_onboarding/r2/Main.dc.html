About becomes two tabs: Tools, on real screens, and a Reference you open from the screen you are on (R2b, after Hugh’s notes)

R2 of the discovery and onboarding brief (§2, §4, §Proposed split). It follows R1’s moments (#1919). Every screenshot here is a real capture of staging at 390, taken read-only from Hugh’s account; the desktop deck page and Collection are rebuilt from src/, as in R1.

C1About’s new shape: two tabs
  • Tools (the default) and Reference. The account menu’s About The 99 still opens About, on Tools. No row is added anywhere.
  • R2b: the letter becomes A note from the creator, the last row of the Reference, and is met once at the end of onboarding (R3). The app names no one outside the note itself. Board N1–N2.
  • The four chapters give way to screens in the Reference. Chapters were the order a reader learns the app; screens are where they are standing when they need it.
C2The showcase: fourteen tools in five groups
  • Grouped by a deck’s life: Start a deck (Help me choose, Precons) · Tune it (Top off, Proposed cuts, Suggested changes, Mana Balance) · Buy smarter (Cheaper stand-ins, Friends’ spares) · Use what you own (Find a deck, Wishlist, Collection Sync) · Play (Matchups, Stats for your decks, Friends and pods).
  • A tool is something a reader presses to get an answer. Marks and states (Card assignments, availability, bracket marks) stay in the Reference, where the ? finds them on the screen that draws them.
  • A card: a real screenshot cropped 3:2 around the control, with R1’s halo on it; the name and cost chip; one line; Try it; How it works. 1280: three columns at 354. 390: a sideways row per group at 300, the next card peeking.
  • Switch off: the card is absent and its group shrinks. Moment dismissed: drawn as usual.
  • R2b: a sixth section, Written for you, groups the five things that use credits (the Overview, Suggested changes, Look deeper, Help me choose with “Anything else?”, Which deck needs it most?), each with its cost (1 credit per run), and How credits work ›. Buying credits joins it with the payments designs.
C3Try it on your deck
  • R2b: the outlined press was heavy. Board Try-Options draws four ways side by side; Design’s pick is C, the whole card is the press, with the footer naming the deck it opens. Hugh picks.
  • One press that names the deck: the fitting deck the reader opened most recently. When several fit, a quiet 2 more are over ▾ lists them.
  • It lands on the deck with the control scrolled into view and haloed for 2.4 seconds, no bubble. A credit tool lands on its tile; nothing is ever run for the reader.
  • When no deck fits, the press is replaced by one line saying what the tool needs (“Needs a deck under 99. All 21 of yours are full.”) and a door when one helps.
  • Tools that need no deck say where they go instead: Open Help me choose →, Browse precons →.
C4The ? on every screen
  • In the chrome, so no screen gains a control: the rail’s foot above the account button at 1280; the header beside the account button at 390, scrolling away with it.
  • Opens this screen’s entries, and on the deck page those of the tab and view in view: a non-modal panel 400 wide at 1280, a sheet at 390. Other tabs are one press away under Elsewhere.
  • A row opens in place; Show me appears only when the entry’s control is on screen, and halos it. Search covers every entry on every screen.
  • Engineering adds screen to each entry, and view for a deck’s tabs. The full mapping, all 71, is the Reference board and the README.
C5The Reference
  • Every entry by screen (21 screens), in the order a reader meets them, one line each until opened. A screens index sits beside the list at 1280; at 390 each screen is a row that opens.
  • An opened entry leads with a real screenshot of its screen, its control haloed, then the caption and keys as shipped. An entry also in Tools keeps its Try it.
  • Search reads titles and text (today it reads titles only). Results name their screen; none says so and offers every entry back.
  • What’s new stays at the top. The How it works appendix stays at the end, inside the Reference, its links both ways unchanged.
C6How the places link
  • The Home banner’s Show me opens the Reference at what’s new for a returning reader, and Tools for a brand-new account.
  • The ? leads to the control, to the entry in the Reference, to the top of the Reference and to Tools. Tools leads to a deck, to the entry and to the Reference. The Reference leads to a deck, to How it works and to Tools.
  • Every end has a way on: board L2 lists them.
