Design’s pick: C The card is already the thing a reader is looking at, so making it the press removes the second focal point Hugh named, and it gives the phone the largest target there is. The footer still names the deck, so nothing is opened by surprise. What C gives up The menu of other decks. The tool itself still works on any deck once opened, and a reader with several decks over 99 is one press from the others. If that matters, B keeps the menu at the cost of a truncated deck name.
