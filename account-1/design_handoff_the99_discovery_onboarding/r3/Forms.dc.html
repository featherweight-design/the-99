Discovery R3 · how the five layers read side by side · Amonkhet

One tool, Suggested changes, met five ways, and no two alike

Each layer has its own form, so a player never mistakes one for another. A moment floats and speaks once. A nudge is a word in place, and stays. A tool card is a picture in About. The “?” lists entries. The tour dims the app, once ever. Every part on this board is the one the other boards mount.

F1The same tool, in each layer’s own form
R1 · A MOMENT
Floats beside a control, with a surface, a shadow, a nib and the diamond. It names the tool in a sentence, once, then is spent. It is not a press: the control is.
R3 · A NUDGE
In place, in the figure’s own slot: two words and an arrow, no surface. It is the press, and it stays while the figure is short. There is no ✕ and no halo.
R2 · A TOOL CARD In About’s Tools: a real screen with the control haloed, one line and the deck it opens on. It is there whenever a player goes looking.
R2 · THE “?”
Asked for: the screen’s entries, with Show me. It is never offered unasked.
R3 · THE TOUR
Once per account, ever: a scrim, a lit control and a card. It is about places, not tools, so Suggested changes is never a stop. Its tools are left to the four forms beside it.
