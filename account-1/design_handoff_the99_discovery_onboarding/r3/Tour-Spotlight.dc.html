The spotlight: a scrim, a cutout on the real control, and R1’s halo as its ring

Spotlights belong to the tour alone (lock 6). Nothing in a moment, a nudge or the “?” dims the app. The tour reuses R1’s halo for the cutout’s ring, so a lit control reads as part of the same family, and lights the control without pressing it.

On a slot · Commanders in the rail’s lock well · 5px of room, radius 14
On a card · step 1 of the checklist · 8px of room, radius 20
The scrim One layer over the whole app at 66%, rail and tab bar included. The cutout is a box shadow, so the lit control is drawn by the app itself, live and in its theme. The ring R1’s halo: 2px accent at 75%, then 6px at 20%. It follows the theme’s accent. The control under it Inert. A press on the cutout does nothing; the tour never presses for the player. Only the card is live. Between steps The cutout glides to the next control in 240ms on --motion-ease-out, and the card fades across in 160ms. Under reduced motion both cut.

Surface
--surface-raised, 1px --border-strong, radius 14, shadow 0 24 60 at 55%. It is the locked-slot note’s surface at card size, not a moment bubble. Placement
At 1280: 340 wide, 16px beside the lit control, and never over it. At 390: 358 wide, docked 12px above the tab bar; the page scrolls so the lit control clears it. Reading order
A mono kicker (2 of 6 · Commanders), then the title in the theme’s display face, a sentence or two, and a picture or the picker when the step has one. The footer holds progress diamonds and Back and Next. Presses
Next is the shipped bone primary; Back (from step 2) is the ghost. Step 6’s Next reads Finish. ✕ is Skip the tour. 32px at 1280; 44px, and the ✕ a 44px hit, at 390. Keys and readers
A modal dialog labelled by its title, with focus held in it and starting on Next. Enter presses Next and Esc skips. Each step’s lit control is named in the dialog’s description, and the step count is spoken. What never happens
No step runs anything, spends a credit or changes data. The one exception is the theme, which saves as the picker always does.
