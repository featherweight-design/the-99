Jottings: one line each, dated for you, at the top of Your deck. Select one to mark it done.

The block is the first thing in the Your deck panel, directly under the tab row, above the decklist toolbar and groups. It belongs to the deck, so it works the same on a draft. Rows are oldest first, with the creation date in mono on the right. Done jottings fold under Done (N). A [[ in the line opens a picker: the deck’s cards first, then any card. A tag is the card’s name, underlined solid if the deck holds it and dashed if it doesn’t, the same convention as the decklist’s owned dot. Pressing it opens card details. Nothing on the block counts down, ages or turns a colour.
