Home · one page, two widths

Both widths carry the same four blocks.

Desktop and mobile now hold the same home: the two CTAs, Ready to build, Pick up where you left off, and Commitments. Nothing else. Your decks is gone from both — it was a second, worse Decks page one tap from the real one, and the rail already has the menu item. What it was actually doing for you — getting you back into a deck — is what the resume block is for, so the resume block grew to take the whole job.

Both blocks are drawn at shipped geometry. The Ready-to-build card keeps its tall crop — art to the card’s full width with the name, type line and fit chip inside it, and the fit line and See the pool on the panel beneath. The resume row keeps its one-line shape: art bleeding flush to the left edge, name above a single meta line, count right-aligned. Art blocks stand in for the commander crops.

Your decks — dropped
Off desktop and off mobile. The count it carried (17) moves to the resume block’s footer link, so the number is still on the page and still one tap from all of them.
Sorted by last touched
Not /decks’ order. Touched means the last edit, commit, or logged game — not opened, or the block would fill with whatever you glanced at. The stamp sits under the count, in the slot the row already has, so the row does not grow a line.
Four rows, one line each
Two up from two, at the shipped row’s own height and occupancy. Desktop runs them 2×2 so the block stays one screen; mobile stacks them and gains the left art bleed desktop already had.
Commitments on mobile
Same three figures, same Review commitments, last on the page — it is the block you read, not the one you act on. On desktop it is the whole right rail now that the deck list has left it.
